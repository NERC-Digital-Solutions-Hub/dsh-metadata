The field **'Recent past 30 year mean 1990 - 2019 (All-relative Z-Score)'** (internal name `zartmaxp95recentpast`) represents the standardized baseline temperature score calculated using the mean and standard deviation across all scenarios.

### Description

This conversions the baseline extreme heat hazard into a standardized "All-Relative" Z-score. Unlike local scores, this metric allows for consistent comparison between the recent past and all potential future warming worlds on a single statistical scale.

* **Assumption:** Standardizing across all scenarios identifies neighbourhoods that were historically "relatively cool" or "relatively hot" compared to the full spectrum of projected climate futures.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is a standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** assessment.
* **Caveat:** A value of zero represents the average temperature across *all* warming scenarios in the database, not just the baseline average.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** baseline data across all scenarios.
