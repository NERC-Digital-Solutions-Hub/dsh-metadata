The field **'Young children (Z-Score)'** (internal name `zyoungchildren`) represents the standardized score for the percentage of the population under five.

### Description

This converts the raw percentage of young children into a standardized value. A score above zero indicates a neighbourhood with an above-average proportion of children.

* **Assumption:** Neighbourhoods with high positive Z-scores for this indicator are more sensitive to climate impacts and should be specifically targeted for pre-schooling and nursery-based guidance.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Sensitivity** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Z-scores represent relative vulnerability within Wales, not an absolute risk level.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **ONS Population Estimates**.
