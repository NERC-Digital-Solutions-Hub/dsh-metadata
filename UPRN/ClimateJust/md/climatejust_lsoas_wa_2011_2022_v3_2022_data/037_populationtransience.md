The field **'Population Transience (Residential Mobility Index)'** (internal name `populationtransience`) represents a measure of how frequently residents move in and out of the neighbourhood.

### Description

Used within the **Local Knowledge** domain, this metric identifies areas where high population turnover prevents the development of community resilience.

* **Assumption:** neighbourhoods with high transience are more vulnerable because residents are less likely to be aware of local hazard risks (like a historical flood boundary) or to have established local social networks for assistance.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Inability to Prepare** and **Ability to Respond** dimensions.
* **Caveat:** This index is an average (e.g., 2012-2020) and may not reflect very recent localized shifts in population.
* **Data Source:** Derived from the **Consumer Data Research Centre (CDRC) Residential Mobility Index**.
