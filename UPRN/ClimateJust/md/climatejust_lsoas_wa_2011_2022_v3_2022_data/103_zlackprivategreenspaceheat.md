The field **'Lack of domestic gardens (% dwellings without gardens) (Z-Score)'** (internal name `zlackprivategreenspaceheat`) represents the standardized score for the proportion of dwellings without gardens in a neighbourhood.

### **Description**
This metric places the lack of private green space on a relative scale across the study area. Standardizing this indicator allows it to be equally weighted with other physical factors, like building height, in the Enhanced Exposure index.

*   **Assumption:** positive Z-scores highlight neighbourhoods where the deficit of gardens significantly enhances residents' exposure to high temperatures compared to the national average.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Physical Environment** domain.
*   **Caveat:** Standardization identifies relative disadvantage but does not account for the quality or type of vegetation in neighbouring public spaces.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **ONS Greenspace** statistics.
