The field **'Relative heat Disadvantage, 2℃ warmer world'** (internal name `zarheatdisadvantagefuture20`) represents the final All-Relative Heat disadvantage score in a 2.0°C warmer world.

### **Description**
This All-Relative metric maps the geographical intersection of intensified 2°C heat hazards with local socio-spatial vulnerability. By standardising across all scenarios, it highlights areas where the transition to a 2°C world creates a significant increase in relative disadvantage compared to lower-warming levels.

*   **Assumption:** Heat disadvantage is highest where biological sensitivity and low adaptive capacity meet acute increases in projected temperatures.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the primary output of the **Heat Exposure and Disadvantage** assessment for the 2°C scenario.
*   **Caveat:** The score identifies geographical hotspots of future disadvantage relative to all other years and scenarios considered.
*   **Data Source:** Calculated as an equally weighted combination of the 2°C UKCP18 temperature projections and the SSHVI.
