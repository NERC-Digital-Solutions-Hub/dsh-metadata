The field **'Income deprived older people in an area (Z-Score)'** (internal name `z_olderadultincomedeprivation`) represents the standardized score for older adults (60+) in income deprivation.

### **Description**
This standardization highlights neighbourhoods with a high concentration of impoverished elderly residents.

*   **Assumption:** Neighbourhoods with high positive Z-scores are of the greatest concern for action, as they contain highly sensitive residents with very low adaptive capacity.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Income** domain within the adaptive capacity dimensions.
*   **Caveat:** Localized "pockets" of elderly deprivation may be less apparent when averaged at the LSOA level.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **WIMD 2019 IDAOPI** data.
