The field **'Inability to Respond Index'** (internal name `inabilitytorespondsshvi`) represents the composite score for the response dimension of the SSHVI.

### Description

This index identifies the capacity of a neighbourhood to avoid heat stress during an active heat event. Response capacity is governed by social factors that enable immediate action, such as whether people know what to do, feel safe to act (e.g., opening windows), and have local support systems.

* **Assumption:** If people lack mobility, social networks, or access to cool public spaces, they have a lower ability to respond to heatwave warnings or extreme temperatures.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is one of the five primary dimensions of the **Socio-Spatial Heat Vulnerability Index**.
* **Caveat:** Response capacity is influenced by both individual mobility and the quality of local "cool" infrastructure.
* **Data Source:** Derived from indicators across domains including **Income**, **Language**, **Internet**, **Social Networks**, **Mobility**, and **General Infrastructure**.
