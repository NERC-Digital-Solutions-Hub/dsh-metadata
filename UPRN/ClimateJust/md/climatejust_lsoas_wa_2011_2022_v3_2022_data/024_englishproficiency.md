The field **'Level of proficiency in English (% population)'** (internal name `englishproficiency`) represents the percentage of residents who cannot speak English well or at all.

### **Description**
Used within the **Language** domain, this is a proxy for **Information Use**. It identifies communities that face barriers to building resilience due to difficulties understanding official guidance.

*   **Assumption:** Higher proportions of people with low English proficiency indicate higher vulnerability, as they are less likely to obtain and use guidance provided to the general public during emergencies.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Inability to Prepare**, **Ability to Respond**, and **Ability to Recover** dimensions.
*   **Caveat:** While this is a high-confidence indicator for language difficulty, it does not account for residents who may rely on translated materials provided by local community groups.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from the **2011 Census** (Table QS205EW).
