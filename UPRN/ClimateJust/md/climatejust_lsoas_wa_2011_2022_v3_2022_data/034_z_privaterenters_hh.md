The field **'Private renters (Z-Score)'** (internal name `z_privaterenters_hh`) represents the standardized score for the percentage of households renting from private landlords.

### **Description**
This metric places private rental prevalence on a standard scale to compare tenure vulnerability between different regions of Wales.

*   **Assumption:** High Z-scores identify neighbourhoods with less control over home modifications and potentially higher population transience.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Inability to Prepare** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** Renting from a private landlord is not always synonymous with low income, though it consistently limits structural adaptive capacity.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **2011 Census** data.
