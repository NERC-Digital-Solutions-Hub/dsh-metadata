The field **'Older people (Z-Score)'** (internal name `zpeopleover75`) represents the standardized score for the percentage of the population aged 75 and over.

### Description

Standardization allows for a direct comparison of elderly populations across different LSOAs. Positive values denote that the LSOA has more older people than the national average.

* **Assumption:** Areas with high Z-scores are prioritized for adaptation responses, such as disseminating good practice to community organizations that support older people.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Sensitivity** dimension.
* **Caveat:** High Z-scores in areas with low overall social vulnerability may still require targeted action due to the physical isolation of elderly individuals.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **ONS Population Estimates**.
