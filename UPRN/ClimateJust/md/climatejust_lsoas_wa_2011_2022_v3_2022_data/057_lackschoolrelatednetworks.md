The field **'Lack of school-related networks (% children not of primary school age)'** (internal name `lackschoolrelatednetworks`) represents the proportion of children in a neighbourhood who do not fall into the primary school age cohort (4-11 years).

### Description

Within the **Social Networks** domain, this metric uses the presence of primary-school-aged children as a proxy for strong, localized community ties. "School gate" networks are recognized as vital conduits for information sharing and mutual aid during emergencies.

* **Assumption:** Neighbourhoods with high proportions of school-related networks are assumed to have stronger community cohesion; therefore, a "lack" of these networks (a high percentage of non-school-age children) indicates lower community resilience.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Inability to Respond** and **Inability to Recover** dimensions.
* **Caveat:** This indicator specifically targets the type of cohesion provided by primary schools; other forms of community support (e.g., faith groups or sports clubs) are not measured here.
* **Data Source:** Derived (and reversed) from **2011 Census** data on child age cohorts.
