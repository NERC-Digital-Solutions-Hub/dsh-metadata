The field **'Number of flats without gardens (Proxy for high-rise flats) (Z-Score)'** (internal name `zhighrisehomes`) represents the standardized score for dwellings that combine the lack of outdoor space with multi-story living.

### **Description**
This metric is used as a proxy for high-rise vulnerability to identify households facing the "double hit" of potentially higher internal temperatures and no personal outdoor space for cooling.

*   **Assumption:** positive Z-scores denote neighbourhoods where residents are significantly more likely to live in flats without gardens, indicating a high level of Enhanced Exposure.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Housing Characteristics** domain.
*   **Caveat:** This is a "derived indicator" and its suitability as a proxy can vary depending on local urban morphology.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **ONS Greenspace** and **Census** data.
