The field **'1.5℃ warmer world (All-relative Z-Score)'** (internal name `zartmaxp95future15`) represents the standardized heat hazard score for a 1.5°C scenario, calculated across the entire dataset.

### **Description**
This "All-Relative" Z-score places the projected hazard of a 1.5°C warming world onto the same scale as the baseline and the 2°C/3°C worlds.

*   **Assumption:** Positive values denote neighbourhoods where extreme heat hazards in a 1.5°C world will be higher than the average calculated across all years and scenarios in the database.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is a standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** assessment.
*   **Caveat:** This relative score identifies geographical hotspots of hazard intensification within the 1.5°C warming pathway.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** projections across all scenarios.
