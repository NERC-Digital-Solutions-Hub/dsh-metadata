The field **'Median price paid for residential properties (£)'** (internal name `housingtrap`) represents the median property sale price within an LSOA.

### Description

Used within the **House Prices** domain, this metric identifies areas of relative household wealth or deprivation. In the context of the SSFVI, low house prices are used as a proxy for a community’s lack of financial assets and reduced ability to "bounce back" after property damage.

* **Assumption:** Lower property prices indicate higher vulnerability because these areas often have lower levels of personal equity and fewer financial resources available to households for repairing damage or relocating during recovery.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Inability to Recover** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Note that higher values in this field (expensive properties) represent **LOWER** vulnerability in this specific framework.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **HPSSA Dataset 46** (Median price paid for residential properties by LSOA).
