The field **'Heat Disadvantage, 2℃ warmer world'** (internal name `zheatdisadvantagefuture20`) represents the final heat disadvantage score in a 2.0°C warmer world.

### **Description**
This metric illustrates how heat disadvantage will geographically expand and intensify as global temperatures rise by 2°C. It maps the "signature" of social vulnerability against a more severe hazard profile.

*   **Assumption:** neighbourhood disadvantage is most acute where high social vulnerability and intensified 2°C heat hazards converge, identifying where the most severe well-being losses are likely to occur.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Primary output for future **Heat Disadvantage**.
*   **Caveat:** geographical changes in disadvantage between scenarios can sometimes be small compared to the difference between emissions pathways.
*   **Data Source:** Calculated as the standardized sum of the **SSHVI** and the **ztmaxp95future20** score.
