The field **'Heat Disadvantage, 3℃ warmer world'** (internal name `zheatdisadvantagefuture30`) represents the final heat disadvantage score in a 3.0°C warmer world.

### **Description**
This metric maps the most severe potential future disadvantage, highlighting areas where high-warming 3°C heat hazards will encounter populations with the lowest resilience.

*   **Assumption:** neighbourhoods with high positive scores in this scenario identify where climate change is likely to most significantly increase social inequalities and negative well-being outcomes.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Primary output for future **Heat Disadvantage**.
*   **Caveat:** A 3°C world represents an extreme high-warming trajectory; actual disadvantage will depend on the effectiveness of global mitigation and local adaptation efforts.
*   **Data Source:** Calculated as the standardized sum of the **SSHVI** and the **ztmaxp95future30** score.
