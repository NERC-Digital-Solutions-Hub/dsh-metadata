The field **'Index of Multiple Deprivation crime score'** (internal name `crime`) represents the standardized score for crime prevalence within an LSOA.

### **Description**
The **Crime** domain accounts for how high levels of local crime can undermine personal and property-level adaptation. It identifies areas where fear of crime may prevent residents from taking necessary resilience actions.

*   **Assumption:** neighbourhood vulnerability is higher where crime is prevalent; residents may be wary of taking preventative measures (e.g., installing flood gates) for fear they will be stolen or might signal an empty property during an evacuation, potentially leading to delays in response.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Inability to Respond** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** The metric represents recorded crime data and may not fully reflect the subjective "fear of crime" that drives resident behavior.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from the **WIMD 2019 Community Safety Domain**.
