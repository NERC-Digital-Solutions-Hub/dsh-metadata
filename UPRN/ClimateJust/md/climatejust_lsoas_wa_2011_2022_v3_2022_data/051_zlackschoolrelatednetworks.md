The field **'Lack of school-related networks (Z-Score)'** (internal name `zlackschoolrelatednetworks`) represents the standardized score for the deficit of primary-school-aged children in an LSOA.

### **Description**
This converts the school-age cohort gap into a standardized value to allow for comparison with other social network indicators like pensioner isolation.

*   **Assumption:** neighbourhoods with high positive Z-scores are relatively disadvantaged in terms of localized, parent-driven social networks, potentially slowing the spread of information during a hazard event.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Social Networks** domain.
*   **Caveat:** Standardized deficit scores are relative and may be skewed in areas with unusual demographics, such as high student populations.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **2011 Census** data.
