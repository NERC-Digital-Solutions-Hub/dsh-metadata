The field **'Older people (% people over 75 years)'** (internal name `peopleover75`) represents the percentage of residents aged 75 and over.

### Description

Used within the **Age** domain, this metric identifies residents most likely to experience negative health outcomes during a climate event. Older people often have more limited physical mobility, making it difficult to use flood defense measures or evacuate.

* **Assumption:** Higher proportions of people over 75 indicate higher social vulnerability; statistically, this group accounted for a disproportionately high percentage of deaths in major historical flood events.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Sensitivity** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Some older individuals remain highly resilient and active; the indicator is a proxy for the increased biological risk associated with the cohort as a whole.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **ONS Mid-year Population Estimates**.
