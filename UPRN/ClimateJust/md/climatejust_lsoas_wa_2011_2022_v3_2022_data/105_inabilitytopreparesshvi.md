The field **'Inability to Prepare Index'** (internal name `inabilitytopreparesshvi`) represents the composite score for the preparedness dimension of the SSHVI.

### Description

This index identifies the average capacity of individuals within a neighbourhood to take precautions before a heat event. A person’s or community’s ability to prepare is governed primarily by social factors that enable advanced planning and environmental modification.

* **Assumption:** Adaptive capacity is limited by social factors; higher index values indicate a lower ability for residents to modify their homes or access information before a crisis.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is one of the five primary dimensions of the **Socio-Spatial Heat Vulnerability Index**.
* **Caveat:** Preparedness is often heavily influenced by structural factors, such as property tenure, which are outside of an individual's immediate control.
* **Data Source:** Derived from an equally-weighted combination of indicators within the **Income**, **Tenure**, **Language**, **Internet**, and **Local Knowledge** domains.
