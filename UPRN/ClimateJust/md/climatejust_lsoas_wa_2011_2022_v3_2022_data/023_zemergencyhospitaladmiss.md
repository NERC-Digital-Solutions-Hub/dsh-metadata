The field **'Rate of emergency admission to hospital (Z-Score)'** (internal name `zemergencyhospitaladmiss`) represents the standardized score for unplanned hospital admissions.

### Description

This metric places the rate of emergency admissions onto a uniform scale, identifying neighbourhoods with significantly higher-than-average baseline health sensitivity.

* **Assumption:** neighbourhoods with high positive Z-scores are more sensitive to the impacts of both flood and heat-related hazards.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Sensitivity** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Regional variations in admission rates can sometimes be driven by clinical practice rather than community health.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **IMD 2019** morbidity data.
