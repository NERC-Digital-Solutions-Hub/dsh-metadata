The field **'Average return travel time to a Pharmacy by private transport (minutes)'** (internal name `returntimepharmacyprivate`) measures the estimated driving time for a round trip to the closest pharmacy.

### Description

This measure of **Service Access** identifies neighbourhoods where geographical isolation creates barriers to recovery even for those with private transport.

* **Assumption:** higher travel times represent a deficit in medical service availability, increasing the vulnerability of a neighbourhood during the recovery phase of a hazard event.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Inability to Recover** dimension.
* **Caveat:** This indicator only measures travel time to the physical location of the service, not the quality or stock levels of the pharmacy itself.
* **Data Source:** Derived from the **WIMD 2019 Service Access Domain**.
