The field **'3℃ warmer world (℃)'** (internal name `tmaxp95future30`) represents projected heat hazard exposure in a world where global mean temperatures have risen by 3.0°C.

### Description

This metric projects the average daily maximum temperature of the top 5% hottest days under a high-warming 3°C scenario. It illustrates the extreme end of potential heat hazard intensification.

* **Assumption:** A 3°C global rise correlates with extreme local temperature increases that will likely lead to severe well-being losses in neighbourhoods with high social vulnerability.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for future scenarios.
* **Caveat:** Projections for 3°C warming represent a high-emissions future and carry greater uncertainty than lower-warming scenarios.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** climate projections.
