The field **'Social renters (Z-Score)'** (internal name `z_socialrenters_hh`) represents the standardized score for the percentage of social renters in an LSOA.

### **Description**
Standardization enables tenure-related vulnerability to be mapped uniformly alongside income and health sensitivity.

*   **Assumption:** High Z-scores represent areas where the lack of property ownership is a significant barrier to household-level flood preparation.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Inability to Prepare** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** The score is relative to the 2011 Census distribution across Wales.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **2011 Census** data.
