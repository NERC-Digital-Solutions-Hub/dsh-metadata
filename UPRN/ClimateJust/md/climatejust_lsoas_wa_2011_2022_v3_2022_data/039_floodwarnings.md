The field **'Flood alert and warning areas (% area not covered)'** (internal name `floodwarnings`) represents the percentage of a flood-prone area that is not covered by Environment Agency or Natural Resources Wales warning systems.

### Description

Used within the **Local Knowledge** domain, this assesses a community's access to formal early warning systems.

* **Assumption:** Areas not covered by warning systems have a lower capacity to prepare or respond effectively, as residents may be taken by surprise by a flood event. Higher "uncovered" percentages represent higher vulnerability.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Inability to Prepare** and **Ability to Respond** dimensions.
* **Caveat:** Coverage does not guarantee that residents have signed up for or will respond correctly to the warnings.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **Environment Agency 2020** or **Natural Resources Wales** Flood Alert and Warning Area data.
