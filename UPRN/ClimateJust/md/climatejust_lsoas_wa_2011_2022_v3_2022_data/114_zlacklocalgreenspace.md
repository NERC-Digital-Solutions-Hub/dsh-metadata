The field **'Lack of local greenspace access (Z-Score)'** (internal name `zlacklocalgreenspace`) represents the standardized score for the deficit of accessible public green space.

### Description

Standardization allows for the absence of public "cool spaces" (parks, gardens, playing fields) to be compared directly with social vulnerability factors like income or age.

* **Assumption:** High Z-scores identify neighbourhoods where residents face a relative disadvantage in terms of access to cooling infrastructure compared to the average neighbourhood in the dataset.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Physical Environment** domain.
* **Caveat:** This metric is often reversed from the original data (which measures greenspace size) to ensure high values correctly represent high vulnerability.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **ONS Greenspace** statistics.
