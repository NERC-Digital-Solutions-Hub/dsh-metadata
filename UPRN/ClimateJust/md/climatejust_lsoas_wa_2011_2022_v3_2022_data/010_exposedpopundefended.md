The field **'Exposed Population Undefended (Count)'** (internal name `exposedpopundefended`) represents the count of residents within a flood hazard zone who are NOT protected by formal flood defenses.

### **Description**
This metric identifies the most immediately at-risk segment of the population—those living within the floodplain without the mitigation of river or coastal defenses.

*   **Assumption:** Undefended populations are the most vulnerable to frequent flood events as there are no formal structural barriers to prevent inundation.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is a critical indicator for the **Population Exposure and Disadvantage** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** This is an estimated count; accurately identifying every undefended household requires high-resolution local data.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from matching population locations against **undefended flood hazard zones**.
