The field **'Income deprived people in an area (% of zone population)'** (internal name `incomedeprivation`) represents the percentage of all residents in an LSOA living in income-deprived households.

### Description

This is the primary indicator for the **Income** domain, capturing material deprivation across the entire neighbourhood.

* **Assumption:** Higher proportions of people on low incomes indicate higher vulnerability; low income households are less likely to have the capacity to fully prepare for future floods or afford temporary accommodation during recovery.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Inability to Prepare**, **Ability to Respond**, and **Ability to Recover** dimensions.
* **Caveat:** Income deprivation may be masked in neighbourhoods with high income inequality if the "average" appears stable.
* **Data Source:** Derived from the **WIMD 2019 Income Deprivation** statistics.
