The field **'Medical and Care Residents (Score)'** (internal name `medicalcareresidents`) represents a score based on the proportion of the population living in medical or care establishments.

### Description

Residents in care environments are particularly sensitive because they rely on institutional routines and complex home-based health care systems that are easily disrupted by flooding or power outages.

* **Assumption:** Higher scores indicate higher vulnerability, as these residents require additional institutional support and are often immobile or in fragile health.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Sensitivity** and **Ability to Respond** dimensions.
* **Caveat:** High vulnerability in this field relies on the flood resilience of the care facilities themselves.
* **Data Source:** Derived from the **Welsh Index of Multiple Deprivation (WIMD) 2019** (Community Safety or Health domains).
