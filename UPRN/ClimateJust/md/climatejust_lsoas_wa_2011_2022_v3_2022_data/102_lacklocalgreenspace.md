The field **'Lack of local greenspace access (%)'** (internal name `lacklocalgreenspace`) represents the proportion of a neighbourhood that lacks access to public green or blue spaces.

### **Description**
This indicator identifies communities that do not have easy access to public parks or open water, which serve as essential "cool refuges" during extreme heat events.

*   **Assumption:** The lack of accessible green or blue space increases local vulnerability because residents have fewer places to seek relief from high temperatures if their homes are poorly adapted.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Enhanced Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** This metric measures the presence of infrastructure, not the quality or safety of the greenspace for all demographic groups.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **Ordnance Survey** and land-use datasets identifying public green and blue spaces.
