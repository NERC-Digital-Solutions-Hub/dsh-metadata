The field **'Inability to Recover Index'** (internal name `inabilitytorecoverssfvi`) represents the composite score for the recovery dimension of the SSFVI for Wales.

### **Description**
This index reflects the degree to which an individual or community can aid their own recovery following a flood. It evaluates the capacity to obtain help and "bounce back" to normalcy after suffering property damage or health impacts.

*   **Assumption:** Recovery is influenced by several social factors; specifically, income levels (for replacing goods), mobility, and the availability of social networks and health services.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is one of the five primary dimensions of the **Socio-Spatial Flood Vulnerability Index**.
*   **Caveat:** Many flood events have highlighted that recovery can take individuals and communities a significant amount of time, often years.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from indicators in the **Income**, **Social Networks**, **Mobility**, and **Service Access** domains.
