The field **'Average return travel time to a GP by private transport (minutes)'** (internal name `returntimegpprivate`) measures the estimated driving time for a round trip to the closest GP surgery.

### Description

This measure of **Service Access** identifies neighbourhoods where geographical isolation creates barriers to medical assistance even for residents with access to a car. While personal transport aids response, extreme distances to GPs still represent a deficit in recovery capacity.

* **Assumption:** higher travel times represent a deficit in medical service availability, increasing the vulnerability of a neighbourhood during and after a heatwave.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Ability to Recover** dimension.
* **Caveat:** This indicator only measures travel time to the physical location and does not account for local surgery capacities.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **DfT/ONS Accessibility statistics**.
