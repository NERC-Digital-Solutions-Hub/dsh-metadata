The field **'Number of properties within historical flood boundary (Z-Score)'** (internal name `z_directfloodexperience`) represents the standardized score for direct community experience of past flood events.

### **Description**
Unlike other indicators, high positive raw scores here denote *lower* vulnerability. In the standardized index, this variable is **reversed** so that high positive Z-scores represent neighbourhoods **WITHOUT** past experience.

*   **Assumption:** High positive Z-scores in this field identify "inexperienced" neighbourhoods that may be more vulnerable because they lack the local knowledge and support available from surviving previous floods.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized (and reversed) input for the **Local Knowledge** domain.
*   **Caveat:** The score represents the "prisoner of experience" phenomenon, where communities remain more vulnerable until they unfortunately have direct experience.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **Sayers et al. (2017)** historical flood data.
