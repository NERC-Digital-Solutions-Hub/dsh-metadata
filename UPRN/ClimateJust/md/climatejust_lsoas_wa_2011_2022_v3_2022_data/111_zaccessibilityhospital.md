The field **'Mean distance to nearest A&E Hospital (Z-Score)'** (internal name `zaccessibilityhospital`) represents the standardized score for linear distance to emergency medical care.

### **Description**
Standardization converts hospital proximity into a relative value, helping to prioritize neighbourhoods for community-based health outreach or cooling interventions.

*   **Assumption:** positive Z-scores highlight neighbourhoods where secondary medical services are significantly more remote than the national average, increasing social vulnerability.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Service Access** domain.
*   **Caveat:** This standardization uses linear distance, which may mask the actual complexity of reaching a hospital in areas with poor road networks.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **hospital location data**.
