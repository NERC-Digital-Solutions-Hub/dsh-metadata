The field **'Exposed Population Undefended (Z-Score)'** (internal name `zexposedpopundefended`) represents the standardized score for the number of undefended people at risk of flooding.

### Description

This metric provides a relative measure of the undefended population across Wales, highlighting areas with disproportionately high numbers of people living in floodplains without structural protection.

* **Assumption:** Higher Z-scores indicate neighbourhoods where the scale of undefended exposure is significantly above the national average.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Population Exposure and Disadvantage** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Standardized scores simplify comparisons but may mask extreme local counts in very large LSOAs.
* **Data Source:** Standardized calculation derived from the **Exposed Population Undefended (Count)**.
