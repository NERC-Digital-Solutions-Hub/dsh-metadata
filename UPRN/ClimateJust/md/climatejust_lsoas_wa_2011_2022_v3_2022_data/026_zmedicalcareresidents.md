The field **'Employment deprived people in an area (Z-Score)'** (internal name `zmedicalcareresidents`) represents the standardized score for employment deprivation within a zone.

### **Description**
Within the framework, employment status is used as a proxy for **Income**. Unemployment limits the financial resources available for residents to prepare for, respond to, and recover from hazard events.

*   **Assumption:** Higher positive Z-scores indicate higher vulnerability; the unemployed are nearly twice as likely to experience persistent poverty, restricting their ability to flood-proof or insure their homes.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Inability to Prepare**, **Respond**, and **Recover** dimensions.
*   **Caveat:** Note that despite the internal name (`zmedicalcareresidents`), the provided label for field 026 in the Welsh dataset refers to **Employment Deprivation (Z-Score)**.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from the **Welsh Index of Multiple Deprivation (WIMD) 2019**.
