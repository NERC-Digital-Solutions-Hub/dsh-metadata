The field **'Average return travel time to a GP by public transport (Z-Score)'** (internal name `zreturntimegppublic`) represents the standardized score for GP access via non-private modes.

### **Description**
Standardization converts the raw travel time into a relative score to compare service accessibility across all neighbourhoods in the study area. A score of zero represents the Welsh/English average.

*   **Assumption:** positive Z-scores denote neighbourhoods that are significantly more isolated from primary medical services than the national average, hindering their Ability to Recover.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Service Access** domain.
*   **Caveat:** Standardized scores highlight relative disadvantage and should be viewed alongside the absolute travel time for local planning.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **DfT/ONS Accessibility statistics**.
