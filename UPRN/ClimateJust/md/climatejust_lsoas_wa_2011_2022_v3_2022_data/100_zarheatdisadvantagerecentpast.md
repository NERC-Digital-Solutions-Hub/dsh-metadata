The field **'Relative heat Disadvantage, recent past 30 year mean 1990 - 2019'** (internal name `zarheatdisadvantagerecentpast`) represents the final All-Relative Heat disadvantage for the recent past (1990-2019) period.

### Description

This metric is an "All-Relative" version of the heat disadvantage score, which combines historical climate data with the Socio-Spatial Heat Vulnerability Index (SSHVI). It is calculated using the mean and standard deviation across all four considered temperature scenarios (recent past, 1.5°C, 2°C, and 3°C), enabling direct statistical comparison between the baseline and future warming worlds.

* **Assumption:** Disadvantage is most acute where high social vulnerability and high extreme heat hazards coincide.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the primary output of the **Heat Exposure and Disadvantage** assessment for the recent past.
* **Caveat:** Standardized "All-Relative" Z-scores represent relative risk within the context of all possible warming levels included in the dataset.
* **Data Source:** Derived from an equally weighted combination of UKCP18 historical temperature data (top 5% hottest days) and the SSHVI.
