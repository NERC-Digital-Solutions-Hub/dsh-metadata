The field **'Rate of emergency admission to hospital'** (internal name `emergencyhospitaladmiss`) represents the standardized rate of unplanned hospital admissions.

### Description

This is a **Morbidity** indicator used within the **Health** domain to assess the underlying fragility of a community's health before a climate disaster occurs.

* **Assumption:** Areas with high rates of emergency admissions are assumed to have a more sensitive population, as residents may have pre-existing conditions that can be exacerbated by the stress of a flood or heat event.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Sensitivity** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Rates may be influenced by local hospital policies and proximity to medical facilities rather than health status alone.
* **Data Source:** Derived from the **IMD 2019 Health Deprivation and Disability Domain** (Acute Morbidity data).
