The field **'Median price paid for residential properties (Z-Score)'** (internal name `zhousingtrap`) represents the standardized score for median house prices.

### Description

Standardization converts property value data into a relative measure of community financial assets.

* **Assumption:** In the standardized index, this variable is **reversed** so that high positive Z-scores represent areas with **LOW** median house prices, indicating higher social vulnerability.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized (and reversed) input for the **House Prices** domain.
* **Caveat:** house price data is an indirect proxy for household wealth and may be skewed by the types of properties (e.g., flats vs. houses) sold in a specific year.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **HPSSA Dataset 46**.
