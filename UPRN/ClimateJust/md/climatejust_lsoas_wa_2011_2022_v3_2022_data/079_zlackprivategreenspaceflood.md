The field **'Lack of domestic gardens (Z-Score)'** (internal name `zlackprivategreenspaceflood`) represents the standardized score for dwellings without private gardens.

### Description

This conversions converts the lack of private green space into a relative value to prioritize urban cooling interventions.

* **Assumption:** High Z-scores identify neighbourhoods where the lack of private gardens significantly enhances residents' exposure to high temperatures and poor drainage.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Physical Environment** domain.
* **Caveat:** Z-scores highlight relative disadvantage and should be interpreted alongside local high-rise housing data.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **ONS Greenspace** statistics.
