The field **'Level of proficiency in English (Z-Score)'** (internal name `z_englishproficiency`) represents the standardized score for the proportion of residents with low proficiency in English.

### Description

Standardization highlights areas where language barriers are significantly more prevalent than the Welsh average, indicating high informational vulnerability.

* **Assumption:** Positive Z-scores represent neighbourhoods where residents are more likely to have difficulty obtaining and acting upon guidance provided to the general public.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Language** domain.
* **Caveat:** Standardized scores are relative to 2011 Census benchmarks and may have changed with more recent migration patterns.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **2011 Census** (QS205EW).
