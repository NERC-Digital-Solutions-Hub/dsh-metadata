The field **'Median combined size of parks and public gardens and playing fields within 1,000 m radius (m²)'** (internal name `averagecommunitygreenspace`) measures the availability of accessible public green space.

### Description

Used within the **Physical Environment** domain, this metric evaluates the availability of "cool spaces" and pervious land cover in the wider neighbourhood. It highlights communities that lack these essential natural offsets to the urban heat island and runoff effects.

* **Assumption:** More public greenspace results in higher infiltration and better local cooling; therefore, neighbourhoods with small or distant public parks are assumed to have higher enhanced exposure.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Enhanced Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Higher values for this indicator represent **LOWER** vulnerability; this variable is reversed in index calculations.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **ONS Greenspace** access statistics.
