The field **'Inability to Recover Index'** (internal name `inabilitytorecoversshvi`) represents the composite score for the recovery dimension of the SSHVI.

### **Description**
This index evaluates a community's capacity to obtain help and bounce back after suffering property damage or health impacts from extreme heat. It focuses on the availability of essential services and the strength of social networks needed for a quick return to normalcy.

*   **Assumption:** Recovery is influenced by several social factors, specifically personal mobility and the accessibility of medical and community support services.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is one of the five primary dimensions of the **Socio-Spatial Heat Vulnerability Index**.
*   **Caveat:** Recovery from heat-related illness often happens on a shorter timescale than recovery from flood-related property damage.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from indicators in the **Social Networks**, **Mobility**, and **Service Access** domains.
