The field **'Built up area (% not greenspace)'** (internal name `urbancover`) represents the proportion of a neighbourhood covered by impervious or built-up surfaces rather than vegetation.

### Description

Used within the **Physical Environment** domain, this metric assesses **Enhanced Exposure**. It identifies neighbourhoods where the lack of natural cooling and drainage provided by greenspace increases the local severity of climate hazards.

* **Assumption:** The more built up an area, the more likely it is that hazard impacts will be severe; a lack of greenspace results in less local cooling (enhancing heatwaves) and less infiltration (enhancing flood runoff).
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Enhanced Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** The type and management of greenspace (e.g., woodland vs. mown grass) significantly affect its actual cooling and drainage functions.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCEH Land Cover Maps 2021** and land-use statistics.
