The field **'Inability to Prepare Index'** (internal name `inabilitytopreparessfvi`) represents the composite score for the preparedness dimension of the SSFVI for Wales.

### Description

This index reflects the actions taken by individuals or communities during normal conditions (in the absence of a flood) that are likely to reduce the harm they suffer when a future flood occurs. It measures the social factors that enable or hinder this advanced preparation.

* **Assumption:** A person’s or community’s ability to prepare for climate and extreme weather events is governed primarily by social factors; higher values indicate a lower capacity to take precautions like home modification or insurance.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is one of the five primary dimensions of the **Socio-Spatial Flood Vulnerability Index**.
* **Caveat:** Preparedness is heavily influenced by external factors such as income and tenure which may be outside of an individual's immediate control.
* **Data Source:** Derived from an equally-weighted combination of indicators within the **Income**, **Tenure**, **Language**, **Internet**, and **Local Knowledge** domains.
