The field **'Socio-Spatial Flood Vulnerability Index'** (internal name `ssfvi`) represents the total aggregate vulnerability score for an LSOA in Wales.

### Description

The **SSFVI** provides a comprehensive insight into the social vulnerability of a neighbourhood should a flood occur. It maps how personal, social, and environmental factors converge to determine the potential for well-being losses.

* **Assumption:** Social vulnerability is a complex signature of five dimensions; higher scores identify neighbourhoods where negative social impacts are more likely to be severe during a flood.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the **total aggregate index** (SSFVI).
* **Caveat:** This area-based representation provides a broad, national-scale indication and should be supplemented with finer-scale local data.
* **Data Source:** An equally-weighted combination of the five dimensions: **Sensitivity**, **Inability to Prepare**, **Inability to Respond**, **Inability to Recover**, and **Enhanced Exposure**.
