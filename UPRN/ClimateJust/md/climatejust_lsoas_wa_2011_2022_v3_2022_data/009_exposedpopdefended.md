The field **'Exposed Population Defended (Count)'** (internal name `exposedpopdefended`) represents the count of residents within a flood hazard zone who are protected by formal flood defenses.

### **Description**
This metric accounts for the presence of river and coastal defenses, identifying people who are technically within the floodplain but benefit from protective infrastructure.

*   **Assumption:** Populations living behind defenses are less likely to experience "frequent" flooding, though they remain at risk of extreme events that may breach or overtop those defenses.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Population Exposure and Disadvantage** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** Not all defended areas are mapped (e.g., small local defenses or surface water interventions), so this may be an estimate.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from published data on areas protected by **river and coastal defenses** matched against population counts.
