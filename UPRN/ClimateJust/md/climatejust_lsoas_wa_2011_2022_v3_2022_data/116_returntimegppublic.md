The field **'Average return travel time to a GP by public transport (minutes)'** (internal name `returntimegppublic`) measures the time required for a round trip to the nearest General Practitioner using non-private transport.

### Description

Used within the **Service Access** domain, this is a critical metric for assessing the ability to recover from heat-related illness. High travel times by public transport identify barriers for the most vulnerable who may struggle to reach medical help quickly if they suffer heat stress.

* **Assumption:** Longer travel times to medical facilities correlate with higher social vulnerability, as communities face delayed access to critical primary healthcare.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Ability to Recover** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Actual travel times may increase during heatwaves if public transport services are disrupted by high temperatures.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **DfT/ONS Accessibility statistics**.
