The field **'Average return travel time to a Pharmacy by public transport (Z-Score)'** (internal name `zreturntimepharmacypublic`) represents the standardized score for pharmacy access via non-private modes.

### Description

Standardization converts travel times into relative scores to compare service accessibility across Wales.

* **Assumption:** positive Z-scores denote neighbourhoods that are significantly more isolated from primary pharmacy services than the national average, hindering their Ability to Recover.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Service Access** domain.
* **Caveat:** The score is relative to 2019 benchmarks and does not account for changes in service location since that date.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **WIMD 2019** travel time statistics.
