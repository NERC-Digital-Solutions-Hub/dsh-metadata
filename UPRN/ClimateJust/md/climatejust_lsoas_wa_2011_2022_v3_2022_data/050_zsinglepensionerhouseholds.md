The field **'Single pensioner households (Z-Score)'** (internal name `zsinglepensionerhouseholds`) represents the standardized score for the proportion of elderly residents living alone.

### **Description**
This metric places the raw percentage of single pensioner households on a uniform scale across Wales. A score of zero represents the Welsh average.

*   **Assumption:** Positive Z-scores denote neighbourhoods with higher-than-average social isolation among the elderly, identifying them as priority areas for community outreach and early warning sign-up campaigns.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Social Networks** domain.
*   **Caveat:** Standardized scores identify relative vulnerability but do not provide the absolute number of individuals requiring support.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **2011 Census** data.
