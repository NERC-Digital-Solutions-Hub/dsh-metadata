The field **'Population'** (internal name `population`) represents the total number of residents within a Lower Super Output Area (LSOA).

### **Description**
This metric provides the baseline count of the total population residing in the area, used as the denominator for calculating various percentage indicators and for normalization within the index.

*   **Assumption:** Higher total populations in vulnerable areas represent a higher aggregate risk to life and well-being.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This serves as a baseline for the **Population Exposure and Disadvantage** assessments.
*   **Caveat:** Population figures are estimates based on specific data years and may not reflect real-time transience or recent migration.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from mid-year population estimates (e.g., **ONS Mid-2019 Estimates** for the 2022 dataset).
