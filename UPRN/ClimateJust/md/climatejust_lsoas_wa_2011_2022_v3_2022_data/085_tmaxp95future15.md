The field **'1.5℃ warmer world (℃)'** (internal name `tmaxp95future15`) represents projected heat hazard exposure in a world where global mean temperatures have risen by 1.5°C above pre-industrial levels.

### Description

This metric projects the average daily maximum temperature of the top 5% hottest days under a 1.5°C global warming scenario. It is used to assess how local heat hazards will intensify compared to the recent past.

* **Assumption:** Higher temperatures in a 1.5°C warmer world represent an increased frequency and intensity of extreme heat events, placing greater stress on vulnerable populations.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for future scenarios.
* **Caveat:** These are probabilistic projections and represent average conditions over a 20-year period centered on the year a 1.5°C rise is reached.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** climate projections.
