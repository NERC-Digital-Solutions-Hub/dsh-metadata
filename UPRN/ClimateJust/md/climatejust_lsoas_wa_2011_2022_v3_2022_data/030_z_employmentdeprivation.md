The field **'Employment deprived people in an area (Z-Score)'** (internal name `z_employmentdeprivation`) represents the standardized score for employment deprivation.

### **Description**
This converts the raw percentage of employment deprivation into a relative score across Wales.

*   **Assumption:** Positive Z-scores denote neighbourhoods where residents are significantly more disadvantaged by a lack of employment than the Welsh average, leading to lower financial adaptive capacity.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Income** domain of the SSFVI.
*   **Caveat:** Z-scores are relative to the national mean of Wales at a specific point in time.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **WIMD 2019** data.
