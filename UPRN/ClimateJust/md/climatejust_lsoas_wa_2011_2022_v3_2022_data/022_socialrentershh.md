The field **'Social renters (% Households renting from Social or Council landlords)'** (internal name `socialrentershh`) represents the percentage of households renting from local authorities or social landlords.

### **Description**
Used within the **Tenure** domain, this metric identifies residents who have limited control over their physical living environment.

*   **Assumption:** Tenants are more vulnerable because they have less ability than homeowners to adapt their homes (e.g., installing flood gates). Social renters also statistically have lower incomes, creating a double disadvantage.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Inability to Prepare** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** Social tenants may sometimes benefit from adaptations put in place by social landlords at a block-wide scale, which can offset their personal inability to prepare.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from the **2011 Census** (Table KS402).
