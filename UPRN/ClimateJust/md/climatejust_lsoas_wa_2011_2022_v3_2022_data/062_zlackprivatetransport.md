The field **'Lack of private transport (Z-Score)'** (internal name `zlackprivatetransport`) represents the standardized score for households without access to a car or van.

### Description

Standardization enables transport-related vulnerability to be combined with health sensitivity and income deprivation in the final aggregate index.

* **Assumption:** high Z-scores highlight neighbourhoods where physical mobility is a significant barrier to personal climate adaptation and emergency response.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Mobility** domain.
* **Caveat:** This relative measure does not account for the quality or accessibility of local public transport networks that might offset a lack of private cars.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **2011 Census** data.
