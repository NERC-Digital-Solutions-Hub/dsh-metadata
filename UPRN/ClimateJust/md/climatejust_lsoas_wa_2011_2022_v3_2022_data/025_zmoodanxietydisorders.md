The field **'Mood and Anxiety Disorder Indicator (Z-Score)'** (internal name `zmoodanxietydisorders`) represents the standardized score for mental health disorders.

### **Description**
This metric identifies neighbourhoods where residents may be less resilient to post-event psychological stress due to high baseline levels of mood and anxiety disorders.

*   **Assumption:** Positive Z-scores represent neighbourhoods with higher mental health sensitivity, where residents may require more intensive long-term recovery support.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Sensitivity** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** Changes in local mental health service provision can influence the scores recorded in this indicator over time.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **IMD 2019** health data.
