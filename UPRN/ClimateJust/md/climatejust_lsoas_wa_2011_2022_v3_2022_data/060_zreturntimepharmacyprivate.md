The field **'Average return travel time to a Pharmacy by private transport (Z-Score)'** (internal name `zreturntimepharmacyprivate`) represents the standardized score for pharmacy driving times.

### **Description**
This standardization enables geographic isolation to be mapped alongside other social vulnerability factors.

*   **Assumption:** High Z-scores identify "medical deserts" where the time required to access essential pharmaceutical support is significantly above the Welsh average.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Service Access** domain.
*   **Caveat:** Standardization highlights relative disadvantage but may mask the absolute travel time in very remote rural areas.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **WIMD 2019** travel time statistics.
