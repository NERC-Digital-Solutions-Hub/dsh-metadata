The field **'Sub-standard Broadband (Z-Score)'** (internal name `z_substandardbroadband`) represents the standardized score for premises below the broadband Universal Service Obligation.

### Description

This metric identifies neighbourhoods with significantly worse-than-average digital connectivity, placing them on a uniform scale with other vulnerability factors.

* **Assumption:** Areas with high positive Z-scores have a lower capacity to respond to hazardous events because residents may struggle to access essential online warnings.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Internet** domain.
* **Caveat:** broadband infrastructure can change rapidly; these scores are based on the 2018 Ofcom snapshot.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **Ofcom 2018** data.
