The field **'Enhanced Exposure Index'** (internal name `enhancedexposuressfvi`) represents the composite score for the exposure-enhancing dimension of the SSFVI for Wales.

### Description

Unlike general hazard exposure, "Enhanced Exposure" refers to aspects of the physical environment and housing that tend to accentuate or mitigate the local severity of a flood event once it occurs.

* **Assumption:** Physical neighbourhood characteristics—such as a lack of greenspace (increasing runoff) or housing types like basements—"enhance" the degree of contact and the severity of impacts from a flood.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is one of the five primary dimensions of the **Socio-Spatial Flood Vulnerability Index**.
* **Caveat:** This metric represents potential for enhanced impact based on physical traits, not the actual depth or frequency of a specific flood event.
* **Data Source:** Calculated as an equally-weighted combination of indicators within the **Physical Environment** and **Housing Characteristics** domains.
