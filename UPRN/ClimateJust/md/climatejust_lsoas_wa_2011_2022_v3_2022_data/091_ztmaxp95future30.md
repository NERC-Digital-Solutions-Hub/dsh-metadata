The field **'Average temperature of top 5% hottest days, 3℃ warmer world (score)'** (internal name `ztmaxp95future30`) represents the relative heat hazard score for a 3.0°C warming scenario.

### Description

This converts the extreme warming projections of a 3°C scenario into a relative score.

* **Assumption:** neighbourhoods with high scores in this field represent the most hazardous future heat environments, requiring the most intensive climate adaptation efforts.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** component.
* **Caveat:** These scores reflect the highest levels of projected hazard and should be used to plan for worst-case scenarios.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** projections.
