The field **'Average temperature of top 5% hottest days, 1.5℃ warmer world (Score)'** (internal name `ztmaxp95future15`) represents the relative heat hazard score for a 1.5°C warming scenario.

### **Description**
This conversions the projected extreme heat hazard for a 1.5°C rise into a relative score, allowing it to be compared with the baseline and other warming levels on the same scale.

*   **Assumption:** Higher scores represent neighbourhoods where the 1.5°C rise will result in extreme heat hazards that are significantly more severe than the global baseline average.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** component.
*   **Caveat:** Scores are relative to the entire dataset and highlight where hazard intensification is most acute.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** projections.
