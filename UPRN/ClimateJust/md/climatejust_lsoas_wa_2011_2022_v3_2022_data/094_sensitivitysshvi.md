The field **'Sensitivity Index'** (internal name `sensitivitysshvi`) represents the composite score for the sensitivity dimension of the Socio-Spatial Heat Vulnerability Index (SSHVI).

### **Description**
Within the heat vulnerability framework, sensitivity refers to personal biophysical characteristics that affect the likelihood that a heatwave event will have negative health and welfare impacts. It identifies neighbourhoods where residents have an inherently higher susceptibility to harm due to physiological factors like age and health.

*   **Assumption:** Vulnerability is partly determined by physiological factors; groups like the elderly and the very young are more susceptible to the effects of high temperatures.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is one of the five primary dimensions of the **Socio-Spatial Heat Vulnerability Index**.
*   **Caveat:** This index focuses on biophysical susceptibility and is often hazard-independent (e.g., sensitivity to heat is often similar to sensitivity to flooding).
*   **Data Source:** Calculated as an equally-weighted combination of indicator Z-scores within the **Age** and **Health** domains.
