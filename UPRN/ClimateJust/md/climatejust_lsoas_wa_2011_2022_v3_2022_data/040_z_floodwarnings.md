The field **'Flood alert and warning areas (Z-Score)'** (internal name `z_floodwarnings`) represents the standardized score for the percentage of a flood-prone area not covered by official warning systems.

### **Description**
This standardization converts the coverage gap into a relative score across Wales, helping to prioritize infrastructure improvements for early warning systems.

*   **Assumption:** positive Z-scores denote neighbourhoods that are significantly less prepared to respond to floods due to a relative lack of formal warning system coverage.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Local Knowledge** domain.
*   **Caveat:** Standardized scores are based on the coverage situation as of August 2021.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **Environment Agency 2020** coverage data.
