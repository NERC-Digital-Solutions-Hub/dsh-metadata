The field **'Sub-standard Broadband (% premises below the Universal Service Obligation)'** (internal name `substandardbroadband`) represents the percentage of premises that do not meet minimum speed requirements.

### Description

Used within the **Internet** domain, this metric assesses digital exclusion as a barrier to receiving real-time information.

* **Assumption:** neighbourhoods with sub-standard broadband have a lower ability to respond to climate hazards, as residents may struggle to access online flood warnings, digital maps, or emergency services updates.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Ability to Respond** dimension.
* **Caveat:** This is a measure of infrastructure availability, not actual resident usage of digital devices.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **Ofcom 2018** data on the Universal Service Obligation (USO).
