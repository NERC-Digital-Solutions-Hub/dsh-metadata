The field **'Comparative illness and disability ratio (Z-Score)'** (internal name `zdisabilityandillhealth`) represents the standardized score for activity-limiting illness and disability.

### Description

This standardization enables health sensitivity to be combined with other social factors (like age) into the final aggregate SSFVI.

* **Assumption:** High Z-scores identify populations where a one-off "hit" from a flood event is most likely to make a pre-existing medical condition worse.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Sensitivity** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Standardization may mask the absolute numbers of people affected in very densely populated urban areas.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **IMD 2019** disability data.
