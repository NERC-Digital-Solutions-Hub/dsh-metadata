The field **'Employment deprived people in an area (% of working population)'** (internal name `employmentdeprivation`) represents the percentage of the working-age population experiencing employment deprivation.

### **Description**
Used within the **Income** domain, this metric identifies neighbourhoods where a lack of steady work limits households' financial adaptive capacity.

*   **Assumption:** Higher percentages represent higher vulnerability; unemployment is a recognised proxy for low income, which restricts access to home insurance and technical flood-proofing measures.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Inability to Prepare**, **Ability to Respond**, and **Ability to Recover** dimensions.
*   **Caveat:** In some cases, being home during the day (due to unemployment) may allow for a quicker response to physical flood threats, though this is often offset by a lack of financial resources.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from the **WIMD 2019 Employment Deprivation Domain**.
