The field **'High rise homes (% households with lowest floor 5th floor or above)'** (internal name `highrisehomes`) represents the percentage of dwellings where the lowest living level is on the fifth floor or higher.

### **Description**
This metric identifies populations living in elevated housing, a key factor in heat vulnerability. Upper-floor apartments often experience higher internal temperatures than ground-floor units during heatwaves due to the stack effect and lack of thermal mass.

*   **Assumption:** Residents in high-rise dwellings are more likely to be affected by heat stress as their homes are more susceptible to accumulated heat and have fewer cooling opportunities.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Enhanced Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** Residents in lower-floor apartments (e.g., 3rd or 4th) may still face high temperatures but are not captured by this specific 5th-floor threshold.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from the **2011 Census**.
