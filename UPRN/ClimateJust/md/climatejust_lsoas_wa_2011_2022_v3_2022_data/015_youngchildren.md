The field **'Young children (% people under 5 years)'** (internal name `youngchildren`) represents the percentage of residents who are under five years of age.

### Description

Used within the **Age** domain, this metric identifies populations with higher biological and situational sensitivity. Young children are less able to adapt their own behavior and may not recognize the dangers of flood waters or high temperatures.

* **Assumption:** Higher proportions of children under 5 years of age in an area indicate a higher social vulnerability due to their physiological sensitivity and reliance on others for safety.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Sensitivity** dimension.
* **Caveat:** The metric is a broad indicator of sensitivity; actual harm depends on the availability and responsiveness of parents and carers.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **ONS Mid-year Population Estimates** (e.g., 2019 estimates for the 2022 update).
