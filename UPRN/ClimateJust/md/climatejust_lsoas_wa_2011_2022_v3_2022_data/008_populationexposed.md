The field **'Population Exposed (Count)'** (internal name `populationexposed`) represents the total number of people within an LSOA who live in areas at risk of flooding.

### **Description**
This metric identifies the scale of potential exposure within a neighbourhood by counting the number of residents located within flood hazard zones.

*   **Assumption:** The number of people exposed provides a direct measure of the scale of potential well-being loss should a flood event occur.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Population Exposure and Disadvantage** dimension of the framework.
*   **Caveat:** To quantify exposure, only those living in ground floor or basement properties are typically considered in England and Wales.
*   **Data Source:** Calculated by combining hazard data (e.g., **Natural Resources Wales Flood Zones**) with residential point datasets.
