The field **'Average temperature of top 5% hottest days, 2℃ warmer world (Score)'** (internal name `ztmaxp95future20`) represents the relative heat hazard score for a 2.0°C warming scenario.

### **Description**
This Converts the projected extreme heat hazard for a 2°C rise into a standardized score.

*   **Assumption:** Higher scores identify neighbourhoods that will experience acute heat hazards in a 2°C world, which may be significantly beyond the current experience of the population.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** component.
*   **Caveat:** Standardized scores simplify complex climate data but can mask the absolute temperature value (in degrees Celsius).
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** projections.
