The field **'Caravan or other mobile or temporary structures (Z-Score)'** (internal name `zcaravanortemporarystructures`) represents the standardized score for non-permanent housing.

### Description

Standardization conversions the prevalence of temporary housing into a relative score to highlight neighbourhoods with the most fragile housing stock.

* **Assumption:** Positive Z-scores highlight communities where residents face disproportionately high risks due to poor housing quality that cannot effectively mitigate climate hazards.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Housing Characteristics** domain.
* **Caveat:** This metric is highly localized; standardized scores may be very high in areas with specific mobile home parks.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **2011 Census** data.
