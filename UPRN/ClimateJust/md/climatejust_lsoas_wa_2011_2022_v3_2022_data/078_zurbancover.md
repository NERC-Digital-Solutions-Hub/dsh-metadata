The field **'Built up area (Z-Score)'** (internal name `zurbancover`) represents the standardized score for urban land cover.

### Description

This standardization converts the built-up surface area into a relative score, allowing "grey infrastructure" prevalence to be compared with social indicators.

* **Assumption:** positive Z-scores highlight neighbourhoods with significantly higher impervious cover than the Welsh average, indicating high enhanced exposure to both heat and runoff.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Physical Environment** domain.
* **Caveat:** The score represents potential for enhanced hazard impacts, not actual recorded hazard events.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCEH/ONS Greenspace** data.
