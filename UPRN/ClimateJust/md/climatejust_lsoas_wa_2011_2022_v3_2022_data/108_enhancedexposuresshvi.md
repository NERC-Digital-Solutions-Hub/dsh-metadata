The field **'Enhanced Exposure Index'** (internal name `enhancedexposuresshvi`) represents the composite score for the exposure-enhancing dimension of the SSHVI.

### Description

Enhanced exposure refers to aspects of the physical environment and housing that tend to accentuate or offset the local severity of extreme heat events. Unlike climate exposure, this index maps the neighbourhood-specific traits that either "trap" heat or provide local cooling.

* **Assumption:** Physical characteristics—such as a lack of green space or high-rise housing—"enhance" the degree of contact and the severity of impacts from a heatwave.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is one of the five primary dimensions of the **Socio-Spatial Heat Vulnerability Index**.
* **Caveat:** This metric represents potential for enhanced impact based on physical traits, not the actual temperature recorded during an event.
* **Data Source:** Calculated as an equally-weighted combination of indicators within the **Physical Environment**, **Physical Geography**, and **Housing Characteristics** domains.
