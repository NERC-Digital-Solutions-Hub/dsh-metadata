The field **'Lack of superfast broadband (Z-Score)'** (internal name `z_lacksuperfastbroadband`) represents the standardized score for the lack of superfast connectivity in an LSOA.

### **Description**
Standardization allows for digital connectivity gaps to be compared directly with social factors like income or age in the aggregate vulnerability index.

*   **Assumption:** neighbourhoods with high Z-scores for this indicator face a relative disadvantage in digital communication and coordination during and after climate disasters.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Internet** domain.
*   **Caveat:** These scores were reconfirmed in 2022 but should be used with caution for absolute planning.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **Ofcom 2018** data.
