The field **'Income deprived people in an area (Z-Score)'** (internal name `z_incomedeprivation`) represents the standardized score for the percentage of the population living in income-deprived households.

### Description

Standardization allows for income deprivation to be equally weighted with other social factors like tenure and language in the final vulnerability index.

* **Assumption:** High Z-scores identify neighbourhoods where low income is a major driver of overall social vulnerability, restricting residents' ability to adapt their personal environments.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is a standardized input for the **Inability to Prepare**, **Respond**, and **Recover** dimensions.
* **Caveat:** Z-scores highlight relative poverty but do not provide the absolute number of households requiring financial assistance.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **WIMD 2019** data.
