The field **'Socio Spatial Heat Vulnerability Index'** (internal name `sshvi`) represents the total aggregate social vulnerability score for a neighbourhood with respect to heat-related hazards.

### **Description**
The **SSHVI** maps how personal, social, and environmental factors converge in particular neighbourhoods to determine where negative social impacts from heat are most likely. This information is essential for understanding how social vulnerability translates into actual heat disadvantage when combined with hazard exposure.

*   **Assumption:** Social vulnerability is a complex "signature" of five dimensions; higher scores identify neighbourhoods where communities are statistically more likely to suffer disproportionate harm during extreme heat events.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the **total aggregate index**.
*   **Caveat:** This area-based representation provides only a broad, national-scale indication and users are encouraged to build on it with finer-scale local data.
*   **Data Source:** An equally-weighted combination of the Z-scores from five dimensions: **Sensitivity**, **Enhanced Exposure**, **Ability to Prepare**, **Ability to Respond**, and **Ability to Recover**.
