The field **'Relative heat Disadvantage, 3℃ warmer world'** (internal name `zarheatdisadvantagefuture30`) represents the final All-Relative Heat disadvantage score in a 3.0°C warmer world.

### Description

This metric represents the most severe potential future disadvantage in the dataset, identifying where high-warming 3°C heat hazards will encounter populations with the lowest resilience. The All-Relative standardization places this extreme scenario on the same statistical scale as the recent past and lower-warming scenarios.

* **Assumption:** Positive scores denote higher disadvantage than average; neighbourhoods with the highest positive scores in this scenario represent the most hazardous future environments.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the primary output of the **Heat Exposure and Disadvantage** assessment for the 3°C scenario.
* **Caveat:** Z-scores highlight relative disadvantage and do not provide an absolute measure of physical heat stress.
* **Data Source:** Derived from an equally weighted combination of 3°C UKCP18 extreme temperature projections and the SSHVI.
