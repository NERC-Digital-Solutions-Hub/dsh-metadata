The field **'Lack of private transport (% households with no car or van)'** (internal name `lackprivatetransport`) represents the percentage of households that do not have access to a private vehicle.

### **Description**
Used within the **Mobility** domain, this is a critical metric for assessing both immediate response and long-term recovery. A lack of transport limits the ability to evacuate, reach medical services, or maintain essential supplies during a disaster.

*   **Assumption:** households without a car or van are more vulnerable because they face significant logistical challenges in responding to warnings (e.g., evacuating with belongings) and recovering after an event (e.g., accessing medical or pharmacy services).
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Inability to Respond** and **Inability to Recover** dimensions.
*   **Caveat:** In highly urbanized areas with excellent public transport, a lack of private cars may not represent the same level of vulnerability as in rural "transport deserts."
*   **Data Source:** Derived from the **2011 Census** (Table KS404SC).
