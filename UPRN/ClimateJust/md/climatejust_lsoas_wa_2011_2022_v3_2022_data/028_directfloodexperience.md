The field **'Number of properties within historical flood boundary'** (internal name `directfloodexperience`) represents the count of properties that have been affected by flooding in the past.

### **Description**
Used within the **Local Knowledge** domain, this metric identifies communities with "institutional memory" of flooding.

*   **Assumption:** Unlike most other metrics, higher proportions here indicate **LOWER vulnerability**. Communities with past experience are assumed to have more knowledge of what to do, how to respond to warnings, and have higher support available from local organizations.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Ability to Prepare** and **Ability to Respond** dimensions.
*   **Caveat:** This field is often reversed in the index calculation so that those *without* experience are marked as having higher vulnerability.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from historical flood zone estimates (e.g., **Sayers et al., 2017**).
