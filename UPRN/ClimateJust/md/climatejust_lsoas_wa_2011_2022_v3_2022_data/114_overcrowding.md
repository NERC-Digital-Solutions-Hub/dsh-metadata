The field **'Household overcrowding indicator'** (internal name `overcrowding`) represents the percentage of households in a neighbourhood that are considered overcrowded.

### **Description**
Overcrowding is used within the **Housing Characteristics** domain to identify dwellings where heat may accumulate more quickly and residents have fewer "cool rooms" to retreat to. It is a major driver of potential well-being losses.

*   **Assumption:** High housing density and overcrowding indicate higher vulnerability because these conditions exacerbate internal temperatures and limit the ability to avoid heat stress at home.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Enhanced Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** The definition of "overcrowded" relies on bedroom occupancy standards from the Census or WIMD.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **2011 Census** data or the **Index of Multiple Deprivation**.
