The field **'Private renters (% Households)'** (internal name `privaterentershh`) represents the percentage of residents renting from private landlords.

### Description

Used within the **Tenure** domain, this metric identifies residents often living in more transient housing with limited legal power to modify properties.

* **Assumption:** Private renters are less able to prepare for climate events as they are often prohibited from making structural changes and may be less inclined to invest in a property they do not own.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Inability to Prepare** dimension.
* **Caveat:** High turnover in private rentals can also lead to a loss of community "local knowledge" regarding past flood events.
* **Data Source:** Derived from the **2011 Census** (Table KS402).
