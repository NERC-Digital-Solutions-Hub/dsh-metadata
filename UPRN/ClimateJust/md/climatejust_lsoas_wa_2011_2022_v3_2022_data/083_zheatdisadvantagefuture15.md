The field **'Heat Disadvantage, 1.5℃ warmer world'** (internal name `zheatdisadvantagefuture15`) represents the final heat disadvantage score in a 1.5°C warmer world.

### **Description**
This metric identifies neighbourhoods where current social vulnerabilities will most severely intersect with projected 1.5°C heat hazards. It accounts for the likelihood of coming into contact with high temperatures and the severity of resulting negative impacts on health and well-being.

*   **Assumption:** Communities with the highest disadvantage scores in this future scenario should be prioritized for long-term resilience building to mitigate the well-being losses caused by climate change.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the primary output for future **Heat Disadvantage** assessments.
*   **Caveat:** This calculation assumes that social vulnerability remains static at current levels, though in reality, it may be worsened by the climate events themselves.
*   **Data Source:** Calculated as the standardized sum of the **SSHVI** and the **ztmaxp95future15** score.
