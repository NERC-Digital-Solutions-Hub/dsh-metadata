The field **'Income deprived older people in an area (% of people 60 or over)'** (internal name `olderadultincomedeprivation`) represents the proportion of residents aged 60+ living in income deprivation.

### Description

This targets a specifically vulnerable group where sensitivity (Age) and lack of adaptive capacity (Income) intersect.

* **Assumption:** Income-deprived older people are at extreme risk as they often lack both the physical mobility and the financial resources to protect themselves or their properties.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Inability to Prepare**, **Ability to Respond**, and **Ability to Recover** dimensions.
* **Caveat:** This indicator targets "older adults" using a threshold of 60+, which is lower than the 75+ threshold used for biophysical sensitivity.
* **Data Source:** Derived from the **WIMD 2019 Income Deprivation Affecting Older People (IDAOPI)** index.
