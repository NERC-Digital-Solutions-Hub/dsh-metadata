The field **'Lack of domestic gardens (% dwellings without gardens)'** (internal name `lackprivategreenspaceheat`) represents the percentage of residential properties in an area that do not have a private garden.

### **Description**
Used within the **Physical Environment** domain, this metric assesses **Enhanced Exposure**. Domestic gardens provide vital localized "ecosystem services," such as evaporative cooling and shading, which are absent in areas dominated by gardenless dwellings.

*   **Assumption:** neighbourhoods with fewer domestic gardens are more vulnerable because they lack the cooling functions that private green spaces provide, leading to more severe local heat impacts.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Enhanced Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** The cooling benefit depends on whether the gardens are maintained as vegetation or are paved over by owners.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **ONS Greenspace** statistics.
