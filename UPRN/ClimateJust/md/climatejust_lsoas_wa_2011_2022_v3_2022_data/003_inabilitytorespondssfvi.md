The field **'Inability to Respond Index'** (internal name `inabilitytorespondssfvi`) represents the composite score for the response dimension of the SSFVI for Wales.

### **Description**
This index identifies the underlying reasons why some individuals or communities act more effectively in the run-up to and during an active flood event. It measures factors that determine whether people know what to do and feel able to take immediate action.

*   **Assumption:** Response capacity is governed by social factors; if people lack mobility, social networks, or language proficiency, they have a lower ability to respond to flood warnings or events.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is one of the five primary dimensions of the **Socio-Spatial Flood Vulnerability Index**.
*   **Caveat:** While broad agreement exists on these factors, the specific weighting of response drivers is an area of continued research.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from indicators across domains including **Income**, **Language**, **Internet**, **Local Knowledge**, **Social Networks**, and **Mobility**.
