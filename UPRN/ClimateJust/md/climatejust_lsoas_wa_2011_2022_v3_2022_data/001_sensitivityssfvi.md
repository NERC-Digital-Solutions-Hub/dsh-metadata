The field **'Sensitivity Index'** (internal name `sensitivityssfvi`) represents the composite score for the sensitivity dimension of the **Socio-Spatial Flood Vulnerability Index (SSFVI)** for Wales.

### **Description**
Within the SSFVI framework, sensitivity refers to personal biophysical characteristics which affect the likelihood that a flood event will have negative health and welfare impacts. This index identifies neighbourhoods where residents have an inherently higher susceptibility to harm due to physiological factors.

*   **Assumption:** Vulnerability is partly determined by physiological factors; individuals like the very young, the elderly, and those in poor health are biologically more susceptible to the physical and mental stress of flood events.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is one of the five primary dimensions of the **Socio-Spatial Flood Vulnerability Index**.
*   **Caveat:** This index focuses on biophysical susceptibility and is often hazard-independent (e.g., sensitivity to heat is often similar to sensitivity to flooding).
*   **Data Source:** Calculated as an equally-weighted combination of domain scores for **Age** and **Health**.
