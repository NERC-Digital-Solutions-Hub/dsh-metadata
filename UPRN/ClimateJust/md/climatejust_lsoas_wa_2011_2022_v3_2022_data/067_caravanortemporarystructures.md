The field **'Caravan or other mobile or temporary structures (% of all households)'** (internal name `caravanortemporarystructures`) represents the percentage of households residing in non-permanent dwellings.

### **Description**
Used within the **Housing Characteristics** domain, this identifies residents in dwellings that offer the least physical protection against climate extremes.

*   **Assumption:** Higher proportions of households in temporary structures indicate higher vulnerability, as these dwellings provide limited protection against flood waters and lack the thermal mass required to regulate temperatures during heatwaves.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Enhanced Exposure** and **Community Support** dimensions.
*   **Caveat:** Residents in mobile homes may also have limited knowledge of the local area and lower ability to move their possessions to safety.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from the **2011 Census** (Table KS401).
