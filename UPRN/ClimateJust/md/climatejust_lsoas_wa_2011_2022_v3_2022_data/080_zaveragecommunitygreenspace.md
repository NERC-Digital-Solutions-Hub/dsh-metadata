The field **'Median combined size of parks and public gardens and playing fields within 1,000 m radius (Z-Score)'** (internal name `zaveragecommunitygreenspace`) represents the standardized score for accessible public green space.

### Description

Standardization conversions the size and proximity of public parks into a relative score across Wales.

* **Assumption:** In the standardized index, this variable is **reversed** so that high positive Z-scores denote neighbourhoods with **LOW** access to public green space, representing higher enhanced exposure.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized (and reversed) input for the **Physical Environment** domain.
* **Caveat:** Z-scores identify neighbourhoods lacking "cool refuge" infrastructure compared to the national average.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **ONS Greenspace** statistics.
