The field **'Average return travel time to a Pharmacy by public transport (minutes)'** (internal name `returntimepharmacypublic`) measures the time required for a round trip to the nearest pharmacy using non-private transport.

### **Description**
This is a measure of **Service Access**, identifying communities that may struggle to obtain essential medicines following a climate disaster. Long travel times by public transport indicate a significant barrier for the most vulnerable, particularly those without private vehicles.

*   **Assumption:** Longer travel times to pharmacies correlate with higher social vulnerability, as communities face delayed access to medical supplies required for post-event recovery.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Inability to Recover** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** Actual travel times may be significantly higher during a flood or heatwave if public transport services are disrupted.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from the **WIMD 2019 Service Access Domain**.
