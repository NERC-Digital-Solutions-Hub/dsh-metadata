The field **'Single pensioner households (%)'** (internal name `singlepensionerhouseholds`) represents the percentage of households occupied by a person aged 65 or over living alone.

### Description

Used within the **Social Networks** domain, this metric identifies residents who are at high risk of **social isolation**. During extreme events, these individuals are less likely to receive informal warnings or have someone immediately available to provide physical assistance.

* **Assumption:** neighbourhoods with higher proportions of single pensioner households are more vulnerable because these residents are statistically more likely to be socially isolated and less likely to ask for help during a crisis.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Inability to Respond** and **Inability to Recover** dimensions.
* **Caveat:** This is a proxy indicator; some single pensioners may have very strong external social ties that are not captured by household composition.
* **Data Source:** Derived from the **2011 Census** (Table QS113).
