The field **'Lack of domestic gardens (% dwellings without gardens)'** (internal name `lackprivategreenspaceflood`) represents the percentage of residential properties that do not have access to a private garden.

### **Description**
This is a measure of **Enhanced Exposure** related to the built environment. Private gardens provide vital localized "ecosystem services," including drainage and cooling, which are lost in areas dominated by gardenless dwellings or high-rise living.

*   **Assumption:** Neighbourhoods with few domestic gardens are more vulnerable because they lack the localized cooling and drainage functions that gardens provide, leading to more severe heat and surface water flood impacts.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Enhanced Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** Actual resilience depends on whether owners maintain their gardens as green space or pave them over.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **ONS Greenspace** statistics.
