The field **'Recent past 30 year mean 1990 - 2019 (℃)'** (internal name `tmaxp95recentpast`) represents the baseline climate hazard exposure for a neighbourhood.

### Description

This metric records the average daily maximum temperature of the top 5% hottest days during the 1990-2019 period. It provides the historical benchmark for current heat hazard exposure.

* **Assumption:** higher temperatures in the recent past indicate a baseline of heat hazard-exposure that communities have already adapted to (to varying degrees).
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** component of the framework.
* **Caveat:** Users should note that people in different parts of the country are used to different baseline temperatures, so heat health thresholds vary by location.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** climate data.
