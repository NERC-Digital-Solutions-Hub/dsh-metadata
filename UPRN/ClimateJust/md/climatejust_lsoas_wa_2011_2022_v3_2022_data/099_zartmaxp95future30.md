The field **'3℃ warmer world (All-relative Z-Score)'** (internal name `zartmaxp95future30`) represents the standardized heat hazard score for a 3.0°C scenario, calculated across the entire dataset.

### Description

This is the "All-Relative" Z-score for the highest projected heat hazard pathway in the database.

* **Assumption:** Neighbourhoods with high positive scores in this field represent the most extreme future heat environments relative to all other years and scenarios considered in the study.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** component.
* **Caveat:** This score reflects a worst-case warming pathway and is used to identify areas requiring urgent long-term adaptation planning.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** projections across all scenarios.
