The field **'Comparative illness and disability ratio'** (internal name `disabilityandillhealth`) represents the standardized ratio of residents receiving disability-related benefits or living with limiting illness.

### Description

Used within the **Health** domain, this metric identifies neighbourhoods where a high proportion of people face day-to-day activity limitations due to ill health.

* **Assumption:** People in ill-health are more vulnerable as floods can restrict access to medicine, disrupt home-based care (e.g., dialysis), or cause severe mental health stress.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Sensitivity** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Higher values represent higher vulnerability; the ratio is a comparative measure against national averages.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **Department for Work and Pensions** data via the **IMD 2019**.
