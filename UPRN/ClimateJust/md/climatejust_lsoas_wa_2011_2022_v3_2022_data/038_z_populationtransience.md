The field **'Population Transience (Z-Score)'** (internal name `z_populationtransience`) represents the standardized score for residential mobility within a neighbourhood.

### **Description**
Standardization identifies LSOAs where the population is significantly more transient than the Welsh average, which can erode community resilience.

*   **Assumption:** High Z-scores denote neighbourhoods where social networks and local knowledge of flood hazards are likely to be weakest due to frequent resident turnover.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Local Knowledge** domain.
*   **Caveat:** Standardized transience is a relative measure and does not capture the specific nature of the resident turnover (e.g., students vs. temporary workers).
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **CDRC 2021** mobility data.
