The field **'People in overcrowded households (%) (Z-Score)'** (internal name `zovercrowding`) represents the standardized score for household overcrowding.

### Description

Standardization allows for overcrowding prevalence to be placed on a relative scale, enabling it to be combined with building traits like high-rise living to assess physical susceptibility.

* **Assumption:** positive Z-scores highlight neighbourhoods where housing density is significantly higher than the average, representing an "enhanced" potential for heat-related harm.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Housing Characteristics** domain.
* **Caveat:** Standardized scores highlight relative risk but do not provide the absolute number of affected people.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from the **WIMD** or **Census** overcrowding data.
