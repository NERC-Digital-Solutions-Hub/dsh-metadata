The field **'2℃ warmer world (All-relative Z-Score)'** (internal name `zartmaxp95future20`) represents the standardized heat hazard score for a 2.0°C scenario, calculated across the entire dataset.

### **Description**
This "All-Relative" Z-score identifies neighbourhoods experiencing extreme heat in a 2°C world that is significantly above the average for all warming paths.

*   **Assumption:** Higher Z-scores represent a more acute shift in heat hazard intensification compared to the baseline and lower-warming scenarios.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** component.
*   **Caveat:** Standardized scores are based on the specific distribution of temperatures within the Welsh/English dataset and cannot be used for direct international comparisons.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** projections across all scenarios.
