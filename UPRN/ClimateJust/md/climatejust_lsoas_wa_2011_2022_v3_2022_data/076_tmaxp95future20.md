The field **'2℃ warmer world (℃)'** (internal name `tmaxp95future20`) represents projected heat hazard exposure in a world where global mean temperatures have risen by 2.0°C.

### **Description**
This metric projects the average daily maximum temperature of the top 5% hottest days under a 2°C global warming scenario. It represents a more severe intensification of heat hazard than the 1.5°C scenario.

*   **Assumption:** As global temperatures rise to 2°C, local extreme heat events become significantly more dangerous, potentially overwhelming current adaptive capacities in highly sensitive areas.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for future scenarios.
*   **Caveat:** The geographical differences between future temperature scenarios can sometimes be small at the local scale.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** climate projections.
