The field **'Mood and Anxiety Disorder Indicator'** (internal name `moodanxietydisorders`) represents a measure of the prevalence of mental health disorders in an area.

### Description

Mental health is a critical component of the **Health** domain. Flooding is a significant cause of recorded psychological stress, including PTSD, depression, and anxiety, which are often long-lasting.

* **Assumption:** Populations with existing mood and anxiety disorders are more vulnerable as the trauma of a climate event is more likely to cause a severe "hit" or accelerate their adverse health trajectory.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Sensitivity** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Mental health data may be under-reported in certain communities due to social stigma or lack of access to diagnosis.
* **Data Source:** Derived from the **IMD 2019 Health Deprivation and Disability Domain**.
