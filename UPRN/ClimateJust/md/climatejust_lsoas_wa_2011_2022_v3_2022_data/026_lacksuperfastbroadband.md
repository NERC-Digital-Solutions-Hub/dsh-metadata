The field **'Lack of superfast broadband (% Premises)'** (internal name `lacksuperfastbroadband`) represents the proportion of premises in an area that do not have access to superfast broadband speeds.

### **Description**
Used within the **Internet** domain, this targets communities with limited high-speed connectivity which can hinder the use of complex recovery tools or information platforms.

*   **Assumption:** A lack of high-speed connectivity is associated with lower digital resilience, making it more difficult for communities to coordinate responses or access support services during a crisis.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Ability to Respond** and **Ability to Recover** dimensions.
*   **Caveat:** This indicator was reversed from original Ofcom data to ensure that high values represent high vulnerability.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **Ofcom 2018** SFBB availability data.
