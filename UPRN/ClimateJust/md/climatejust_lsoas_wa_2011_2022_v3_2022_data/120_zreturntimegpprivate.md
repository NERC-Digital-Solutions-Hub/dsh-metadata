The field **'Average return travel time to a GP by private transport (Z-Score)'** (internal name `zreturntimegpprivate`) represents the standardized score for GP driving times.

### Description

This standardization enables geographic isolation for car-users to be mapped on a uniform scale with other social vulnerability factors, such as income deprivation or health sensitivity.

* **Assumption:** High positive Z-scores identify neighbourhoods where even those with private transport face significant travel barriers to reaching primary care.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is the standardized input for the **Service Access** domain.
* **Caveat:** While standardized, this metric represents a relative measure of isolation and does not quantify absolute physical risk.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **DfT/ONS Accessibility statistics**.
