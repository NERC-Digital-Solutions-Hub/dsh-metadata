The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature moderate emissions 100 year return period 2020 median' (internal name tasmax_rcp45_rp100_2020_50p) represents the central estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave for the current period under RCP 4.5
.
Description
This metric provides the 50th percentile (median) estimate for daytime extreme maximums that have a 1% annual chance of occurrence today
. It identifies the "likely" current severity of these extremely rare heat events for communities today in a moderate emissions scenario
.
Assumption: Increased exposure to extreme heat leads to higher social disadvantage when it coincides with neighbourhoods containing sensitive populations
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: Median values provide a central probabilistic outlook but should be viewed alongside absolute high-end projections for emergency management
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
