The field **'Summer average temperature high emissions 2070-2090 90th percentile'** (internal name `tas_rcp60_2079_90p`) represents the high estimate of projected summer average temperatures in Wales for the period 2070–2090 under a high emissions scenario (RCP 6.0).

### **Description**
This provides a **90th percentile** high-estimate projection for late-century temperatures. It identifies areas of potentially acute heat hazard today if climate response to high emissions is higher than central estimates suggest.

*   **Assumption:** Areas with the highest projected temperatures face the greatest challenge in protecting residents from heat-related health and welfare impacts.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** This is a probabilistic high-end estimate representing the top 10% of likely temperature outcomes for this specific late-century scenario.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
