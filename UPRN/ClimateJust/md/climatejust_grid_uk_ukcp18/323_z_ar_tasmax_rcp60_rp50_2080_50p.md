### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature high emissions 50 year return period 2080 median

#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized measure of the likely late-century hottest part of a 2% annual-chance extreme summer day. Standardizing across all scenarios identifies neighbourhoods likely to face extreme intensification of relative daytime extreme hazards compared to the database average by the 2080s in a high global emissions future.

*   **Assumption:** Neighbourhoods with higher relative scores are assumed to have a greater chance of exposure to acute heat hazards, driving up total heat disadvantage.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** assessment.
*   **Caveat:** Values represent conditions over a 20-year average and do not reflect absolute peak temperatures reached during a specific event.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** late-century maximum temperature projections.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
