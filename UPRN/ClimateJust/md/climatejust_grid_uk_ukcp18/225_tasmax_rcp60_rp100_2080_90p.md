The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature high emissions 100 year return period 2080 90th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp60_rp100_2080_90p) represents the high estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period under RCP 6.0.
Description
This metric represents the 90th percentile high estimate, providing a cautious "worst-case" projection for extremely rare daytime extreme events by the end of the century
. It identifyies where rare extreme heat hazards could become catastrophically severe if climate response to greenhouse gases is high in a world of rising emissions
.
Assumption: Higher extreme temperatures signify a greater potential for residents to experience catastrophic heat-related health impacts, driving up geographical disadvantage
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for future scenarios
.
Caveat: This probabilistic high-estimate represents a scenario with only a 10% chance of being exceeded according to the model
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
