### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 20 year return period 2050 10th percentile

#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the 10th percentile mid-century projection for 5% annual-chance extreme heat events into a standardized score. It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face the minimum expected intensification of relative extreme heat hazards by the 2050s in a low emissions future, relative to all possible warming levels in the database.

*   **Assumption:** Understanding the geographical distribution of future relative extreme heat hazards is essential for ensured justice in adaptation planning.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
*   **Caveat:** Values represent averages over the 20-year window centered on 2050.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century probabilistic projections.
