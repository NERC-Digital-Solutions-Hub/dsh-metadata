The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 20 year return period 2050 10th percentile' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp26_rp20_2050_10p) represents the standardized low estimate of mid-century 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave temperatures under RCP 2.6
.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score converts the 10th percentile mid-century projection for 5% annual-chance extreme heat events into a standardized score
. It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face the minimum expected intensification of relative extreme heat hazards by the 2050s in a low emissions future, relative to all possible warming levels in the database
.
Assumption: Understanding the geographical distribution of future relative extreme heat hazards is essential for ensured justice in adaptation planning
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: Values represent averages over the 20-year window centered on 2050
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 mid-century probabilistic projections
.
