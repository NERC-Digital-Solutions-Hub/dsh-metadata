The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature moderate emissions 20 year return period 2080 10th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp45_rp20_2080_10p`) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period under RCP 4.5.

### Description

This projection maps the **10th percentile** low estimate for rare extreme heat events (5% annual probability) toward the end of the century. It provides a long-term outlook for the minimum expected intensification of heat hazards across Wales in a world that transitions slowly to a low-carbon future.

* **Assumption:** Understanding the geographical distribution of future rare heat hazards is necessary for long-term community resilience and justice in adaptation planning.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for the late-century.
* **Caveat:** Late-century projections carry significant probabilistic uncertainty and higher levels of uncertainty due to the long time scales.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
