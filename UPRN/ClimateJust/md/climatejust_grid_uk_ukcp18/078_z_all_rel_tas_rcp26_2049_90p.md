The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature low emissions 2040-2060 90th percentile'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tas_rcp26_2049_90p`) represents the standardized high estimate (90th percentile) of mid-century summer temperatures under RCP 2.6.

### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** standardized the high-end probabilistic projection for mid-century summer temperatures across all database scenarios. It identifies where heat hazard exposure may become significantly higher than the database average even under a low emissions path if climate response is high.

* **Assumption:** positive Z-scores highlight neighbourhoods where mid-century extreme temperatures are projected to be significantly above the global dataset average, representing high future hazard exposure.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is a standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** This is a high-impact climate outcome, representing a scenario with only a 10% chance of being exceeded according to the probabilistic model.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** probabilistic climate projections.
