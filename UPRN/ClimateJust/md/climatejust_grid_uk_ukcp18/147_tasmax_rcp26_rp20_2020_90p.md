The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature low emissions 20 year return period 2020 90th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp26_rp20_2020_90p`) represents the high estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the current period under RCP 2.6.

### Description

This metric represents the **90th percentile** high estimate for daytime maximums during a rare extreme heat event. It identifies areas potentially facing acute current heat exposure if the climate system is more sensitive to low levels of greenhouse gases than central estimates suggest.

* **Assumption:** Higher temperatures equate to a higher likelihood of communities coming into contact with harmful heat events, driving up geographical disadvantage.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Values in this field are statistically 90% likely to be higher than the actual average temperatures for this specific scenario and period.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
