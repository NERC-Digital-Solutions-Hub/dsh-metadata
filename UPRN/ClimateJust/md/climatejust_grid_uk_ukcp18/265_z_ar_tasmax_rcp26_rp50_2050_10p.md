The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 50 year return period 2050 10th percentile' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp26_rp50_2050_10p) represents the standardized low estimate of mid-century temperatures during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave under RCP 2.6.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score converts the 10th percentile mid-century projection for 2% annual-chance extreme heat events into a standardized score.
 It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face the minimum expected intensification of relative 1-in-50 year daytime heat hazards by the 2050s in a low emissions future.
Assumption: Understanding the geographical distribution of future relative extreme heat hazards is essential for ensuring justice in adaptation planning.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure.
Caveat: Probabilistic estimates represent average conditions over a 20-year window centered on 2050.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 mid-century probabilistic data.
