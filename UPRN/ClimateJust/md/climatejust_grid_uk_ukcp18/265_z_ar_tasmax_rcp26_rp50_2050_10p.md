### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 50 year return period 2050 10th percentile

#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the 10th percentile mid-century projection for 2% annual-chance extreme heat events into a standardized score. It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face the minimum expected intensification of relative 1-in-50 year daytime heat hazards by the 2050s in a low emissions future.

* **Assumption:** Understanding the geographical distribution of future relative extreme heat hazards is essential for ensuring justice in adaptation planning.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Probabilistic estimates represent average conditions over a 20-year window centered on 2050.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century probabilistic data.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
