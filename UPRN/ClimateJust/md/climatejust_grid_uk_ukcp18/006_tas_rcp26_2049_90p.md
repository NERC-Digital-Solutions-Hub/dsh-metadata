The field **'Summer average temperature low emissions 2040-2060 90th percentile'** (internal name `tas_rcp26_2049_90p`) represents the high estimate of projected average summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2040–2060 under a low emissions scenario (RCP 2.6).

### **Description**
This metric provides a **90th percentile** high-estimate projection for average summer temperatures in the 2050s. It identifies areas of potentially acute future heat exposure if global climate responses to greenhouse gases are at the higher end of the projected range.

*   **Assumption:** Higher temperatures equate to a higher likelihood of communities coming into contact with harmful heat events, driving up geographical disadvantage.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** Users are encouraged to view this alongside median estimates to understand the range of uncertainty in future climate response.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
