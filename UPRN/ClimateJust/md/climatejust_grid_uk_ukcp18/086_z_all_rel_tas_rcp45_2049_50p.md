The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature moderate emissions 2040-2060 median'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tas_rcp45_2049_50p`) represents the standardized central estimate of projected mid-century summer temperatures under RCP 4.5.

### **Description**
This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized "likely" mid-century projection. Under the framework, the median of the moderate emissions scenario is the **recommended starting point** for assessing future heat risks.

*   **Assumption:** neighbourhood disadvantage is most acute where high socio-spatial vulnerability meets acute increases in projected relative temperatures.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is a standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** assessment.
*   **Caveat:** Standardized scores simplify complex data but do not reflect absolute Celsius temperatures reached during specific heatwaves.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century temperature projections.
