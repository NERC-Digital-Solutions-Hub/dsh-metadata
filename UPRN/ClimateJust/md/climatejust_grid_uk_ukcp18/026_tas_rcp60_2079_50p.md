The field **'Summer average temperature high emissions 2070-2090 median'** (internal name `tas_rcp60_2079_50p`) represents the central estimate of projected average temperatures for the period 2070–2090 in Wales under a high emissions scenario (RCP 6.0).

### **Description**
This metric provides the **50th percentile (median)** estimate for average summer temperatures in the 2080s. It identifies neighbourhoods likely to face extreme intensification of heat hazards by late century if global emissions follow a high trajectory.

*   **Assumption:** Neighbourhoods projected to have the highest temperatures are the most at risk for well-being losses if they also have high biophysical sensitivity.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** It represents a 20-year average and may mask shorter, more intense periods of heat stress; furthermore, heat health thresholds will likely shift by this time.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
