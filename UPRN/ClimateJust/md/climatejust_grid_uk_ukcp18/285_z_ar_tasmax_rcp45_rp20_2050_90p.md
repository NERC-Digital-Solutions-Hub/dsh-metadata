#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** standardized the high-end mid-century projection for 5% annual-chance daytime maximums against all other database scenarios. It identifies geographical areas projected to face acute mid-century extreme heat hazard exposure relative to the entire dataset average if the climate system is highly sensitive to moderate greenhouse gas levels.

* **Assumption:** Higher relative maximum temperatures signify a greater potential for residents to experience catastrophic heat-related impacts.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** This is a probabilistic high-estimate, representing a scenario that is only 10% likely to be exceeded according to the model.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century probabilistic projections.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
