The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 20 year return period 2050 90th percentile' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp45_rp20_2050_90p) represents the standardized high estimate of projected mid-century temperatures during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave under RCP 4.5.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score standardized the high-end mid-century projection for 5% annual-chance daytime maximums against all other database scenarios
. It identifies geographical areas projected to face acute mid-century extreme heat hazard exposure relative to the entire dataset average if the climate system is highly sensitive to moderate greenhouse gas levels
.
Assumption: Higher relative maximum temperatures signify a greater potential for residents to experience catastrophic heat-related impacts
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: This is a probabilistic high-estimate, representing a scenario that is only 10% likely to be exceeded according to the model
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 mid-century probabilistic projections
.
