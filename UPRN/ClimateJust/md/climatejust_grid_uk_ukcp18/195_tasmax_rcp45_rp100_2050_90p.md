The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature moderate emissions 100 year return period 2050 90th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp45_rp100_2050_90p) represents the high estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period under RCP 4.5
.
Description
This metric represents the 90th percentile (high estimate), providing a cautious projection for extremely rare daytime events by mid-century
. It identifyies where rare extreme heat hazards could become catastrophically severe if climate response to greenhouse gases is high, even under a moderate emissions pathway
.
Assumption: Neighbourhood disadvantage is statistically higher where high social vulnerability and intensified hazards for very rare events converge
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for future scenarios
.
Caveat: High estimates represent a scenario that is only 10% likely to be exceeded according to the probabilistic model
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
