The field 'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 2010-2030 10th percentile' (internal name z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp45_2019_10p) represents the standardized low estimate of current average daily maximum summer temperatures under a moderate emissions scenario.
Description
This metric is an "All-Relative" Z-score, meaning it is calculated using the mean and standard deviation across all scenarios in the database
. It converts the 10th percentile (low estimate) for current (2010–2030) daytime maximums under RCP 4.5 into a standardized relative score
.
Assumption: Higher relative daytime highs indicate a baseline of exposure that can lead to losses in community well-being
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: Actual current daytime maximums are statistically 90% likely to be higher than these values for this specific scenario
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 current period probabilistic maximum temperature data
.
