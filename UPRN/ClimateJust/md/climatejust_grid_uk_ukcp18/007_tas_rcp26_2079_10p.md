The field 'Summer average temperature low emissions 2070-2090 10th percentile' (internal name tas_rcp26_2079_10p) represents the low estimate of projected average summer temperatures in Wales for the late-century period (2070–2090) under a low emissions scenario (RCP 2.6).
Description
This metric identifies the 10th percentile estimate for summer temperatures towards the end of the century. It provides a baseline for long-term adaptation planning, mapping the areas least likely to see significant average temperature increases even in a low-emissions future
.
Assumption: Higher average temperatures increase the frequency of heat-related well-being losses; mapping these projections helps prioritize long-term resilient infrastructure
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This indicator informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for the 2080s time horizon
.
Caveat: Late-century projections carry higher levels of uncertainty due to the long time scales and complex climate feedback mechanisms
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
