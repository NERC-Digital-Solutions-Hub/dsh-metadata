The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature low emissions 20 year return period 2020 10th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp26_rp20_2020_10p`) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature at the hottest part of the day during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave in the current period.

### **Description**
This metric evaluates **Hazard Exposure** by identifying the severity of a "20-year return period" event—an extreme daytime temperature that has a 5% (1 in 20) chance of occurring in any given year. This value represents the 10th percentile low estimate for the 2020 window (2010–2030) under a low emissions scenario.

*   **Assumption:** Areas with higher projected extreme maximum temperatures during heatwaves are likely to see more severe community well-being losses when combined with high social vulnerability.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** These absolute temperature values represent averages over a 20-year time period centered on the year 2020.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
