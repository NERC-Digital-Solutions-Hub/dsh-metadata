The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 2040-2060 10th percentile'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp45_2049_10p`) represents the standardized low estimate of mid-century average daily maximum summer temperatures under a moderate emissions scenario.

### **Description**
This metric is an **"All-Relative" Z-score**, meaning it is calculated using the mean and standard deviation across all four considered temperature scenarios and all time periods (2020s, 2050s, 2080s) in the database. It converts the 10th percentile (low estimate) projection for mid-century (2040–2060) typical daytime highs into a standardized relative score, identifying neighbourhoods where the minimum expected future hazard remains relatively low compared to the entire spectrum of climate futures.

*   **Assumption:** Higher relative daytime maximum temperatures indicate a baseline of exposure that can lead to losses in community well-being.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** Standardized scores highlight relative risk within the context of all possible warming levels included in the dataset but do not quantify absolute physical risk in degrees Celsius.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** (United Kingdom Climate Projections 2018) probabilistic projections on a 25km grid.
