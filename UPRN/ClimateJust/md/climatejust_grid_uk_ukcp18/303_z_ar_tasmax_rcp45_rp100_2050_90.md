The field **'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 100 year return period 2050 90th percentile'** (internal name `z_ar_tasmax_rcp45_rp100_2050_90`) represents the standardized high estimate of projected mid-century temperatures during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave under RCP 4.5.

### **Description**
This **"All-Relative" Z-score** standardized the high-end mid-century projection for exceptionally rare 1% annual-chance daytime maximums against all other database scenarios. It identifies geographical areas potentially facing catastrophic mid-century relative extreme heat hazard exposure if the climate system is highly sensitive to moderate greenhouse gas levels.

* **Assumption:** Positive relative Z-scores highlight neighbourhoods where future climate change is likely to most significantly increase social inequalities.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** This is a high-impact climate outcome, representing a scenario that is only 10% likely to be exceeded according to the probabilistic model.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century probabilistic projections.
