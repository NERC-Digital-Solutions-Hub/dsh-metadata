The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature low emissions 100 year return period 2050 10th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp26_rp100_2050_10p) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period under RCP 2.6.
Description
This projection identifies the 10th percentile low estimate for extremely rare heat events (1% annual probability) three decades into the future.
 It maps where the minimum expected mid-century extreme heat hazard remains relatively lower if global carbon mitigation efforts are successful.
Assumption: Understanding the geographical distribution of future extremely rare heat hazards is necessary for just adaptation and resilient infrastructure planning.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for future scenarios.
Caveat: Estimates represent conditions over a 20-year window (2040–2060) centered on 2050.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections.
