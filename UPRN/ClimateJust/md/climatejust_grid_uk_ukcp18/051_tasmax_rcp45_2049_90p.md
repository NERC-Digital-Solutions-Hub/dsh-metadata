The field **'Summer average maximum temperature moderate emissions 2040-2060 90th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp45_2049_90p`) represents the high estimate of projected average daily maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2040–2060 under a moderate emissions scenario (RCP 4.5). 
### **Description** 
This metric evaluates **Hazard Exposure** by identifying the expected temperature at the hottest part of a typical summer day (averaged across June, July, and August) three decades into the future. It provides a **90th percentile** projection, mapping where typical daytime highs could become acute if the climate is highly sensitive to moderate greenhouse gas levels. 
* **Assumption:** Higher average maximum temperatures indicate an increased exposure risk, where daytime heat events are more likely to cause well-being losses in vulnerable communities. 
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension of the Socio-Spatial Climate Vulnerability framework. 
* **Caveat:** As a 90th percentile high-estimate, this represents a scenario with only a 10% probability of being exceeded according to the probabilistic model. 
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** (United Kingdom Climate Projections 2018) probabilistic projections.
