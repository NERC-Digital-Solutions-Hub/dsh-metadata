The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature high emissions 20 year return period 2020 90th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp60_rp20_2020_90p`) represents the high estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the current period under RCP 6.0.

### **Description**
This metric identifies the **90th percentile** high estimate for daytime maximums during a 5% annual-chance heat event today. It maps areas facing acute current extreme heat exposure if the climate system responds more aggressively to rising greenhouse gas levels than central estimates predict.

*   **Assumption:** Higher extreme temperatures indicate an increased exposure risk, driving up geographical disadvantage in neighbourhoods containing sensitive populations.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** This is a high-end projection; statistally, actual temperatures during such an event are 90% likely to be below these values for this specific scenario and period.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
