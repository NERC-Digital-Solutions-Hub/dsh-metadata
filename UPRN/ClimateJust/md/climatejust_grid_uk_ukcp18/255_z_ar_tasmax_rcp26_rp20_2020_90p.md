The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 20 year return period 2020 90th percentile' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp26_rp20_2020_90p) represents the standardized high estimate of current 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave temperatures under RCP 2.6
.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score converts the near-term high-end projection for 5% annual-chance heat events into a standardized relative score
. It identifies areas of high current extreme heat exposure relative to the entire dataset average across all time periods and scenarios, representing a high-impact climate outcome
.
Assumption: Higher relative temperatures signify a greater potential for residents to experience catastrophic heat-related health and welfare impacts
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: This is a high estimate; actual temperatures are statistically 90% likely to be below these values for this specific scenario
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 probabilistic climate projections
.
