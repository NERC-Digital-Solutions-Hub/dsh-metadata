The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature high emissions 50 year return period 2080 90th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp60_rp50_2080_90p`) represents the high estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period under RCP 6.0.

### Description

This metric represents the **90th percentile** high estimate, providing a cautious "worst-case" projection for very rare daytime extreme events by the end of the century. It identifies where late-century extreme heat hazards could become catastrophic if the climate responds aggressively to high emissions.

* **Assumption:** neighbourhood disadvantage is statistically higher where high biophysical sensitivity and acute extreme heat hazards coincide.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for future scenarios.
* **Caveat:** There is only a 10% probability that the actual extreme temperature for this period and scenario will exceed this value.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
