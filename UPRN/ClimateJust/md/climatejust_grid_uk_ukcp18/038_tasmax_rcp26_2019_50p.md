The field **'Summer average maximum temperature low emissions 2010-2030 median'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp26_2019_50p`) represents the central estimate of projected average daily maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2010–2030 under a low emissions scenario (RCP 2.6).

### **Description**
This metric provides the **50th percentile (median)** estimate for average maximum temperatures, representing the typical temperature at the hottest part of a summer's day.

*   **Assumption:** Higher average maximum temperatures indicate increased exposure risk, where daytime heat events are more likely to cause well-being losses in vulnerable communities.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** It represents the median of a range of probabilistic outcomes and does not account for extreme outlier years.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
