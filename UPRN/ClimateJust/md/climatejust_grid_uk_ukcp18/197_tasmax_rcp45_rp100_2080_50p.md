The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature moderate emissions 100 year return period 2080 median' (internal name tasmax_rcp45_rp100_2080_50p) represents the central estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period under RCP 4.5
.
Description
This metric provides the 50th percentile (median) estimate for the severity of a 1% annual-chance extreme temperature event at the end of the century
. It identifies neighbourhoods likely to face intensified rare extreme heat hazards by late century even assuming global climate stabilization occurs by then
.
Assumption: Neighbourhoods projected to have the highest extreme temperatures face the greatest challenge in protecting sensitive residents from catastrophic health impacts
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for future scenarios
.
Caveat: Values represent conditions over the 20-year time period centered on 2080 (2070–2090)
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
