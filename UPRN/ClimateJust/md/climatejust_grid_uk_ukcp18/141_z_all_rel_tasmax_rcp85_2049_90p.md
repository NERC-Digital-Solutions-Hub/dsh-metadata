The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature very high emissions 2040-2060 90th percentile'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp85_2049_90p`) represents the standardized high estimate of projected mid-century average daily maximum summer temperatures under RCP 8.5.

### Description

This metric is an **"All-Relative" Z-score**, meaning it is calculated using the mean and standard deviation across all scenarios and time periods in the database. It converts the 90th percentile high-estimate projection for mid-century (2040–2060) typical daytime highs into a standardized score, allowing for direct comparison against the current baseline and other futures.

* **Assumption:** Higher relative maximum temperatures correlate with an increased likelihood of communities coming into contact with harmful heat events.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is a standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** component of the framework.
* **Caveat:** Standardized scores highlight relative risk across all considered warming levels but mask the absolute Celsius values.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** probabilistic maximum temperature projections.
