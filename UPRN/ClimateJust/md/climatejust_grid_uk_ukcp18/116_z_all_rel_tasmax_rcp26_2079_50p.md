The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 2070-2090 median'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp26_2079_50p`) represents the standardized central estimate of late-century average daily maximum summer temperatures under RCP 2.6.

### **Description**
This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized measure of the likely late-century hottest part of a typical summer's day. It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face extreme intensification of relative daytime maximums compared to the database average by the 2080s.

*   **Assumption:** Positive relative scores highlight neighbourhoods where future climate change is likely to most significantly increase social inequalities.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
*   **Caveat:** Values around 0 in this field denote that projected daytime highs are around the average for the entire database.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** late-century maximum temperature projections.
