The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature moderate emissions 20 year return period 2020 median'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp45_rp20_2020_50p`) represents the central estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the current period under RCP 4.5.

### Description

This metric provides the **50th percentile (median)** estimate for current extreme daytime temperatures that have a 5% annual probability. It identifies the likely current severity of these rare heat events for communities today in an intermediate scenario where global emissions stop increasing past the 2030s.

* **Assumption:** Increased exposure to extreme heat leads to higher social disadvantage when it coincides with neighbourhoods containing elderly residents, children, or those in ill health.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** While the median is a central estimate, it represents a middle point in a range of probabilistic climate responses.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
