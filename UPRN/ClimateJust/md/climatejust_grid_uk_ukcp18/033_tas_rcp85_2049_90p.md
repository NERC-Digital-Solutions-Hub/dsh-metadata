The field 'Summer average temperature very high emissions 2040-2060 90th percentile' (internal name tas_rcp85_2049_90p) represents the high estimate of projected summer average temperatures in Wales for the mid-century period (2040–2060) under a very high emissions scenario (RCP 8.5)
.
Description
This metric provides a 90th percentile high estimate, mapping the most hazardous potential future heat environments relative to other mid-century scenarios
.
Assumption: Neighbourhoods where intensified hazard exposure coincides with high sensitivity and low adaptive capacity are identified as having extreme heat disadvantage
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: This probabilistic high-end estimate represents a cautious "worst-case" projection for this emissions pathway
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
