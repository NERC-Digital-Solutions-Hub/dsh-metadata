The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature low emissions 50 year return period 2020 median'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp26_rp50_2020_50p`) represents the central estimate (median) of the expected temperature during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave for the current period under RCP 2.6.

### **Description**
This metric provides the central central estimate for the severity of an extremely rare daytime heat event (2% annual probability) today. Within the framework, it identifies the "likely" severity of these rare events for communities in a world tracking towards low emissions.

*   **Assumption:** neighbourhood disadvantage is higher where high social vulnerability and intensified hazards for very rare events converge.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** Median values provide a central probabilistic outlook but should be viewed alongside absolute extremes for emergency planning.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
