The field **'Summer average maximum temperature moderate emissions 2010-2030 median'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp45_2019_50p`) represents the central estimate of projected average daily maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2010–2030 under a moderate emissions scenario (RCP 4.5).

### **Description**
This represents the **50th percentile (median)** estimate for current daytime maximum temperatures. It provides a likely estimate of the heat hazard exposure for communities today.

*   **Assumption:** Increased heat exposure leads to higher social disadvantage when it coincides with neighbourhoods containing sensitive populations.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** It represents a 20-year average centered on 2020 on a 25km grid, meaning it may not capture local heat islands.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
