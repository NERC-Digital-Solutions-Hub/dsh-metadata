The field **'Summer average temperature high emissions 2040-2060 90th percentile'** (internal name `tas_rcp60_2049_90p`) represents the high estimate of projected summer average temperatures in Wales for the 2050s under a high emissions scenario (RCP 6.0).

### Description

This metric represents the **90th percentile** estimate, providing a high-end projection for mid-century average temperatures. It identifies areas of acute future heat hazard if the climate is highly sensitive to rising greenhouse gas levels.

* **Assumption:** Higher temperatures correlate with an increased chance of hazardous heat events, worsening potential well-being losses for sensitive populations.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** This probabilistic high-end estimate is used for cautious long-term planning, as it represents a scenario with only a 10% chance of being exceeded.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
