The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 2010-2030 median'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp45_2019_50p`) represents the standardized central estimate (median) of current average daily maximum summer temperatures under RCP 4.5.

### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized measure of the likely current daytime maximum temperatures. Standardizing across all scenarios identifies neighbourhoods that are currently "relatively hot" compared to the dataset mean across all time periods.

* **Assumption:** Increased heat exposure leads to higher social disadvantage when it coincides with neighbourhoods containing sensitive populations.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** This identifies geographical hotspots of relative hazard but does not quantify absolute physical risk without social data.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** current period maximum temperature projections.
