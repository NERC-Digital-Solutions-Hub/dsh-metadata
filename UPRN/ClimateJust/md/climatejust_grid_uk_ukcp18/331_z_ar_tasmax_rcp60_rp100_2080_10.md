#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the 10th percentile projection for the 2080 window (2070–2090) during an exceptionally rare 1% annual-probability heat event into a standardized score. It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face the minimum expected intensification of these relative hazards toward the end of the century in a world where global greenhouse gas emissions peak mid-century.

* **Assumption:** Mapping future relative hazard distributions helps prioritize long-term resilient infrastructure for the most vulnerable communities.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Late-century projections carry significant probabilistic uncertainty and do not reflect absolute physical risk in Celsius.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** late-century maximum temperature projections.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
