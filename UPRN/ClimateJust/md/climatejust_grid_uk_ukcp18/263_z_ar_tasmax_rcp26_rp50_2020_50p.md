The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 50 year return period 2020 median' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp26_rp50_2020_50p) represents the standardized central estimate of current temperatures during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave under RCP 2.6.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score provides a standardized measure of the likely current severity of a 2% annual-chance daytime heat event in a world tracking towards low global emissions.
 Standardizing across all scenarios identifies neighbourhoods where current rare extreme hazards are "relatively severe" compared to all database years and scenarios.
Assumption: Higher relative temperatures denote a greater potential for residents to experience harmful heat events, driving up geographical disadvantage.
Vulnerability Dimension: This is a standardized input for the Hazard Exposure component.
Caveat: Standardized scores simplify complex probabilistic data but do not reflect the absolute peak Celsius temperatures reached during a specific event.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 probabilistic projections.
