### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 50 year return period 2020 median

#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized measure of the likely current severity of a 2% annual-chance daytime heat event in a world tracking towards low global emissions. Standardizing across all scenarios identifies neighbourhoods where current rare extreme hazards are "relatively severe" compared to all database years and scenarios.

* **Assumption:** Higher relative temperatures denote a greater potential for residents to experience harmful heat events, driving up geographical disadvantage.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is a standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** component.
* **Caveat:** Standardized scores simplify complex probabilistic data but do not reflect the absolute peak Celsius temperatures reached during a specific event.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
