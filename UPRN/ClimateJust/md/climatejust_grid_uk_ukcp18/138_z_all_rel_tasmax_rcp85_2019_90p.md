The field 'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature very high emissions 2010-2030 90th percentile' (internal name z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp85_2019_90p) represents the standardized high estimate of current average daily maximum summer temperatures under RCP 8.5
.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score converts the near-term high-end daytime maximum projection into a standardized relative score
. It identifies where current relative daytime heat exposure could be significantly above the dataset average if global climate sensitivity is high in a world where emissions rise rapidly
.
Assumption: Higher relative maximum temperatures signify a greater potential for communities to come into contact with harmful heat events
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: This is a high estimate; actual current daytime maximums are statistically 90% likely to be below these values for this specific scenario
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 probabilistic maximum temperature data
.
