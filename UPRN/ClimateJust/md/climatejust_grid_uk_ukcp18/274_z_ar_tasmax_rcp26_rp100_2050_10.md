The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 100 year return period 2050 10th percentile' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp26_rp100_2050_10) represents the standardized low estimate of mid-century temperatures during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave under RCP 2.6.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score converts the 10th percentile mid-century projection for 1% annual-chance extreme events into a standardized score.
 It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face the minimum expected intensification of relative 100-year daytime heat hazards by the 2050s in a low emissions future.
Assumption: Understand the relative geographical distribution of future relative extreme hazards is necessary for ensured justice in adaptation planning.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure.
Caveat: Standardized scores simplify complex data but mask absolute Celsius values reached during individual events.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 mid-century probabilistic data.
