#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the 90th percentile (high estimate) of current daytime maximums during a 5% annual-chance heat event into a standardized relative score. It identifies neighbourhoods that would experience acute current extreme heat exposure relative to the entire database average if climate sensitivity is high in a world where global emissions peak around 2040.

* **Assumption:** Higher relative temperatures signify a greater potential for residents to experience catastrophic heat-related health and welfare impacts.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** This is a high estimate; actual temperatures are statistically 90% likely to be below these values for this specific scenario and period.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** probabilistic climate data.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
