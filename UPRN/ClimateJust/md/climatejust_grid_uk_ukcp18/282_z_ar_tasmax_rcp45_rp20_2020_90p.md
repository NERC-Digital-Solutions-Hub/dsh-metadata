The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 20 year return period 2020 90th percentile' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp45_rp20_2020_90p) represents the standardized high estimate of current temperatures during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave under RCP 4.5.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score converts the 90th percentile (high estimate) of current daytime maximums during a 5% annual-chance heat event into a standardized relative score
. It identifies neighbourhoods that would experience acute current extreme heat exposure relative to the entire database average if climate sensitivity is high in a world where global emissions peak around 2040
.
Assumption: Higher relative temperatures signify a greater potential for residents to experience catastrophic heat-related health and welfare impacts
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: This is a high estimate; actual temperatures are statistically 90% likely to be below these values for this specific scenario and period
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 probabilistic climate data
.
