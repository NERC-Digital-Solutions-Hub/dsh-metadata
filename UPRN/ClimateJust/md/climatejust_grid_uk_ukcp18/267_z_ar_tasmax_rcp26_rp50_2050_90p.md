The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 50 year return period 2050 90th percentile' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp26_rp50_2050_90p) represents the standardized high estimate of projected mid-century temperatures during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave under RCP 2.6.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score standardized the high-end mid-century projection for 2% annual-chance daytime maximums against all other database scenarios.
 It identifies neighbourhoods facing acute mid-century extreme heat exposure relative to the entire dataset even in a low global emissions scenario if climate response is high.
Assumption: Higher relative maximum temperatures indicate an increased exposure risk, which can cause severe well-being losses in sensitive communities.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure.
Caveat: High-end estimates represent a scenario that is only 10% likely to be exceeded according to the model.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 probabilistic climate projections.
