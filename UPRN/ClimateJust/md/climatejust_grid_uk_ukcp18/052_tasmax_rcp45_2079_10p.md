The field 'Summer average maximum temperature moderate emissions 2070-2090 10th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp45_2079_10p) represents the low estimate of projected average daily maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the late-century period (2070–2090) under RCP 4.5
.
Description
This metric provides the 10th percentile estimate for typical daytime highs at the end of the century
. It serves as a baseline for understanding the minimum expected shift in summer daytime maximums assuming a global transition to a low-carbon future begins after the 2030s
.
Assumption: Understanding the geographical distribution of future daytime maximum temperatures is necessary for long-term community resilience and justice in adaptation planning
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This indicator informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for the late-century time horizon
.
Caveat: This is a low estimate, meaning there is a 90% probability that actual average maximum temperatures for this scenario will be higher
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
