The field **'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature high emissions 50 year return period 2020 10th percentile'** (internal name `z_ar_tasmax_rcp60_rp50_2020_10p`) represents the standardized low estimate of current temperatures during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave under RCP 6.0.

### **Description**
This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the 10th percentile (**low estimate**) for current (2020 window) daytime maximums during an extremely rare 2% annual-probability extreme event into a standardized relative score. By standardizing across all warming levels in the database, it highlights neighbourhoods where the minimum expected current relative hazard for these events is relatively low.

* **Assumption:** Neighbourhood disadvantage is assumed to be higher where high social vulnerability and intensified relative hazards for rare events converge.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** These absolute temperature values are statistically 90% likely to be higher than recorded in this field.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** current period probabilistic maximum temperature projections.
