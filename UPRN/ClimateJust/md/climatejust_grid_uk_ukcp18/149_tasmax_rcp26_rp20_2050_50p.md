The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature low emissions 20 year return period 2050 median'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp26_rp20_2050_50p`) represents the central estimate (median) of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period under RCP 2.6.

### **Description**
This metric provides a standardized "likely" mid-century projection for typical extreme daytime highs that have a 5% annual chance of occurrence. It maps the intensification of rare heat hazards by the 2050s, identifying neighbourhoods likely to face a significant shift in their extreme temperature profile.

*   **Assumption:** Higher average maximum temperatures during extreme events indicate an increased exposure risk, which can cause severe well-being losses in sensitive communities.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is a primary input for the **Hazard Exposure** component for the mid-century.
*   **Caveat:** Median estimates are recommended starting points but do not account for absolute "worst-case" climate response.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
