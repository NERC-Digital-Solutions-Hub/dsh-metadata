The field 'Summer average maximum temperature moderate emissions 2010-2030 10th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp45_2019_10p) represents the low estimate of projected average daily maximum temperatures during summer in Wales for the period 2010–2030 under a moderate emissions scenario (RCP 4.5)
.
Description
This metric evaluates near-term Hazard Exposure by providing the 10th percentile low estimate for typical daytime highs under moderate emissions
.
Assumption: Higher average daytime highs indicate a baseline of exposure that can lead to losses in community well-being
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: While this is a low estimate for the moderate emissions path, actual temperatures are statistically 90% likely to be higher than these values
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
