The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature low emissions 50 year return period 2020 90th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp26_rp50_2020_90p`) represents the high estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave for the current period under RCP 2.6.

### **Description**
This metric identifies the **90th percentile** high estimate for daytime maximums during a very rare heat event today. It maps areas facing acute current extreme heat exposure if the climate system responds more aggressively to low emissions than central estimates predict.

*   **Assumption:** Higher temperatures signify a greater potential for residents to experience catastrophic heat-related health impacts.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** Values in this field are 90% likely to be below the actual temperature for this scenario and period according to the probabilistic model.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
