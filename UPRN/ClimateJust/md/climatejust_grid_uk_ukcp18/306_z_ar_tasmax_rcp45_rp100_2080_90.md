The field **'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 100 year return period 2080 90th percentile'** (internal name `z_ar_tasmax_rcp45_rp100_2080_90`) represents the standardized high estimate of projected late-century temperatures during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave under RCP 4.5.

### **Description**
This **"All-Relative" Z-score** standardized the high-end projection for exceptionally rare 1% annual-chance heat events at the end of the century against all other dataset scenarios. It identifies geographical areas potentially facing catastrophic late-century relative extreme heat hazards if global climate patterns are more sensitive to greenhouse gases than central estimates suggest.

* **Assumption:** Higher relative temperatures denote higher baseline exposure that can lead to significant community well-being losses.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Statistically, there is only a 10% probability that actual temperatures during such an event would exceed these values.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** late-century maximum temperature data.
