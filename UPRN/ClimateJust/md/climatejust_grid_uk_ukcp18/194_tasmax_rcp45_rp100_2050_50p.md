The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature moderate emissions 100 year return period 2050 median'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp45_rp100_2050_50p`) represents the central estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period under RCP 4.5.

### Description

This metric provides a standardized **50th percentile (median)** projection for daytime extreme maximums that have a 1% annual probability of occurrence in the 2050s. It identifies neighbourhoods likely to face significantly more severe rare heat hazards by mid-century, especially where intensified future heat hazards and high social vulnerability may converge.

* **Assumption:** Higher extreme temperatures indicate an increased exposure risk, which can lead to negative impacts on community well-being.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is a primary input for the **Hazard Exposure** component for the mid-century.
* **Caveat:** While the median is a central estimate, absolute peak temperatures during an event may still exceed these averaged projections.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
