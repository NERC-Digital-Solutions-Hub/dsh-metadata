The field **'Summer average temperature very high emissions 2070-2090 90th percentile'** (internal name `tas_rcp85_2079_90p`) represents the high estimate of projected summer average temperatures in Wales for the period 2070–2090 under a very high emissions scenario (RCP 8.5).

### Description

This represents the most extreme high-warming pathway in the database, providing the **90th percentile** projection. It identifies areas of potentially catastrophic future heat hazard if climate response to very high emissions is high.

* **Assumption:** Neighbourhoods with high temperatures in this scenario identify where climate change is likely to most significantly increase social inequalities and negative well-being outcomes.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** This probabilistic high-end estimate represents the top 10% of likely temperature outcomes for this specific extreme late-century scenario.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
