The field **'Summer average temperature high emissions 2010-2030 90th percentile'** (internal name `tas_rcp60_2019_90p`) represents the high estimate of projected daily average temperatures during summer months in Wales for the period 2010–2030 under a high emissions scenario (RCP 6.0).

### **Description**
This metric evaluates **Hazard Exposure** by providing the 90th percentile estimate, which identifies areas that could experience significant heat exposure even in the near term if climate sensitivity is high under high global emissions.

*   **Assumption:** Areas with higher projected average temperatures face a greater chance of residents coming into contact with harmful heat-related events, leading to an increased risk of well-being loss.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension, which is used alongside socio-spatial vulnerability indices to determine overall **Heat Disadvantage**.
*   **Caveat:** This is a high-end probabilistic estimate; there is a 10% probability that the actual average temperature for this period and scenario will exceed this value.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** (United Kingdom Climate Projections 2018) probabilistic projections.
