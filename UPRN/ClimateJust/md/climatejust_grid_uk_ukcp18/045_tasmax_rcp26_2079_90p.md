The field **'Summer average maximum temperature low emissions 2070-2090 90th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp26_2079_90p`) represents the high estimate of projected average maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2070–2090 under a low emissions scenario (RCP 2.6).

### Description

This represents the **90th percentile** projection, identifying a high-end daytime temperature scenario for the late 21st century.

* **Assumption:** Areas with the highest projected maximum temperatures face the greatest challenge in protecting residents from heat-related health impacts.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** This is a probabilistic high-estimate and represents the top 10% of likely temperature outcomes for this specific scenario.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
