The field **'Summer average temperature high emissions 2040-2060 median'** (internal name `tas_rcp60_2049_50p`) represents the central estimate of projected average temperatures for the period 2040–2060 in Wales under a high emissions scenario (RCP 6.0).

### Description

This metric provides the **50th percentile (median)** projection for mid-century summer temperatures. It identifies areas with a likely chance of coming into contact with significantly higher baseline temperatures as a result of a high-emissions climate pathway.

* **Assumption:** Intensifying heat hazards increase the potential for negative health and welfare impacts, especially in neighbourhoods where personal and social factors already limit adaptive capacity.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is a primary input for the **Hazard Exposure** component of the framework.
* **Caveat:** It represents an average over 20 years and does not reflect the specific peak temperatures reached during extreme heatwaves within that window.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
