The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature high emissions 50 year return period 2020 median'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp60_rp50_2020_50p`) represents the central estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave for the current period under RCP 6.0.

### **Description**
This metric provides the **50th percentile (median)** estimate for daytime extreme maximums that have a 2% annual chance of occurrence today. It identifies the "likely" current severity of these extremely rare heat events for communities today in a high emissions scenario.

*   **Assumption:** Increased exposure to extreme heat leads to higher social disadvantage when it coincides with neighbourhoods containing elderly residents, children, or those in ill health.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** Median values provide a central probabilistic outlook but should be viewed alongside absolute high-end projections for emergency planning.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
