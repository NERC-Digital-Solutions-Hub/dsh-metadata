#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** standardized the high-end projection for exceptionally rare 1% annual-chance heat events at the end of the century against all other dataset scenarios. It identifies areas potentially facing catastrophic late-century relative extreme heat hazards relative to the entire dataset average if the climate responds aggressively to high emissions.

* **Assumption:** Positive relative Z-scores highlight neighbourhoods where future climate change is likely to most significantly increase social inequalities.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Statistically, there is only a 10% probability that actual temperatures during such an event would exceed these high-end values.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** late-century maximum temperature data.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
