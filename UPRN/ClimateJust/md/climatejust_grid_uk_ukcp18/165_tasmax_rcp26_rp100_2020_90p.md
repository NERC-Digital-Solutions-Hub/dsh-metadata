The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature low emissions 100 year return period 2020 90th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp26_rp100_2020_90p`) represents the high estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave for the current period under RCP 2.6.

### Description

This metric identifies the **90th percentile** high estimate for daytime maximums during an extremely rare (1% annual chance) heat event today. It maps areas facing acute current heat exposure if the climate system responds more aggressively to greenhouse gases than central estimates predict.

* **Assumption:** Higher temperatures signify a greater potential for residents to experience catastrophic heat-related health impacts.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Values in this field are statistically 90% likely to be higher than the actual average temperatures for this specific period.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
