The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 20 year return period 2080 90th percentile' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp45_rp20_2080_90p) represents the standardized high estimate of projected late-century temperatures during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave under RCP 4.5.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score standardized the high-end projection for 5% annual-chance heat events at the end of the century against all other dataset scenarios
. It identifies areas potentially facing catastrophic late-century relative extreme heat hazards relative to the entire dataset average if climate sensitivity is high
.
Assumption: Neighbourhoods with the highest relative maximum temperatures face the greatest challenge in protecting sensitive residents from heat stress
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: Statistically, there is only a 10% probability that actual temperatures during such an event would exceed these values
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 late-century maximum temperature data
.
