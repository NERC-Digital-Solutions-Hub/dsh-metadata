The field 'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature moderate emissions 2040-2060 90th percentile' (internal name z_all_rel_tas_rcp45_2049_90p) represents the standardized high estimate of projected mid-century summer temperatures under RCP 4.5.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score converts the 90th percentile mid-century projection into a standardized relative score. It identifies where heat hazard exposure may become acute by the 2050s relative to the entire dataset if climate sensitivity is high
.
Assumption: Higher relative temperatures indicate an increased exposure risk, which can lead to negative impacts on community well-being
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: This is a probabilistic high-estimate, representing a scenario with only a 10% chance of being exceeded
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
