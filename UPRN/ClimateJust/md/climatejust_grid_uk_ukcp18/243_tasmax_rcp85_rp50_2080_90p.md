The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature very high emissions 50 year return period 2080 90th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp85_rp50_2080_90p) represents the high estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period under RCP 8.5
.
Description
This metric represents the 90th percentile high estimate, providing a cautious "worst-case" projection for very rare daytime extreme events at the end of the century
. It identifies where late-century extreme heat hazards could become catastrophically severe if the climate responds aggressively to very high global emissions
.
Assumption: Neighbourhood disadvantage is statistically higher where high social vulnerability and acute extreme heat hazards coincide
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for future scenarios
.
Caveat: High-end estimates represent a scenario that is only 10% likely to be exceeded according to the probabilistic model
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
