The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature very high emissions 2010-2030 10th percentile'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tas_rcp85_2019_10p`) represents the standardized low estimate of projected summer average temperatures for the current period under a very high emissions scenario.

### **Description**
This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the near-term 10th percentile estimate for a very high emissions pathway (RCP 8.5) into a standardized value. By standardizing across all possible warming scenarios, it maps the minimum expected baseline relative hazard for communities today.

*   **Assumption:** Higher relative baseline temperatures indicate a baseline of exposure that can lead to losses in community well-being.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
*   **Caveat:** Actual current temperatures are statistically 90% likely to be higher than these values for this extreme emissions scenario.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
