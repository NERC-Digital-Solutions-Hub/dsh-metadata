The field **'Summer average maximum temperature low emissions 2010-2030 10th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp26_2019_10p`) represents the low estimate of projected average daily maximum temperatures during summer in Wales for the period 2010–2030 under a low emissions scenario (RCP 2.6).

### Description

This metric evaluates **Hazard Exposure** by identifying the expected temperature at the hottest part of a typical summer's day. Within the framework, this value represents the 10th percentile low estimate.

* **Assumption:** Areas with higher projected average maximum temperatures face a greater chance of residents coming into contact with harmful heat-related events.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** This is a low estimate, meaning there is a 90% probability that actual average maximum temperatures will be higher than these values.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
