### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature very high emissions 100 year return period 2080 90th percentile

#### Description

This standardizes high-end late-century projections for rare heat events.

* **Assumption:** Highlights areas of extreme future risk.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Only a 10% exceedance probability.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** projections.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
