The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature low emissions 50 year return period 2080 10th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp26_rp50_2080_10p) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period (2080 window) under RCP 2.6
.
Description
This projection maps the 10th percentile (low estimate) for very rare extreme heat events (2% annual probability) toward the end of the century
. It provides a long-term outlook for the minimum expected intensification of catastrophic heat hazards across Wales in a global low-emissions future
.
Assumption: mapping future rare hazard distributions helps prioritize long-term resilient infrastructure for vulnerable communities
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for the late-century
.
Caveat: late-century projections carry significant probabilistic uncertainty and carry higher levels of uncertainty due to the long time scales
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
