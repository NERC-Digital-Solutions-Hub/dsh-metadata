The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 50 year return period 2080 90th percentile' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp45_rp50_2080_90p) represents the standardized high estimate of projected late-century temperatures during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave under RCP 4.5.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score standardized the high-end projection for extremely rare 2% annual-chance heat events at the end of the century
. It identifyies geographical areas potentially facing catastrophic late-century relative extreme heat hazards relative to the entire dataset average if the climate responds aggressively
.
Assumption: Neighbourhoods with the highest relative maximum temperatures face the greatest challenge in protecting sensitive residents from catastrophic health impacts
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: There is only a 10% probability that actual temperatures during such an event would exceed these values
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 late-century maximum temperature projections
.
