The field **'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature high emissions 20 year return period 2050 median'** (internal name `z_ar_tasmax_rcp60_rp20_2050_50p`) represents the standardized central estimate of projected mid-century temperatures during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave under RCP 6.0.

### **Description**
This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized **"likely"** mid-century projection for extreme daytime maximums that have a 5% annual probability of occurrence. Standardizing across all scenarios identifies neighbourhoods likely to face a significant permanent shift in their relative extreme heat profile by mid-century as a result of high global emissions.

* **Assumption:** Neighbourhood disadvantage is most acute where high socio-spatial vulnerability and acute increases in projected relative hazards converge.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Median values provide a central probabilistic outlook but absolute peak temperatures during a specific event may still be higher.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century maximum temperature projections.
