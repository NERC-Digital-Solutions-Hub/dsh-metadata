The field 'Summer average maximum temperature very high emissions 2070-2090 median' (internal name tasmax_rcp85_2079_50p) represents the central estimate of projected average daily maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2070–2090 under RCP 8.5
.
Description
This represents the 50th percentile (median) projection for late-century typical daytime maximums
. It identifyies neighbourhoods likely to face the most acute intensification of heat hazards in a world of unmitigated global emissions
.
Assumption: positive values denote neighbourhoods where extreme daytime heat hazards will be significantly higher than current experience, requiring intensive adaptation
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: It represents an average temperature at the hottest part of the day over a 20-year window
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
