The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature high emissions 100 year return period 2050 median' (internal name tasmax_rcp60_rp100_2050_50p) represents the central estimate of the expected temperature at the hottest part of the day during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period under a high emissions scenario (RCP 6.0).
Description
This metric evaluates Hazard Exposure by identifying the likely severity of an extremely rare heat event—one with only a 1% annual probability of occurring (a "100-year return period")
. Within the framework, it provides the 50th percentile (median) projection for these rare events in the 2050 window (the 20-year average from 2040–2060), assuming a future where global greenhouse gas emissions peak around 2060
.
Assumption: Areas with higher projected temperatures during extremely rare heatwaves face a greater risk of catastrophic impacts on community health and well-being if social vulnerability is high
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This indicator informs the Hazard Exposure dimension, used to determine overall Heat Disadvantage
.
Caveat: These probabilistic estimates represent conditions over a 20-year time window and do not reflect absolute peak temperatures during an individual event
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 (United Kingdom Climate Projections 2018) probabilistic projections
.
