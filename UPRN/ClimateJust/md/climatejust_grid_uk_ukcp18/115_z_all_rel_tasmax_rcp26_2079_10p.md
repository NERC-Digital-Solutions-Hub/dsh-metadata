The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 2070-2090 10th percentile'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp26_2079_10p`) represents the standardized low estimate of late-century average daily maximum summer temperatures under RCP 2.6.

### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the 10th percentile projection for the 2080s (2070–2090) into a standardized score. It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face the minimum expected intensification of relative daytime heat hazards toward the end of the century in a world with successful carbon mitigation.

* **Assumption:** Understand the relative distribution of future daytime maximums is essential for ensuring justice in adaptation planning.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Late-century projections carry significant probabilistic uncertainty and carry higher levels of uncertainty due to the long time scales.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** late-century maximum temperature projections.
