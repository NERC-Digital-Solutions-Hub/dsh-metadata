The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature moderate emissions 20 year return period 2080 median'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp45_rp20_2080_50p`) represents the central estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period under RCP 4.5.

### **Description**
This metric provides the **50th percentile (median)** estimate for the severity of a 5% annual-chance extreme temperature event at the end of the century. It identifies neighbourhoods likely to face extreme intensification of rare extreme heat hazards by the 2080s even with global transition to a low-carbon future.

*   **Assumption:** Neighbourhoods projected to have the highest extreme temperatures face the greatest challenge in protecting sensitive residents from negative health impacts.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for future scenarios.
*   **Caveat:** Values represent the average over the 20-year time period centered on 2080 (2070–2090).
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
