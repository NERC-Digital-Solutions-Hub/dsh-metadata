The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature low emissions 20 year return period 2050 90th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp26_rp20_2050_90p`) represents the high estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period under RCP 2.6.

### **Description**
This metric represents the **90th percentile** high estimate, providing a cautious projection for rare daytime extreme events by the 2050s. It identifies where mid-century extreme heat hazard exposure may become acute even under a low emissions pathway if global climate sensitivity is high.

*   **Assumption:** neighbourhood disadvantage is statistically higher where high social vulnerability and high extreme heat hazards coincide.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for future scenarios.
*   **Caveat:** This is a high-end estimate; there is only a 10% probability that the actual extreme temperature for this period and scenario will exceed this value.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
