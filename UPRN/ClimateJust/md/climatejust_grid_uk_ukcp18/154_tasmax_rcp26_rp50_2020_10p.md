The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature low emissions 50 year return period 2020 10th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp26_rp50_2020_10p) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature at the hottest part of the day during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave in the current period
.
Description
This metric evaluates Hazard Exposure for a "50-year return period" event—an extremely rare temperature that has only a 2% annual probability of occurring (1 in 50 chance)
. This value represents the 10th percentile low estimate for the 2020 window (2010–2030) under a low emissions scenario
.
Assumption: Areas with higher projected temperatures during very rare extreme heatwaves face a greater risk of catastrophic impacts on community health and well-being
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: These values represent the average condition over the 20-year time period about the central year of 2020
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
