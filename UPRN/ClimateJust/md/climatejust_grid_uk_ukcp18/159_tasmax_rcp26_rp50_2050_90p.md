The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature low emissions 50 year return period 2050 90th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp26_rp50_2050_90p`) represents the high estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period under RCP 2.6.

### **Description**
This metric represents the **90th percentile** high estimate, providing a cautious projection for very rare daytime extreme events by mid-century under a low emissions pathway. It identifies where rare extreme heat hazards could become catastrophically severe if climate response to greenhouse gases is high.

*   **Assumption:** neighbourhood disadvantage is statistically higher where high social vulnerability and acute rare hazards coincide.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for future scenarios.
*   **Caveat:** This is a probabilistic high-estimate, representing a scenario with only a 10% chance of being exceeded.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
