The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature high emissions 2070-2090 90th percentile'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tas_rcp60_2079_90p`) represents the standardized high estimate of late-century summer average temperatures under RCP 6.0.

### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the 90th percentile high estimate for the 2080s into a standardized relative value. It identifyies where late-century relative heat hazard exposure could become catastrophic under high global emissions if climate response is high.

* **Assumption:** Neighbourhoods with high relative temperatures identify where climate change is likely to most significantly increase social inequalities.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** This probabilistic high-end estimate represents the top 10% of likely temperature outcomes for this specific late-century scenario.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** late-century projections.
