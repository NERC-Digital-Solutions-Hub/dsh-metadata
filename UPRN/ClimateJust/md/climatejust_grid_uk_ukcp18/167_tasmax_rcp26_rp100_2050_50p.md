The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature low emissions 100 year return period 2050 median'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp26_rp100_2050_50p`) represents the central estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period under RCP 2.6.

### **Description**
This metric provides a standardized **50th percentile (median)** projection for daytime extreme maximums that have a 1% annual probability of occurrence. It identifies neighbourhoods likely to face significantly more severe rare heat hazards by the 2050s, identifying where intensified future heat hazards and high social vulnerability may converge.

*   **Assumption:** Higher extreme temperatures indicate an increased exposure risk, which can lead to negative impacts on community well-being.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is a primary input for the **Hazard Exposure** component for the mid-century.
*   **Caveat:** While the median is a central estimate, absolute peak temperatures during an event may still exceed these averaged projections.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
