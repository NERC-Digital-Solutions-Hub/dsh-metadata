The field 'Summer average maximum temperature low emissions 2070-2090 median' (internal name tasmax_rcp26_2079_50p) represents the central estimate of projected summer average maximum temperatures in Wales for the period 2070–2090 under a low emissions scenario (RCP 2.6)
.
Description
This provides the 50th percentile (median) estimate for average daily daytime maximums at the end of the century
.
Assumption: Neighbourhoods projected to have the highest daytime highs face the greatest risk of well-being losses if they also have high biophysical sensitivity
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: It represents an average temperature at the hottest part of the day across all summer months over a 20-year period
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
