The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature low emissions 2070-2090 10th percentile'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tas_rcp26_2079_10p`) represents the standardized low estimate of summer average temperatures for the late-century period under RCP 2.6.

### **Description**
This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the 10th percentile (low estimate) for the 2080s (2070–2090) into a standardized score. It allows late-century temperature projections under a low-emissions pathway to be compared statistically against current baseline temperatures.

*   **Assumption:** Understanding the relative geographical distribution of future heat hazards is necessary for long-term community resilience and justice in adaptation planning.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
*   **Caveat:** Late-century projections carry higher levels of uncertainty due to the long time scales involved.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** late-century temperature data.
