The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature low emissions 20 year return period 2020 median'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp26_rp20_2020_50p`) represents the central estimate (median) of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the current period under RCP 2.6.

### Description

This metric provides the central central estimate for daytime extreme temperatures that have a 5% annual probability of occurrence. Within the Socio-Spatial Climate Vulnerability framework, it identifies the likely current severity of rare heat events for communities today in a world tracking towards low global emissions.

* **Assumption:** Increased exposure to extreme heat leads to higher social disadvantage when it coincides with neighbourhoods containing elderly residents, children, or those in ill health.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** While the median is a central estimate, it represents a middle point in a range of probabilistic climate responses.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
