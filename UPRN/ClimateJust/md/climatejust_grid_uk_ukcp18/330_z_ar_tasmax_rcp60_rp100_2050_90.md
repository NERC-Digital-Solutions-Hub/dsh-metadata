#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** standardized the high-end mid-century projection for exceptionally rare 1% annual-chance daytime maximums against all other database scenarios. It identifies neighbourhoods facing acute mid-century extreme heat hazard exposure relative to the entire dataset average if climate response to greenhouse gases is more aggressive than central estimates predict.

* **Assumption:** Higher relative maximum temperatures signify a greater potential for residents to experience catastrophic heat-related impacts.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** This is a probabilistic high-estimate, representing a scenario with only a 10% chance of being exceeded.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century probabilistic projections.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
