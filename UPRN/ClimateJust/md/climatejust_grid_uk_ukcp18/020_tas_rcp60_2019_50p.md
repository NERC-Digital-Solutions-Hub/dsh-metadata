The field **'Summer average temperature high emissions 2010-2030 median'** (internal name `tas_rcp60_2019_50p`) represents the central estimate of projected average summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2010–2030 under a high emissions scenario (RCP 6.0).

### Description

This metric evaluates **Hazard Exposure** by providing the 50th percentile (median) estimate for daily average temperatures (averaged across June, July, and August). Within the Socio-Spatial Climate Vulnerability framework, it identifies the "likely" near-term baseline for summer temperatures in a world where greenhouse gas emissions continue to rise and peak around 2060.

* **Assumption:** Areas with higher projected average temperatures face a greater chance of residents coming into contact with heat-related hazard events, which can lead to negative impacts on health and well-being.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension, which is used alongside socio-spatial vulnerability indices to determine overall **Heat Disadvantage**.
* **Caveat:** These probabilistic estimates are calculated over a 25km grid and represent averages over a 20-year window centered on 2020; they do not reflect specific peak temperatures during individual extreme heatwave events.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** (United Kingdom Climate Projections 2018) probabilistic projections.
