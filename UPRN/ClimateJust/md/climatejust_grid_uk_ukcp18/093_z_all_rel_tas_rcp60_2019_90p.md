The field 'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature high emissions 2010-2030 90th percentile' (internal name z_all_rel_tas_rcp60_2019_90p) represents the standardized high estimate of summer average temperatures for the current period under RCP 6.0.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score converts the near-term high-end projection into a standardized score. It identifies where current relative heat exposure could be significantly above the dataset average if climate sensitivity to high greenhouse gas levels is high
.
Assumption: Higher relative temperatures indicate an increased exposure risk, which can cause well-being losses in sensitive communities
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: This is a high-end projection; values in this field are likely to be above the actual average temperature 90% of the time
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 current period projections
.
