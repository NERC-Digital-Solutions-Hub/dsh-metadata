The field **'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature high emissions 20 year return period 2050 10th percentile'** (internal name `z_ar_tasmax_rcp60_rp20_2050_10p`) represents the standardized low estimate of mid-century temperatures during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave under RCP 6.0.

### **Description**
This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the 10th percentile mid-century projection for 5% annual-chance extreme events into a standardized score. It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face the minimum expected intensification of relative 20-year daytime heat hazards by the 2050s in a world where global emissions continue to follow a high trajectory.

* **Assumption:** Understanding the relative geographical distribution of future relative extreme hazards is necessary for ensuring justice in adaptation planning.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Standardized scores simplify complex data but mask absolute Celsius values reached during individual events.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century probabilistic projections.
