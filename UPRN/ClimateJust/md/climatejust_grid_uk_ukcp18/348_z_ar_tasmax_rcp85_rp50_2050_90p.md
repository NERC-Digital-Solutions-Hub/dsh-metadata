#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** standardized the **90th percentile (high estimate)** mid-century projection for 2% annual-chance extreme events against all other database scenarios. It identifies geographical areas facing acute mid-century extreme heat exposure relative to the dataset average.

* **Assumption:** Higher relative maximum temperatures equate to increased exposure risk.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** This is a probabilistic high-estimate, representing a scenario with only a 10% chance of being exceeded.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century probabilistic data.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
