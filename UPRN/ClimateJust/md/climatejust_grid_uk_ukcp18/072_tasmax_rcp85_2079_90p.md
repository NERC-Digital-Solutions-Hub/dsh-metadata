The field **'Summer average maximum temperature very high emissions 2070-2090 90th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp85_2079_90p`) represents the high estimate of projected average daily maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2070–2090 under RCP 8.5.

### Description

This represents the most extreme high-warming pathway in the database, providing the **90th percentile** projection for the hottest part of typical summer days. It identifies areas of potentially catastrophic daytime heat hazard by the end of the century.

* **Assumption:** neighbourhoods with the highest temperatures in this scenario identify where climate change is likely to most significantly increase social inequalities.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** This high-end estimate represents the top 10% of likely outcomes for this specific extreme late-century scenario.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
