The field **'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature high emissions 20 year return period 2080 median'** (internal name `z_ar_tasmax_rcp60_rp20_2080_50p`) represents the standardized central estimate of projected late-century temperatures during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave under RCP 6.0.

### **Description**
This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized measure of the likely late-century hottest part of a typical 5% annual-chance extreme summer day. Positive values identify neighbourhoods likely to face intensified relative daytime extreme hazards by late century even in a scenario where global emissions peak mid-century.

* **Assumption:** Positive relative Z-scores highlight neighbourhoods where future climate change is likely to most significantly increase social inequalities.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Standardized scores simplify complex probabilistic data but do not reflect the absolute peak Celsius temperatures reached during an individual event.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** late-century maximum temperature projections.
