The field 'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature low emissions 2070-2090 median' (internal name z_all_rel_tas_rcp26_2079_50p) represents the standardized central estimate of late-century summer temperatures under RCP 2.6.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score provides a standardized measure of the "likely" late-century summer temperatures in a world that has successfully transitioned to low emissions. It identifies how these future averages compare to the mean temperature calculated across all years and scenarios in the database
.
Assumption: Values around 0 denote that projected temperatures are around the average for the entire database, helping to identify areas with the least expected intensification of heat hazards
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This is a standardized input for the Hazard Exposure component
.
Caveat: Standardized scores simplify complex data but mask absolute Celsius values
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 summer average temperature projections
.
