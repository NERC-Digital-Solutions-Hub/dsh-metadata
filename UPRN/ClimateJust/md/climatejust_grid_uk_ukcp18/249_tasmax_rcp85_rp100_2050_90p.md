#### Description

This metric represents the **90th percentile** high estimate, providing a cautious projection for extremely rare daytime events by mid-century. It identifyies where rare extreme heat hazards could become catastrophically severe if climate response to very high greenhouse gas levels is high.

* **Assumption:** Neighbourhood disadvantage is statistically higher where high social vulnerability and acute extreme heat hazards coincide.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for future scenarios.
* **Caveat:** High estimates represent a scenario that is only 10% likely to be exceeded according to the probabilistic model.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
