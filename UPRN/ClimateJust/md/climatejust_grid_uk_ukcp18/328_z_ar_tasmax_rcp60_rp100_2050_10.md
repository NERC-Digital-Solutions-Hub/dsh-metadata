#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the 10th percentile mid-century projection for 1% annual-chance extreme heat events into a standardized score. It identifies neighbourhoods where the minimum expected intensification of these exceptionally rare relative hazards by the 2050s remains relatively low compared to the entire spectrum of climate futures in the database.

*   **Assumption:** Mapping future relative hazard distributions is necessary for just adaptation and resilient infrastructure planning.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
*   **Caveat:** Estimates represent average conditions over a 20-year window (2040–2060) centered on 2050.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century probabilistic data.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
