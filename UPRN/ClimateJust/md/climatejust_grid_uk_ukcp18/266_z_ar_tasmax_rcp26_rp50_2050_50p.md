The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 50 year return period 2050 median' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp26_rp50_2050_50p) represents the standardized central estimate of projected mid-century temperatures during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave under RCP 2.6.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score provides a standardized "likely" mid-century projection for extreme daytime maximums that have a 2% annual probability of occurrence.
 Standardizing across all scenarios allows these future rare hazards to be mapped on the same statistical scale as the current baseline and more extreme warming scenarios.
Assumption: neighbourhood disadvantage is most acute where high socio-spatial vulnerability and acute increases in projected relative hazards converge.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for the Hazard Exposure assessment.
Caveat: Standardized scores identify geographical hotspots of hazard but mask absolute physical risk values.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 mid-century maximum temperature projections.
