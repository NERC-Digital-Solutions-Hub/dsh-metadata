#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized "likely" mid-century projection for extreme daytime maximums that have a 2% annual probability of occurrence. Standardizing across all scenarios allows these future rare hazards to be mapped on the same statistical scale as the current baseline and more extreme warming scenarios.

* **Assumption:** Neighbourhood disadvantage is most acute where high socio-spatial vulnerability and acute increases in projected relative hazards converge.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** assessment.
* **Caveat:** Standardized scores identify geographical hotspots of hazard but mask absolute physical risk values.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century maximum temperature projections.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
