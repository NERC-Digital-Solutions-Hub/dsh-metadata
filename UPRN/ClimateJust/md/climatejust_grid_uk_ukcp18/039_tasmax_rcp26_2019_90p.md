The field **'Summer average maximum temperature low emissions 2010-2030 90th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp26_2019_90p`) represents the high estimate of projected average maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2010–2030 under a low emissions scenario (RCP 2.6).

### Description

This metric represents the **90th percentile** estimate, providing a high-end projection for typical summer daytime highs in the current period.

* **Assumption:** Higher projected daytime temperatures correlate with a greater likelihood of hazardous extreme heat events that can impact community well-being.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** This is a high estimate; values in this field are likely to be above the actual average maximum temperature 90% of the time.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
