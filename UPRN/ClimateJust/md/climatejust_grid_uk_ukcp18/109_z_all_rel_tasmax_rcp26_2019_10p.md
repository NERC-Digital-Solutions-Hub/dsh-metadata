The field 'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 2010-2030 10th percentile' (internal name z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp26_2019_10p) represents the standardized low estimate of current average daily maximum summer temperatures under a low emissions scenario.
Description
This metric is an "All-Relative" Z-score, meaning it is calculated using the mean and standard deviation across all scenarios in the database
. It converts the 10th percentile (low estimate) for current (2010–2030) daytime maximums—the expected temperature at the hottest part of a typical summer's day—into a standardized score for relative comparison
.
Assumption: Positive scores denote higher relative daytime highs than average across the database, identifying current locations of relative heat hazard
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: This value represents the average over the 20-year window about 2020 and uses a 25km grid resolution
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 probabilistic maximum temperature data
.
