The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature high emissions 20 year return period 2080 10th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp60_rp20_2080_10p) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period under RCP 6.0
.
Description
This projection maps the 10th percentile (low estimate) for 5% annual-probability extreme heat events toward the end of the century
. It provides a long-term outlook for the minimum expected intensification of heat hazards across Wales in a world where global emissions begin to stabilize after 2060
.
Assumption: mapping future rare hazard distributions helps prioritize long-term resilient infrastructure for vulnerable communities
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for the late-century time horizon
.
Caveat: Late-century projections carry significant probabilistic uncertainty and carry higher levels of uncertainty due to the long time scales
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
