The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature high emissions 2070-2090 median'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp60_2079_50p`) represents the standardized central estimate of late-century average daily maximum summer temperatures under RCP 6.0.

### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized measure of the likely late-century hottest part of a typical summer's day. It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face extreme intensification of relative daytime maximums compared to the database average by the 2080s as a result of high global emissions.

* **Assumption:** Positive relative scores highlight neighbourhoods where future climate change is likely to most significantly increase social inequalities.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** component.
* **Caveat:** Values around 0 in this field denote that projected daytime highs are around the average for the entire database.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** late-century maximum temperature data.
