The field 'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature very high emissions 2070-2090 10th percentile' (internal name z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp85_2079_10p) represents the standardized low estimate of late-century average daily maximum summer temperatures under RCP 8.5
.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score converts the 10th percentile projection for the 2080s (2070–2090) into a standardized score
. It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face the minimum expected intensification of relative daytime heat hazards at the end of the century in a world of unmitigated global emissions growth
.
Assumption: Understanding the relative geographical distribution of future daytime highs is essential for ensure justice in adaptation planning
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: Late-century projections carry significantly higher levels of uncertainty due to the long time scales and complex climate feedback mechanisms
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 late-century maximum temperature projections
.
