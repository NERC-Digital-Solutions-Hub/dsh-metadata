The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature very high emissions 2040-2060 10th percentile'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tas_rcp85_2049_10p`) represents the standardized low estimate of projected summer average temperatures for mid-century under a very high emissions scenario.

### **Description**
This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the 10th percentile (low estimate) for mid-century (2040–2060) summer average temperatures under RCP 8.5 into a standardized score. It identifies neighbourhoods where the minimum expected mid-century heat hazard remains relatively low compared to the full spectrum of projected scenarios in the database.

*   **Assumption:** Mapping future relative hazard distributions helps prioritize long-term resilient infrastructure for vulnerable communities.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
*   **Caveat:** There is a 90% probability that actual average temperatures for this mid-century scenario will be higher than the values recorded in this field.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** probabilistic mid-century temperature projections.
