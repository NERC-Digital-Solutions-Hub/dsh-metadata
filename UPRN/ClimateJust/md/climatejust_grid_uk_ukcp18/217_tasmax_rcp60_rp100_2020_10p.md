The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature high emissions 100 year return period 2020 10th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp60_rp100_2020_10p`) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature at the hottest part of the day during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave in the current period under RCP 6.0.

### **Description**
This metric evaluates **Hazard Exposure** for a "100-year return period" event—a temperature so extreme it has only a 1% annual probability of occurring. It provides the **10th percentile** low estimate for the 2020 window (2010–2030), mapping the minimum expected severity of these extremely rare events for communities today.

*   **Assumption:** Higher extreme temperatures indicate an increased exposure risk, which can lead to severe losses in community well-being.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** This low estimate means there is a 90% probability that actual temperatures during such an event would be higher than the values recorded here.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
