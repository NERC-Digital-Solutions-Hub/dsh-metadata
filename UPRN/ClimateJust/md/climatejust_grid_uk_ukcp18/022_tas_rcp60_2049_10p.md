The field **'Summer average temperature high emissions 2040-2060 10th percentile'** (internal name `tas_rcp60_2049_10p`) represents the low estimate of projected summer average temperatures in Wales for the mid-century period (2040–2060) under a high emissions scenario (RCP 6.0).

### **Description**
This projection provides the **10th percentile** (low estimate) for mid-century temperatures. It is part of the **Hazard Exposure** suite used to map the long-term intensification of heat hazards under a high emissions trajectory.

*   **Assumption:** Higher temperatures represent a greater chance of exposure to heat hazards; combined with social vulnerability, this helps identify future areas of heat disadvantage.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for future scenarios.
*   **Caveat:** These probabilistic estimates are based on 25km grid cells and represent averages over a 20-year window centered on 2050.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
