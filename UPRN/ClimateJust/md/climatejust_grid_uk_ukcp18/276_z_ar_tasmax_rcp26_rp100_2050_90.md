The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 100 year return period 2050 90th percentile' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp26_rp100_2050_90) represents the standardized high estimate of projected mid-century temperatures during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave under RCP 2.6.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score standardized the high-end mid-century projection for 1% annual-chance extreme events against all other dataset scenarios.
 It identifies neighbourhoods facing acute mid-century extreme heat exposure relative to the entire dataset even in a low emissions world if global climate sensitivity is high.
Assumption: Higher relative maximum temperatures signify a greater potential for residents to experience catastrophic heat-related impacts.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure.
Caveat: This high estimate represents a scenario that is only 10% likely to be exceeded according to the probabilistic model.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 probabilistic projections.
