The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature low emissions 2040-2060 median'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tas_rcp26_2049_50p`) represents the standardized central estimate (median) of projected summer average temperatures for mid-century under RCP 2.6.

### **Description**
This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the median mid-century temperature projection into a standardized value. By standardizing across all scenarios, it places the likely summer temperatures of the 2050s on the same statistical scale as the recent past and more extreme future scenarios.

*   **Assumption:** neighbourhoods with higher relative scores are assumed to have a greater chance of exposure to heat hazards, driving up their total heat disadvantage when combined with social vulnerability.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is a standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** assessment.
*   **Caveat:** These probabilistic estimates represent averages over a 20-year window (2040–2060) and are calculated on a 25km grid.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century temperature projections.
