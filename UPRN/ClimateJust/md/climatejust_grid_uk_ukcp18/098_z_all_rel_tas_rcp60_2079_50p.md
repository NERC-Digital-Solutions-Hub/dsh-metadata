The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature high emissions 2070-2090 median'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tas_rcp60_2079_50p`) represents the standardized central estimate of late-century summer temperatures under RCP 6.0.

### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized "likely" estimate of late-century summer average temperatures. It identifyies neighbourhoods projected to face extreme intensification of relative heat hazards compared to the database average by the 2080s.

* **Assumption:** Higher relative average temperatures increase the frequency of heat-related well-being losses.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Values in this field represent averages over a 20-year window centered on 2080.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** late-century projections.
