The field 'Summer average maximum temperature moderate emissions 2010-2030 90th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp45_2019_90p) represents the high estimate of projected average daily maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2010–2030 under a moderate emissions scenario (RCP 4.5)
.
Description
This metric represents the 90th percentile high estimate for current typical daytime highs under moderate emissions
.
Assumption: Higher temperatures equate to a higher likelihood of communities coming into contact with harmful heat events, driving up geographical disadvantage
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: This high estimate represents a scenario that is only 10% likely to be exceeded according to the probabilistic model
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
