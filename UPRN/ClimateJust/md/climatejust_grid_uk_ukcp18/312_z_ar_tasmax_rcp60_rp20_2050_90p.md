The field **'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature high emissions 20 year return period 2050 90th percentile'** (internal name `z_ar_tasmax_rcp60_rp20_2050_90p`) represents the standardized high estimate of projected mid-century temperatures during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave under RCP 6.0.

### **Description**
This **"All-Relative" Z-score** standardized the high-end mid-century projection for 5% annual-chance daytime maximums against all other database scenarios. It identifies neighbourhoods facing acute mid-century extreme heat exposure relative to the entire dataset if climate response to high greenhouse gas levels is more aggressive than central estimates predict.

* **Assumption:** Higher relative maximum temperatures signify a greater potential for residents to experience catastrophic heat-related impacts.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** This is a probabilistic high-estimate, representing a scenario with only a 10% chance of being exceeded.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century probabilistic projections.
