The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature very high emissions 50 year return period 2050 10th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp85_rp50_2050_10p) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period under RCP 8.5.
Description
This projection identifies the 10th percentile low estimate for extremely rare heat events (2% annual probability) three decades into the future (2040–2060 window)
. It maps where the minimum expected mid-century extreme heat hazard remains relatively lower in an unmitigated emissions world
.
Assumption: Understanding the geographical distribution of future very rare heat hazards is necessary for just adaptation and resilient infrastructure planning
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for future scenarios
.
Caveat: This low estimate means there is a 90% probability that actual temperatures during a mid-century 50-year event would be higher than these values
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
