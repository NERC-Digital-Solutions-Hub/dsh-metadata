The field 'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature very high emissions 2010-2030 median' (internal name z_all_rel_tas_rcp85_2019_50p) represents the standardized central estimate of summer average temperatures for the current period under an extreme emissions scenario.
Description
This metric is an "All-Relative" Z-score, calculated using the mean and standard deviation across all four considered temperature scenarios and all time periods in the database
. It converts the median (50th percentile) projected summer average temperature (June, July, August) for the 2020 window under RCP 8.5 into a standardized value, allowing for direct statistical comparison between this near-term "very high emissions" world and other potential climate futures
.
Assumption: Higher relative scores indicate a greater potential for residents to come into contact with heat-related events, identifying neighbourhoods with high baseline hazard exposure
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This is a standardized input for the Hazard Exposure assessment
.
Caveat: These probabilistic estimates represent a 20-year average centered on 2020 and are calculated on a 25km grid
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 (United Kingdom Climate Projections 2018) probabilistic projections
.
