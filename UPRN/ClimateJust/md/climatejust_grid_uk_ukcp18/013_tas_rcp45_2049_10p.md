The field **'Summer average temperature moderate emissions 2040-2060 10th percentile'** (internal name `tas_rcp45_2049_10p`) represents the low estimate of projected average summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2040–2060 under a moderate emissions scenario (RCP 4.5).

### **Description**
This projection provides the **10th percentile** estimate for summer average temperatures in the 2050s. It identifies areas where heat hazard exposure is likely to remain relatively lower even as global climate change progresses under moderate emissions.

*   **Assumption:** Higher temperatures represent a baseline risk for heat hazard-exposure that must be accounted for in adaptation planning.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for mid-century scenarios.
*   **Caveat:** This estimate corresponds to the low end of the projected probability range for the moderate emissions scenario.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
