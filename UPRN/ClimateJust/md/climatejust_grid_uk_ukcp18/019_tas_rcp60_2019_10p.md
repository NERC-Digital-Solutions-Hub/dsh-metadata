The field 'Summer average temperature high emissions 2010-2030 10th percentile' (internal name tas_rcp60_2019_10p) represents the low estimate of projected average summer temperatures in Wales for the current period (2010–2030) under a high emissions scenario (RCP 6.0).
Description
This metric evaluates current Hazard Exposure under a high emissions trajectory, providing the 10th percentile (low estimate). It identifies the minimum expected baseline heat hazard for communities today if global emissions remain high
.
Assumption: Higher average summer temperatures indicate a baseline of exposure that can lead to losses in community well-being
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: While this is a low estimate for the high-emissions path, actual temperatures are statistically 90% likely to be higher than these values in this scenario
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
