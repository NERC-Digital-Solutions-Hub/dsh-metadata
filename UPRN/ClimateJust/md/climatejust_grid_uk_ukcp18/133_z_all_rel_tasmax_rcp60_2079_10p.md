The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature high emissions 2070-2090 10th percentile'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp60_2079_10p`) represents the standardized low estimate of late-century average daily maximum summer temperatures under RCP 6.0.

### **Description**
This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the 10th percentile projection for the 2080s (2070–2090) into a standardized score. It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face the minimum expected intensification of relative daytime heat hazards toward the end of the century in a world where global greenhouse gas emissions continue to rise before stabilizing.

*   **Assumption:** Understanding the relative geographical distribution of future daytime maximums is essential for ensure justice in adaptation planning.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
*   **Caveat:** Late-century projections carry significant probabilistic uncertainty and do not reflect absolute peak temperatures during individual events.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** late-century maximum temperature data.
