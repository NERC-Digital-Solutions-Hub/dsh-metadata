### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature very high emissions 20 year return period 2080 median

#### Description

This metric is an **"All-Relative" Z-score**, meaning it is calculated using the mean and standard deviation across all four considered temperature scenarios and all time periods (2020s, 2050s, 2080s) in the database. It converts the **median (50th percentile)** projection for late-century (2070–2090 average) daytime maximums during a 5% annual-probability extreme event into a standardized score. This identifies neighbourhoods projected to face extreme intensification of relative daytime hazards compared to the entire database average in a world of unmitigated global emissions growth.

* **Assumption:** Neighbourhoods with higher relative scores are assumed to have a greater chance of exposure to acute heat hazards, driving up total heat disadvantage.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Standardized scores highlight relative risk across all considered warming levels but mask absolute physical risk in degrees Celsius.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
