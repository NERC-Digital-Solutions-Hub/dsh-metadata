The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature high emissions 2010-2030 median'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tas_rcp60_2019_50p`) represents the standardized central estimate of summer average temperatures for the current period under RCP 6.0.

### **Description**
This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized measure of the likely current summer temperatures in a world with rising emissions. Standardizing across all scenarios identifies neighbourhoods that are currently "relatively hot" compared to the dataset mean.

*   **Assumption:** neighbourhood disadvantage is higher where high social vulnerability and high extreme relative heat hazards coincide.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
*   **Caveat:** This identifies geographical hotspots of hazard relative to all possible warming levels in the database.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** summer average temperature projections.
