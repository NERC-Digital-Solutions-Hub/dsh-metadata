The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature very high emissions 100 year return period 2020 10th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp85_rp100_2020_10p) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave in the current period under RCP 8.5
.
Description
This metric evaluates Hazard Exposure for a "100-year return period" event—a temperature so extreme it has only a 1% annual probability of occurring
. It provides the 10th percentile low estimate for the 2020 window (2010–2030), mapping the minimum expected severity of these extremely rare events for communities today in a world tracking towards very high emissions
.
Assumption: Higher extreme temperatures indicate an increased exposure risk, which can lead to severe losses in community well-being
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: Actual current temperatures during such an event are statistically 90% likely to be higher than these values
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
