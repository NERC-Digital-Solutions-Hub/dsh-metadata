The field **'Summer average maximum temperature very high emissions 2040-2060 median'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp85_2049_50p`) represents the central estimate of projected average daily maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2040–2060 under RCP 8.5. 
### **Description** 
This represents the **50th percentile (median)** projection for typical mid-century daytime maximums in an unmitigated emissions world. It identifyies areas of acute hazard intensification resulting from an extreme global warming future. 
* **Assumption:** Heat disadvantage is highest where biophysical sensitivity and low adaptive capacity meet acute increases in projected daytime temperatures. 
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension. 
* **Caveat:** It represents an average maximum across summer months and does not reflect peak extreme events. 
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
