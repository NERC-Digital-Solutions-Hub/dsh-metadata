The field 'Summer average temperature moderate emissions 2070-2090 10th percentile' (internal name tas_rcp45_2079_10p) represents the low estimate of projected average summer temperatures in Wales for the late-century period (2070–2090) under a moderate emissions scenario (RCP 4.5).
Description
This projection maps the 10th percentile estimate for summer temperatures in the 2080s. It provides a long-term outlook for heat hazard exposure across Wales, showing the minimum expected rise under moderate global emissions
.
Assumption: Understanding the geographical distribution of future heat hazards is necessary for long-term community resilience and justice in adaptation planning
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for the late-century time horizon
.
Caveat: Late-century projections are probabilistic and carry greater uncertainty regarding the actual outcomes of emissions pathways
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
