The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 2010-2030 90th percentile'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp26_2019_90p`) represents the standardized high estimate of current average daily maximum summer temperatures under RCP 2.6.

### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the near-term high-end daytime maximum projection into a standardized relative score. It identifyies areas of high current daytime heat exposure relative to the entire dataset average across all time periods and scenarios.

* **Assumption:** Higher relative maximum temperatures signify a greater potential for residents to experience harmful heat events.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** This is a high estimate; actual current daytime highs are statistically 90% likely to be below these values for this specific scenario.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** probabilistic climate projections.
