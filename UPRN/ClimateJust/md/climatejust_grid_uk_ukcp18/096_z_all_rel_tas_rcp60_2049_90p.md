The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature high emissions 2040-2060 90th percentile'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tas_rcp60_2049_90p`) represents the standardized high estimate of mid-century summer temperatures under RCP 6.0.

### **Description**
This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the 90th percentile high-estimate for the 2050s into a standardized value. It identifies geographical areas projected to experience the most hazardous future relative heat environments in this scenario.

*   **Assumption:** neighbourhood disadvantage is most acute where high social vulnerability and intensified relative hazards converge.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
*   **Caveat:** This is a high-end probabilistic estimate used for cautious long-term planning.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** probabilistic climate projections.
