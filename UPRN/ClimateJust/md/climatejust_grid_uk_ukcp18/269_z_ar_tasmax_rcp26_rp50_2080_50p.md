The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 50 year return period 2080 median' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp26_rp50_2080_50p) represents the standardized central estimate of late-century temperatures during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave under RCP 2.6.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score provides a standardized measure of the likely late-century hottest part of a typical 2% annual-chance extreme summer day.
 It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face extreme intensification of relative daytime extreme hazards compared to the database average by the 2080s even with successful global climate stabilization.
Assumption: neighbourhood disadvantage is higher where high biophysical sensitivity and high relative hazards for very rare events coincide.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure.
Caveat: Values represent conditions over a 20-year window and do not capture absolute peak temperatures reached during a specific event.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 late-century maximum temperature projections.
