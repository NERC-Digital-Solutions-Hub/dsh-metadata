### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature very high emissions 100 year return period 2050 10th percentile

#### Description

This converts low estimates of mid-century rare heat events into standardized scores.

* **Assumption:** Mapping future hazards supports adaptation planning.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Represents averaged conditions.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** data.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
