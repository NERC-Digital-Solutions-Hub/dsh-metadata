The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature very high emissions 20 year return period 2080 10th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp85_rp20_2080_10p) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period under RCP 8.5.
Description
This projection maps the 10th percentile (low estimate) for rare extreme heat events (5% annual probability) at the end of the century (2070–2090 window)
. It provides a long-term outlook for the minimum expected intensification of catastrophic heat hazards in a world of unmitigated global emissions
.
Assumption: higher daytime maximum temperatures increase the likelihood of heat-related well-being losses
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for the late-century
.
Caveat: Values in this field are statistically 90% likely to be exceeded by the actual temperatures experienced during such an event for this period and scenario
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
