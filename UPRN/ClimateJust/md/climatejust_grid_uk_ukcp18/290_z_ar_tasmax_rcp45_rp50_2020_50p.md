The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 50 year return period 2020 median' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp45_rp50_2020_50p) represents the standardized central estimate of current temperatures during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave under RCP 4.5.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score provides a standardized measure of the likely current severity of a 2% annual-chance daytime heat event in a world where global emissions stop increasing past the 2030s
. Standardizing across all scenarios highlights neighbourhoods that are currently "relatively hot" during these very rare events compared to the dataset mean across all scenarios and time periods
.
Assumption: Neighbourhoods with higher relative scores indicate a baseline of exposure that can lead to significant well-being losses
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This is a standardized input for the Hazard Exposure component
.
Caveat: These probabilistic estimates represent conditions over a 20-year average and do not Capture absolute peak temperatures reached during a specific event
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
