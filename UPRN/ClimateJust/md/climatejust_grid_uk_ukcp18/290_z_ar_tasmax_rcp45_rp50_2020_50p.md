### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 50 year return period 2020 median

#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized measure of the likely current severity of a 2% annual-chance daytime heat event in a world where global emissions stop increasing past the 2030s. Standardizing across all scenarios highlights neighbourhoods that are currently "relatively hot" during these very rare events compared to the dataset mean across all scenarios and time periods.

* **Assumption:** Neighbourhoods with higher relative scores indicate a baseline of exposure that can lead to significant well-being losses.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is a standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** component.
* **Caveat:** These probabilistic estimates represent conditions over a 20-year average and do not capture absolute peak temperatures reached during a specific event.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
