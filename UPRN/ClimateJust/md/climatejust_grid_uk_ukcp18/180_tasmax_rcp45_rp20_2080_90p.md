The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature moderate emissions 20 year return period 2080 90th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp45_rp20_2080_90p) represents the high estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period under RCP 4.5.
Description
This metric represents the 90th percentile high estimate, providing a cautious "worst-case" projection for rare daytime extreme events by late-century.
 It identifies where rare extreme heat hazards could become catastrophically severe at the end of the century if climate response is high under moderate emission levels.
Assumption: Neighbourhood disadvantage is statistically higher where high social vulnerability and acute extreme heat hazards coincide.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for future scenarios.
Caveat: This is a probabilistic high-estimate, representing the top 10% of likely temperature outcomes for this specific late-century scenario.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections.
