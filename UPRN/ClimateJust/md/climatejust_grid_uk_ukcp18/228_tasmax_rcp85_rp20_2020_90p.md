The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature very high emissions 20 year return period 2020 90th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp85_rp20_2020_90p) represents the high estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the current period under RCP 8.5.
Description
This metric identifies the 90th percentile high estimate for daytime maximums during a rare heat event today
. It maps areas facing acute current heat exposure if the climate system responds more aggressively to very high greenhouse gas levels than central estimates suggest
.
Assumption: Higher temperatures equate to a higher likelihood of communities coming into contact with harmful heat events, driving up geographical disadvantage
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: Values in this field are 90% likely to be below the actual temperature for this scenario and period according to the probabilistic model
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
