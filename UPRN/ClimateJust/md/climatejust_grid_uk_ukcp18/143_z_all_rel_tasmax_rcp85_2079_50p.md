The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature very high emissions 2070-2090 median'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp85_2079_50p`) represents the standardized central estimate of late-century average daily maximum summer temperatures under RCP 8.5.

### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized measure of the likely late-century hottest part of a typical summer's day in an unmitigated emissions world. Positive values denote neighbourhoods where extreme relative daytime heat hazards will be significantly higher than the average calculated across all database scenarios and time periods.

* **Assumption:** Positive relative Z-scores highlight neighbourhoods where future climate change is likely to most significantly increase social inequalities and well-being losses.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Standardized scores identify geographical hotspots of hazard but do not reflect absolute peak temperatures during specific heatwaves.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** late-century maximum temperature projections.
