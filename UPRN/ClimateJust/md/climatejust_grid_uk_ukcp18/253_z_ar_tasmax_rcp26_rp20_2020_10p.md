### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 20 year return period 2020 10th percentile

#### Description

This metric is an **"All-Relative" Z-score**, meaning it is calculated using the mean and standard deviation across all scenarios and time periods in the database. It converts the 10th percentile (low estimate) for current (2020 window) daytime maximums during a 5% annual-chance extreme event into a standardized score to allow for direct comparison between the present and future.

*   **Assumption:** Positive relative Z-scores denote higher temperatures than average relative to the whole dataset, identifying current locations of acute relative extreme heat hazard.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** Standardized scores highlight relative risk across all considered warming levels but mask absolute Celsius values.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** probabilistic maximum temperature data.
