The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 20 year return period 2020 10th percentile' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp26_rp20_2020_10p) represents the standardized low estimate of current 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave temperatures under a low emissions scenario (RCP 2.6)
.
Description
This metric is an "All-Relative" Z-score, meaning it is calculated using the mean and standard deviation across all scenarios and time periods in the database
. It converts the 10th percentile (low estimate) for current (2020 window) daytime maximums during a 5% annual-chance extreme event into a standardized score to allow for direct comparison between the present and future
.
Assumption: Positive relative Z-scores denote higher temperatures than average relative to the whole dataset, identifying current locations of acute relative extreme heat hazard
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: Standardized scores highlight relative risk across all considered warming levels but mask absolute Celsius values
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 probabilistic maximum temperature data
.
