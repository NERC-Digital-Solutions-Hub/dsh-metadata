The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature low emissions 2010-2030 10th percentile'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tas_rcp26_2019_10p`) represents the standardized low estimate of summer average temperatures for 2010–2030 under RCP 2.6. 
### **Description** 
This metric is an **"All-Relative" Z-score**, meaning it is calculated using the mean and standard deviation across all scenarios in the database. It converts the 10th percentile (low estimate) for summer average temperatures into a standardized score to allow for direct comparison between the current period and future warming worlds. 
* **Assumption:** Positive scores denote higher temperatures than average relative to the whole dataset (including future worlds), identifyging current locations of relative heat hazard. 
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is a standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** assessment. 
* **Caveat:** This is a relative score based on the specific distribution of temperatures within the Welsh/English dataset. 
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** probabilistic summer temperature data.
