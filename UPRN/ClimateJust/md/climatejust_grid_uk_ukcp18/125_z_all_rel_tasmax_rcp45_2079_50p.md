The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 2070-2090 median'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp45_2079_50p`) represents the standardized central estimate of late-century average daily maximum summer temperatures under RCP 4.5.

### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized measure of the likely late-century hottest part of a typical summer's day. It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face extreme intensification of relative daytime maximums compared to the database average by the 2080s even in a world that transitions toward a low-carbon future.

* **Assumption:** Understanding the geographical distribution of future relative daytime maximums is essential for ensuring justice in adaptation planning.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** component.
* **Caveat:** Values around 0 in this field denote that projected daytime highs are around the average for the entire database, which includes higher emissions pathways.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** late-century maximum temperature projections.
