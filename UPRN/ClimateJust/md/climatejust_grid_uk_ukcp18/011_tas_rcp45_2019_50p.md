The field 'Summer average temperature moderate emissions 2010-2030 median' (internal name tas_rcp45_2019_50p) represents the central estimate of projected average summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2010–2030 under a moderate emissions scenario (RCP 4.5).
Description
This represents the 50th percentile (median) estimate for current heat hazard exposure. The 50th percentile of the moderate emissions scenario is recommended as a standard starting point for assessing heat-related risks
.
Assumption: Increased heat exposure leads to higher social disadvantage when it coincides with neighbourhoods containing elderly residents, children, or those in ill health
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: It represents a 20-year average centered on 2020 and is calculated on a 25km grid, meaning it may not capture very local urban heat island effects
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
