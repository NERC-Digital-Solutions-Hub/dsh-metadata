### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature very high emissions 100 year return period 2050 median

#### Description

This provides a likely mid-century projection for rare heat events using standardized scores.

* **Assumption:** Combined vulnerability and hazard increases disadvantage.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Median estimates may underestimate extremes.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** projections.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
