The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature low emissions 50 year return period 2080 median' (internal name tasmax_rcp26_rp50_2080_50p) represents the central estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period under a low emissions scenario (RCP 2.6).
Description
This metric evaluates Hazard Exposure by identifying the likely severity of an extremely rare daytime heat event—one with only a 2% (1 in 50) annual probability of occurring.
 Within the framework, it provides the 50th percentile (median) projection for these rare events in the 2080 window (2070–2090), assuming a future where the world achieves rapid, sustained reductions in greenhouse gas emissions.
Assumption: Areas with higher projected temperatures during very rare extreme heatwaves face a greater risk of catastrophic impacts on community health and quality of life if social vulnerability is high.
Vulnerability Dimension: This indicator informs the Hazard Exposure dimension.
Caveat: These absolute temperature values represent the average condition over a 20-year time window and carry higher uncertainty due to the late-century timeframe.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 (United Kingdom Climate Projections 2018) probabilistic projections.
