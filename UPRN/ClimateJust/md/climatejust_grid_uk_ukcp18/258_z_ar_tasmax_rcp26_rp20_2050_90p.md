### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 20 year return period 2050 90th percentile

#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** standardized the high-end mid-century projection for 5% annual-chance daytime maximums against all other database scenarios. It identifies neighbourhoods facing acute mid-century extreme heat exposure relative to the entire dataset even in a low global emissions scenario if climate response is high.

*   **Assumption:** Higher relative maximum temperatures indicate an increased exposure risk, driving up geographical disadvantage in neighbourhoods containing sensitive populations.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
*   **Caveat:** High-end estimates represent a scenario that is only 10% likely to be exceeded according to the probabilistic model.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century probabilistic projections.
