The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature moderate emissions 50 year return period 2020 10th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp45_rp50_2020_10p`) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature at the hottest part of the day during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave in the current period under a moderate emissions scenario (RCP 4.5).

### **Description**
This metric evaluates **Hazard Exposure** by identifying the severity of a "50-year return period" event—an extremely rare temperature that has only a 2% annual probability of occurring (1 in 50 chance). Within the framework, this value represents the **10th percentile (low estimate)** for the 2020 window (2010–2030).

*   **Assumption:** Areas with higher projected temperatures during very rare extreme heatwaves face a greater risk of catastrophic impacts on community health and well-being if social vulnerability is high.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension, which is combined with socio-spatial vulnerability indices to determine overall **Heat Disadvantage**.
*   **Caveat:** These absolute temperature values represent the average condition over a 20-year time period centered on 2020 and do not reflect absolute peak temperatures during a specific individual event.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** (United Kingdom Climate Projections 2018) probabilistic projections.
