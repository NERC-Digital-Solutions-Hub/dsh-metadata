The field 'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 2040-2060 median' (internal name z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp45_2049_50p) represents the standardized central estimate of projected mid-century average daily maximum summer temperatures under RCP 4.5
.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score provides a standardized "likely" mid-century projection for typical daytime highs—the temperature at the hottest part of the day
. Standardizing across all scenarios allows these future maximums to be statistically mapped on the same scale as the present day and more extreme warming scenarios, identifying neighbourhoods facing the most acute shifts in their relative heat profile compared to the dataset average
.
Assumption: Increased heat exposure leads to higher social disadvantage when it coincides with neighbourhoods containing sensitive populations
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for the Hazard Exposure assessment
.
Caveat: These probabilistic estimates represent averages over a 20-year window (2040–2060) and may mask shorter, more intense periods of extreme heat stress
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 probabilistic mid-century maximum temperature projections
.
