The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 50 year return period 2080 90th percentile' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp26_rp50_2080_90p) represents the standardized high estimate of late-century temperatures during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave under RCP 2.6.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score standardized the high-end projection for extremely rare heat events at the end of the century.
 It identifies areas potentially facing catastrophic late-century extreme heat hazards if the climate responses aggressively to low greenhouse gas levels.
Assumption: Positive relative Z-scores highlight neighbourhoods where future climate change is likely to most significantly increase social inequalities.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure.
Caveat: Statistically, there is only a 10% probability that the actual extreme temperature for this period and scenario will exceed this value.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 late-century maximum temperature projections.
