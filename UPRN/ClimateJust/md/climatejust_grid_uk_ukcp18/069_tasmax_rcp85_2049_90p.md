The field **'Summer average maximum temperature very high emissions 2040-2060 90th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp85_2049_90p`) represents the high estimate of projected average daily maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2040–2060 under RCP 8.5.

### Description

This metric provides the **90th percentile** high estimate, identifying the most hazardous potential future daytime environments by mid-century under extreme emissions growth.

* **Assumption:** neighbourhoods where this extreme hazard exposure coincides with low social resilience are prioritized for urgent adaptation.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** This is a high-end probabilistic estimate used for "worst-case" scenario planning.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
