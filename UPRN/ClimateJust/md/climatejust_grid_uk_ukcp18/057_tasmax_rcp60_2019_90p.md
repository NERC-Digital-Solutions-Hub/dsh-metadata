The field 'Summer average maximum temperature high emissions 2010-2030 90th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp60_2019_90p) represents the high estimate of projected average daily maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2010–2030 under RCP 6.0
.
Description
This metric identifies the 90th percentile high estimate for typical summer daytime maximums today
. It maps areas facing acute current heat exposure if global climate sensitivity to high emissions is higher than central estimates suggest
.
Assumption: Higher daytime highs signify a greater potential for communities to come into contact with heat-related events, leading to more severe health impacts
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: High estimates represent a scenario that is only 10% likely to be exceeded according to the probabilistic model
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
