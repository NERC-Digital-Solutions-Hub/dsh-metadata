### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 20 year return period 2050 10th percentile

#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the 10th percentile (low estimate) mid-century projection for 5% annual-chance extreme heat events into a standardized score. It identifies neighbourhoods where the minimum expected mid-century extreme heat hazard remains relatively low compared to the entire spectrum of climate futures in the database.

* **Assumption:** Understanding the geographical distribution of future relative extreme hazards is essential for ensured justice in adaptation planning.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** There is a 90% probability that actual temperatures during a mid-century 20-year event will be higher than the values recorded in this field.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century probabilistic data.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
