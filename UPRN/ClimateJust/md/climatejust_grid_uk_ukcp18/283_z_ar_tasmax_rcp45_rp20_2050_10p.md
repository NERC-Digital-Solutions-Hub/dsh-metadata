The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 20 year return period 2050 10th percentile' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp45_rp20_2050_10p) represents the standardized low estimate of mid-century temperatures during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave under RCP 4.5.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score converts the 10th percentile (low estimate) mid-century projection for 5% annual-chance extreme heat events into a standardized score
. It identifies neighbourhoods where the minimum expected mid-century extreme heat hazard remains relatively low compared to the entire spectrum of climate futures in the database
.
Assumption: Understanding the geographical distribution of future relative extreme hazards is essential for ensured justice in adaptation planning
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: There is a 90% probability that actual temperatures during a mid-century 20-year event will be higher than the values recorded in this field
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 mid-century probabilistic data
.
