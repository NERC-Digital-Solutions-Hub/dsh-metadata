The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 50 year return period 2080 median' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp45_rp50_2080_50p) represents the standardized central estimate of projected late-century temperatures during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave under RCP 4.5.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score provides a standardized measure of the likely late-century hottest part of an extremely rare 2% annual-chance extreme summer day
. It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face extreme intensification of relative daytime extreme hazards compared to the database average by the 2080s
.
Assumption: Positive relative Z-scores highlight neighbourhoods where future climate change is likely to most significantly increase social inequalities and quality-of-life losses
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: Values represent conditions over a 20-year window and do not Capture absolute peak temperatures reached during a specific event
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 late-century maximum temperature projections
.
