The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 20 year return period 2020 median' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp45_rp20_2020_50p) represents the standardized central estimate of current temperatures during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave under a moderate emissions scenario.
Description
This metric is an "All-Relative" Z-score, meaning it is calculated using the mean and standard deviation across all four considered temperature scenarios and all time periods (2020s, 2050s, 2080s) in the database
. It converts the median (50th percentile) projection for current (2010–2030) daytime maximums during a 5% annual-probability extreme event (20-year return period) into a standardized score
. This allows for a direct statistical comparison between the likely severity of rare current heat events and other potential climate futures
.
Assumption: Positive relative Z-scores denote higher temperatures than average relative to the entire dataset, identifying current locations of acute relative extreme heat hazard
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This is a standardized input for the Hazard Exposure assessment
.
Caveat: These probabilistic estimates represent a 20-year average centered on 2020 and do not capture the absolute peak temperatures reached during a specific event
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 (United Kingdom Climate Projections 2018) probabilistic projections
.
