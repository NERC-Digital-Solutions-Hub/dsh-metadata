The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature moderate emissions 50 year return period 2020 90th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp45_rp50_2020_90p`) represents the high estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave for the current period under RCP 4.5.

### **Description**
This metric identifies the **90th percentile (high estimate)** for daytime maximums during a very rare (2% annual chance) heat event today. It maps areas facing acute current extreme heat exposure if the climate system responds more aggressively to greenhouse gases than central estimates predict.

*   **Assumption:** Higher temperatures signify a greater potential for residents to experience catastrophic heat-related health impacts.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** High-end estimates represent a scenario that is only 10% likely to be exceeded according to the probabilistic model.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
