### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 100 year return period 2020 10th percentile

#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the 10th percentile (low estimate) for current (2020 window) daytime maximums during an exceptionally rare daytime heat event—one with only a 1% annual probability—into a standardized relative score. It identifies geographical areas facing the minimum expected current relative hazard for these events.

* **Assumption:** Higher relative temperatures denote higher baseline exposure that can lead to significant well-being losses.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** This is a standardized score and does not quantify absolute physical risk in Celsius.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** current period probabilistic maximum temperature data.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
