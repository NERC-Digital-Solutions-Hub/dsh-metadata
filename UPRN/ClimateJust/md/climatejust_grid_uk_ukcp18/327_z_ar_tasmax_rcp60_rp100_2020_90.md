#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the near-term high-end projection for exceptionally rare 1% annual-probability heat events into a standardized relative score. It identifies where current relative extreme heat exposure could be significantly above the dataset average if global climate patterns are highly sensitive to rising emissions.

*   **Assumption:** Neighbourhood disadvantage is statistically higher where high social vulnerability and acute relative hazards for rare events coincide.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
*   **Caveat:** Values in this field represent a high-impact climate outcome, statistically 90% likely to be higher than actual recorded temperatures for this period.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** probabilistic climate projections.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
