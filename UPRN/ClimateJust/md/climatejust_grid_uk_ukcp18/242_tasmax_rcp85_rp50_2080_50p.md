The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature very high emissions 50 year return period 2080 median' (internal name tasmax_rcp85_rp50_2080_50p) represents the central estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period under RCP 8.5
.
Description
This metric identifies the 50th percentile (median) projection for the severity of an extremely rare heat event (2% annual probability) by the 2080s
. It maps the intensification of rare heat hazards in an unmitigated global emissions future, identifying neighbourhoods likely to face extreme shifts in their extreme temperature profile
.
Assumption: Neighbourhoods projected to have the highest extreme temperatures face the greatest challenge in protecting sensitive residents from negative health impacts
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for future scenarios
.
Caveat: While the median is a central estimate, absolute peak temperatures during an event may exceed these averaged 20-year projections
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
