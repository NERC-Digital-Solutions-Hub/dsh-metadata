The field 'Summer average temperature low emissions 2070-2090 median' (internal name tas_rcp26_2079_50p) represents the central estimate of projected average summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2070–2090 under a low emissions scenario (RCP 2.6).
Description
This metric provides the 50th percentile (median) estimate for average summer temperatures late in the century. It serves as the "likely" scenario for heat hazard exposure in the 2080s assuming successful global transition to a low emissions pathway
.
Assumption: Projecting future heat hazard exposure allows for the identification of neighbourhoods where future climate impacts will most severely intersect with current social vulnerabilities
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: It represents an average condition over 20 years; heat health thresholds may vary significantly across different parts of Wales by this time
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
