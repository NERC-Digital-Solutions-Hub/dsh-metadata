The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 100 year return period 2050 median' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp26_rp100_2050_50) represents the standardized central estimate of projected mid-century temperatures during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave under RCP 2.6.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score provides a standardized "likely" mid-century projection for extreme daytime maximums that have a 1% annual probability of occurrence.
 Standardizing across all scenarios allows these future rare hazards to be mapped on the same statistical scale as the present day and identifies neighbourhoods likely to face a significant shift in their relative extreme heat profile.
Assumption: Neighbourhood disadvantage is most acute where high socio-spatial vulnerability and acute increases in projected relative hazards converge.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for the Hazard Exposure dimension.
Caveat: Median values provide a central probabilistic outlook but absolute peak temperatures during an event may still be higher.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 mid-century maximum temperature projections.
