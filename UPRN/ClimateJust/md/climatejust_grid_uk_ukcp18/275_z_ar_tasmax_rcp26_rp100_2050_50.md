### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 100 year return period 2050 median

#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized "likely" mid-century projection for extreme daytime maximums that have a 1% annual probability of occurrence. Standardizing across all scenarios allows these future rare hazards to be mapped on the same statistical scale as the present day and identifies neighbourhoods likely to face a significant shift in their relative extreme heat profile.

* **Assumption:** Neighbourhood disadvantage is most acute where high socio-spatial vulnerability and acute increases in projected relative hazards converge.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Median values provide a central probabilistic outlook but absolute peak temperatures during an event may still be higher.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century maximum temperature projections.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
