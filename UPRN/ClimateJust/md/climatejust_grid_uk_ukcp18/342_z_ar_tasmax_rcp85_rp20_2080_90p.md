#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the **90th percentile (high estimate)** projection for the 2080 window during a 5% annual-probability heat event into a standardized score. It identifies geographical areas potentially facing catastrophic late-century relative extreme heat hazards relative to the entire dataset average if the climate responds aggressively to very high global emissions.

*   **Assumption:** Higher relative maximum temperatures signify a greater potential for residents to experience catastrophic heat-related health and welfare impacts.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
*   **Caveat:** Statistically, actual temperatures during such an event are 90% likely to be below these high-end values for this specific scenario and period.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** probabilistic climate projections.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
