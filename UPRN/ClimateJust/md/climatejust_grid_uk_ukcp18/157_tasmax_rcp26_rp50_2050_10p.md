The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature low emissions 50 year return period 2050 10th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp26_rp50_2050_10p`) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period (2050 window) under RCP 2.6.

### Description

This projection identifies the 10th percentile (low estimate) for very rare extreme heat events (2% annual probability) three decades into the future. It maps where the minimum expected mid-century extreme heat hazard remains relatively low if global mitigation efforts succeed.

* **Assumption:** Understanding the geographical distribution of future very rare heat hazards is necessary for just adaptation and emergency planning.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for future scenarios.
* **Caveat:** Estimates represent the average over a 20-year window (2040–2060) centered on 2050.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
