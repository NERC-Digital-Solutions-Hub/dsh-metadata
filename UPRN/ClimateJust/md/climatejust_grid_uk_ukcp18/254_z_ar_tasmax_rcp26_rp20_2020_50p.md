### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 20 year return period 2020 median

#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized measure of the likely current severity of a 5% annual-chance daytime heat event in a world tracking towards low global emissions. Standardizing across all scenarios identifies neighbourhoods where current rare extreme heat hazards are "relatively severe" compared to the entire spectrum of climate futures in the database.

* **Assumption:** Neighbourhoods with higher relative scores are assumed to have a greater chance of exposure to acute heat hazards, driving up total heat disadvantage.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** These probabilistic estimates represent a 20-year average centered on 2020 and do not capture absolute peak temperatures reached during a specific event.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
