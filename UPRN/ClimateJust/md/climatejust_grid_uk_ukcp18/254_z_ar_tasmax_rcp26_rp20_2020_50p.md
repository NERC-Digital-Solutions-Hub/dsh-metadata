The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 20 year return period 2020 median' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp26_rp20_2020_50p) represents the standardized central estimate of current 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave temperatures under RCP 2.6
.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score provides a standardized measure of the likely current severity of a 5% annual-chance daytime heat event in a world tracking towards low global emissions
. Standardizing across all scenarios identifies neighbourhoods where current rare extreme heat hazards are "relatively severe" compared to the entire spectrum of climate futures in the database
.
Assumption: Neighbourhoods with higher relative scores are assumed to have a greater chance of exposure to acute heat hazards, driving up total heat disadvantage
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: These probabilistic estimates represent a 20-year average centered on 2020 and do not capture absolute peak temperatures reached during a specific event
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
