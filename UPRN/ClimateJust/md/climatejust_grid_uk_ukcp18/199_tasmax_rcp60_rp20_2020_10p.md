The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature high emissions 20 year return period 2020 10th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp60_rp20_2020_10p) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature at the hottest part of the day during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave in the current period under a high emissions scenario (RCP 6.0)
.
Description
This metric evaluates Hazard Exposure by identifying the severity of a "20-year return period" event—an extreme daytime temperature that has a 5% (1 in 20) annual probability of occurring
. Within the framework, it provides the 10th percentile (low estimate) for the 2020 window (2010–2030), mapping the minimum expected current extreme heat hazard for communities today
.
Assumption: Areas with higher projected temperatures during heatwaves are likely to see more severe community well-being losses when combined with high social vulnerability
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This indicator informs the Hazard Exposure dimension, which is combined with socio-spatial vulnerability indices to determine overall Heat Disadvantage
.
Caveat: These absolute temperature values represent averages over a 20-year time period centered on 2020 and do not reflect absolute peak temperatures during a specific individual event
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
