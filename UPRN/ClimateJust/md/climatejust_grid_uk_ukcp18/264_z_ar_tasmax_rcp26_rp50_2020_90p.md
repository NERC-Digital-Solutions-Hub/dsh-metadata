The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 50 year return period 2020 90th percentile' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp26_rp50_2020_90p) represents the standardized high estimate of current temperatures during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave under RCP 2.6.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score converts the near-term high-end projection for 2% annual-chance heat events into a standardized relative score.
 It identifies areas of high current extreme heat exposure relative to the entire dataset average across all time periods, representing a high-impact climate outcome today if the climate system is sensitive to greenhouse gases.
Assumption: neighbourhood disadvantage is statistically higher where high social vulnerability and acute relative hazards for rare events coincide.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure.
Caveat: This high estimate represents a scenario that is only 10% likely to be exceeded according to the model.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 current period projections.
