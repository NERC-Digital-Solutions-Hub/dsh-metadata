The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature low emissions 100 year return period 2020 median'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp26_rp100_2020_50p`) represents the central estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave for the current period under RCP 2.6.

### **Description**
This metric provides the **50th percentile (median)** estimate for daytime extreme maximums that have a 1% annual chance of occurrence. It identifies the "likely" current severity of these extremely rare heat events for communities today in a world tracking towards low global emissions.

*   **Assumption:** Increased exposure to extreme heat leads to higher social disadvantage when it coincides with neighbourhoods containing sensitive populations.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** Median values provide a central probabilistic outlook but should be viewed alongside absolute high-end projections for emergency management.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
