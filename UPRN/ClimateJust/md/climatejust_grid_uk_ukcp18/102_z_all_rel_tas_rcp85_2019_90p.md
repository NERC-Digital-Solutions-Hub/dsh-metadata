The field 'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature very high emissions 2010-2030 90th percentile' (internal name z_all_rel_tas_rcp85_2019_90p) represents the standardized high estimate of summer average temperatures for the current period under RCP 8.5.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score converts the 90th percentile (high estimate) of current summer average temperatures into a standardized relative score
. It identifies neighbourhoods that would experience acute current heat exposure relative to the entire dataset if climate sensitivity is high in a world with rapidly rising greenhouse gas emissions
.
Assumption: Positive relative Z-scores denote higher temperatures than average relative to the whole dataset, identifying current locations of acute relative heat hazard
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: This is a high estimate; there is only a 10% probability that actual average temperatures for this period and scenario will exceed this value
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 probabilistic summer temperature data
.
