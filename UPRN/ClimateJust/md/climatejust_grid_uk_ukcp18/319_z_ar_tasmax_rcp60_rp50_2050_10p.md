### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature high emissions 50 year return period 2050 10th percentile

#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the 10th percentile mid-century projection for very rare 2% annual-chance extreme events into a standardized score. It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face the minimum expected intensification of relative 50-year daytime heat hazards by the 2050s in a world where global greenhouse gas emissions are peaking.

* **Assumption:** Understanding the geographical distribution of future relative extreme hazards is necessary for ensured justice in adaptation planning.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Standardized scores simplify complex data but mask absolute Celsius values reached during individual events.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century probabilistic projections.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
