The field **'Summer average maximum temperature high emissions 2070-2090 median'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp60_2079_50p`) represents the central estimate of projected average daily maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2070–2090 under RCP 6.0.

### Description

This metric represents the **50th percentile (median)** projection for average late-century daytime highs. It identifies neighbourhoods where summer daytime maximums are likely to be significantly higher than current experience by the 2080s due to high emissions.

* **Assumption:** neighbourhood disadvantage is most acute where high biophysical sensitivity meet acute increases in projected temperatures.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is a primary input for the **Hazard Exposure** component.
* **Caveat:** It represents an average over 20 years and does not reflect individual extreme heatwave events.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
