### Summer extreme maximum temperature very high emissions 100 year return period 2050 median

#### Description

This metric provides the **50th percentile (median)** projection for daytime extreme maximums that have a 1% annual probability of occurrence in the 2050s. It identifies neighbourhoods likely to face acute intensification of extremely rare heat hazards by mid-century as a result of rising global emissions.

*   **Assumption:** Neighbourhood disadvantage is most acute where high social vulnerability and intensified future heat hazards converge.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is a primary input for the **Hazard Exposure** component for the mid-century.
*   **Caveat:** While the median is a central estimate, absolute peak temperatures during an event may still exceed these averaged 20-year projections.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
