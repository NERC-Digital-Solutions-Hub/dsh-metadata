The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature very high emissions 100 year return period 2050 median' (internal name tasmax_rcp85_rp100_2050_50p) represents the central estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period under RCP 8.5
.
Description
This metric provides the 50th percentile (median) projection for daytime extreme maximums that have a 1% annual probability of occurrence in the 2050s
. It identifies neighbourhoods likely to face acute intensification of extremely rare heat hazards by mid-century as a result of rising global emissions
.
Assumption: Neighbourhood disadvantage is most acute where high social vulnerability and intensified future heat hazards converge
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This is a primary input for the Hazard Exposure component for the mid-century
.
Caveat: While the median is a central estimate, absolute peak temperatures during an event may still exceed these averaged 20-year projections
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
