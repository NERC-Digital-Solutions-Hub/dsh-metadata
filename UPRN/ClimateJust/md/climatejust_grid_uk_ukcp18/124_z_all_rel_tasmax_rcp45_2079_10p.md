The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 2070-2090 10th percentile'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp45_2079_10p`) represents the standardized low estimate of late-century average daily maximum summer temperatures under RCP 4.5.

### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the 10th percentile projection for the 2080s (2070–2090) into a standardized score. It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face the minimum expected intensification of relative daytime heat hazards at the end of the century under a moderate emissions pathway, relative to all possible warming levels in the database.

* **Assumption:** Higher relative daytime highs signify a greater potential for residents to experience harmful heat events.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Late-century projections carry significantly higher levels of uncertainty due to the long time scales and complex climate feedback mechanisms involved.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** late-century maximum temperature projections.
