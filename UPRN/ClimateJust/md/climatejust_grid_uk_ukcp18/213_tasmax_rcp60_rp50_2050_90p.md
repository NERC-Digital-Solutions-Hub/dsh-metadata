The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature high emissions 50 year return period 2050 90th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp60_rp50_2050_90p) represents the high estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period under RCP 6.0
.
Description
This metric represents the 90th percentile high estimate, providing a cautious projection for very rare daytime extreme events by mid-century
. It identifyies where rare extreme heat hazards could become catastrophically severe if climate response to high greenhouse gas levels is high
.
Assumption: neighbourhood disadvantage is statistically higher where high social vulnerability and intensified hazards for very rare events converge
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for future scenarios
.
Caveat: High estimates represent a scenario that is only 10% likely to be exceeded according to the probabilistic model
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
