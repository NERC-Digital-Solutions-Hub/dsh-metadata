The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature moderate emissions 2070-2090 median'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tas_rcp45_2079_50p`) represents the standardized central estimate of late-century summer temperatures under RCP 4.5.

### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized "likely" estimate of summer averages toward the end of the century. It identifyies neighbourhoods projected to face a significant shift in their relative heat profile by the 2080s compared to the database average.

* **Assumption:** Positive values denote neighbourhoods where extreme relative heat hazards will be higher than the average calculated across all database years and scenarios.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is a standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** assessment.
* **Caveat:** Scores represent 20-year averages and may mask shorter, more intense periods of heat stress.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** late-century projections.
