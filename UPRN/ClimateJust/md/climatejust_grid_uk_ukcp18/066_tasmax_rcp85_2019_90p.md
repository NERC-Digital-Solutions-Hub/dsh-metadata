The field **'Summer average maximum temperature very high emissions 2010-2030 90th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp85_2019_90p`) represents the high estimate of projected average daily maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2010–2030 under RCP 8.5. 
### **Description** 
This provides the **90th percentile** estimate, identifying areas of acute near-term daytime heat exposure if climate sensitivity and emissions are both high. 
* **Assumption:** Higher daytime highs equate to a higher likelihood of communities coming into contact with harmful heat events. 
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension. 
* **Caveat:** This is a high-end projection; values in this field are 90% likely to be below the actual average maximum for this scenario. 
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
