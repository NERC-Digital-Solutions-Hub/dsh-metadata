The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature moderate emissions 50 year return period 2020 median'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp45_rp50_2020_50p`) represents the central estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave for the current period under RCP 4.5.

### Description

This metric provides the **50th percentile (median)** estimate for daytime extreme maximums today that have a 2% annual chance of occurrence. It identifies the "likely" current severity of these rare heat events for communities in a world where global emissions are projected to peak around 2040.

* **Assumption:** neighbourhood disadvantage is higher where high social vulnerability and intensified hazards for very rare events converge.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** While the median is a central estimate, absolute peak temperatures during an event may still exceed these averaged projections.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
