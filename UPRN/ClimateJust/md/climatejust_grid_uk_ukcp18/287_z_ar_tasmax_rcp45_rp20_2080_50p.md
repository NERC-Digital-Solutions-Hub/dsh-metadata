### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 20 year return period 2080 median

#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized measure of the likely late-century hottest part of a 5% annual-chance extreme summer day. Positive values denote neighbourhoods where intensification of relative daytime extreme hazards will be higher than the average calculated across all database scenarios and time periods.

* **Assumption:** Positive relative Z-scores highlight neighbourhoods where future climate change is likely to most significantly increase social inequalities.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Standardized scores simplify complex data but do not reflect absolute peak temperatures reached during specific heatwaves.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** late-century maximum temperature projections.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
