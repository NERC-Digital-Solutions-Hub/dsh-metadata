The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 20 year return period 2080 median' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp45_rp20_2080_50p) represents the standardized central estimate of projected late-century temperatures during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave under RCP 4.5.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score provides a standardized measure of the likely late-century hottest part of a 5% annual-chance extreme summer day
. Positive values denote neighbourhoods where intensification of relative daytime extreme hazards will be higher than the average calculated across all database scenarios and time periods
.
Assumption: Positive relative Z-scores highlight neighbourhoods where future climate change is likely to most significantly increase social inequalities
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: Standardized scores simplify complex data but do not reflect absolute peak temperatures reached during specific heatwaves
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 late-century maximum temperature projections
.
