The field 'Summer average maximum temperature very high emissions 2010-2030 median' (internal name tasmax_rcp85_2019_50p) represents the central estimate of projected average daily maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2010–2030 under RCP 8.5
.
Description
This represents the 50th percentile (median) estimate for current daytime highs in an unmitigated global emissions path
. It provides a likely baseline for current heat hazard exposure
.
Assumption: Increased heat exposure leads to higher social disadvantage when it coincides with neighbourhoods containing sensitive populations
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: It represents an average daily maximum and does not reflect individual peak extreme heat events
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
