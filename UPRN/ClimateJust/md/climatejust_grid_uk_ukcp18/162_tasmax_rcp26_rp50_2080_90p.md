The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature low emissions 50 year return period 2080 90th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp26_rp50_2080_90p`) represents the high estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period under RCP 2.6.

### **Description**
This metric identifies the **90th percentile** high estimate for daytime maximums during a very rare heat event at the end of the century. It identifies where late-century catastrophic heat hazard exposure could become extreme even in a low-emissions world if the climate system is highly sensitive to greenhouse gases.

*   **Assumption:** Neighborhood disadvantage is statistically higher where high social vulnerability and acute extreme hazards for rare events coincide.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** This is a high-end projection; values in this field are 90% likely to be below the actual temperature for this scenario and period according to the probabilistic model.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
