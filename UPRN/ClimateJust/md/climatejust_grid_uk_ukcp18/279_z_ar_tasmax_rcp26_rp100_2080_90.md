The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 100 year return period 2080 90th percentile' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp26_rp100_2080_90) represents the standardized high estimate of late-century temperatures during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave under RCP 2.6.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score standardized the high-end projection for exceptionally rare 1% annual-chance heat events at the end of the century.
 It identifyies geographical areas potentially facing catastrophic late-century relative extreme heat hazards relative to the entire dataset average, even in a global low-emissions future.
Assumption: Neighbourhoods with the highest relative maximum temperatures face the greatest challenge in protecting sensitive residents from heat stress.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure.
Caveat: There is only a 10% probability that the actual extreme temperature for this period and scenario will exceed this value.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 late-century maximum temperature projections.
