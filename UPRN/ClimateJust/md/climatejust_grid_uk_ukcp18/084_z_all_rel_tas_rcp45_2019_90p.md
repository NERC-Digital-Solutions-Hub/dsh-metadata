The field 'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature moderate emissions 2010-2030 90th percentile' (internal name z_all_rel_tas_rcp45_2019_90p) represents the standardized high estimate of projected summer temperatures for the current period under RCP 4.5.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score converts the near-term high-end temperature projection into a standardized relative score. It identifyies areas of high current heat exposure relative to the entire dataset average across all time periods
.
Assumption: Higher relative temperatures signify a greater potential for residents to experience heat-related health and welfare impacts
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: This is a high estimate; there is only a 10% probability that actual average temperatures for this period and scenario will exceed this value
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 probabilistic climate projections
.
