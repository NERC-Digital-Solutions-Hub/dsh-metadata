The field 'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 2040-2060 90th percentile' (internal name z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp26_2049_90p) represents the standardized high estimate of projected mid-century average daily maximum summer temperatures under RCP 2.6.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score standardized the high-end mid-century projection for typical daytime highs against all other database scenarios
. It identifies neighbourhoods facing acute mid-century daytime heat exposure relative to the entire dataset even in a low global emissions scenario if climate response is high
.
Assumption: Higher relative maximum temperatures correlate with an increased likelihood of communities coming into contact with harmful heat events
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: This probabilistic high-impact climate outcome represents a scenario with only a 10% probability of being exceeded
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 probabilistic mid-century projections
.
