The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 2040-2060 10th percentile'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp26_2049_10p`) represents the standardized low estimate of mid-century average daily maximum summer temperatures under RCP 2.6.

### **Description**
This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the 10th percentile projection for mid-century (2040–2060) typical daytime highs into a standardized score. It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face the minimum expected intensification of relative daytime heat hazards by the 2050s in a low emissions future.

*   **Assumption:** Understanding the relative geographical distribution of future daytime highs is essential for planning long-term resilient infrastructure.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
*   **Caveat:** These probabilistic estimates represent averages over a 20-year window centered on 2050.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century maximum temperature projections.
