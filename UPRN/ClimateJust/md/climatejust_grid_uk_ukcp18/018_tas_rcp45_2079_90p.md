The field 'Summer average temperature moderate emissions 2070-2090 90th percentile' (internal name tas_rcp45_2079_90p) represents the high estimate of projected average summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2070–2090 under a moderate emissions scenario (RCP 4.5).
Description
This provides a 90th percentile high-estimate projection for average summer temperatures at the end of the century. It identifies areas of acute future heat hazard if climate response is high under moderate emission levels
.
Assumption: Areas with the highest projected temperatures face the greatest challenge in protecting residents from heat-related health and welfare impacts
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: This estimate is intended for cautious long-term planning, as it represents a high-impact climate outcome
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
