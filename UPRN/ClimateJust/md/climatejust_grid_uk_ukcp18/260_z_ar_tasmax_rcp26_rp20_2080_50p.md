The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 20 year return period 2080 median' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp26_rp20_2080_50p) represents the standardized central estimate of projected late-century 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave temperatures under RCP 2.6
.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score provides a standardized measure of the likely late-century hottest part of a typical 5% annual-chance extreme summer day
. It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face acute intensification of relative daytime extreme hazards compared to the database average by the 2080s even with successful global climate stabilization
.
Assumption: Positive relative Z-scores highlight neighbourhoods where future climate change is likely to most significantly increase social inequalities
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: Values in this field represent conditions over a 20-year average and do not reflect absolute peak temperatures during an individual event
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 late-century probabilistic projections
