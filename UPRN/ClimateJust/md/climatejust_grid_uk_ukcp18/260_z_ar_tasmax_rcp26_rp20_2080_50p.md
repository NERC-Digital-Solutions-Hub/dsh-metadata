### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 20 year return period 2080 median

#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized measure of the likely late-century hottest part of a typical 5% annual-chance extreme summer day. It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face acute intensification of relative daytime extreme hazards compared to the database average by the 2080s even with successful global climate stabilization.

* **Assumption:** Positive relative Z-scores highlight neighbourhoods where future climate change is likely to most significantly increase social inequalities.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Values in this field represent conditions over a 20-year average and do not reflect absolute peak temperatures during an individual event.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** late-century probabilistic projections.
