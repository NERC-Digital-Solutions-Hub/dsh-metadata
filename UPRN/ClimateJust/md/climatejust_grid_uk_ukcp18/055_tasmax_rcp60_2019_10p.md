The field **'Summer average maximum temperature high emissions 2010-2030 10th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp60_2019_10p`) represents the low estimate of projected average daily maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the current period (2010–2030) under a high emissions scenario (RCP 6.0). 
### **Description** 
This metric evaluates current **Hazard Exposure** by providing the **10th percentile** estimate for daytime highs in a scenario where emissions peak around 2060. It serves as a baseline for the minimum expected current daytime heat hazard. 
* **Assumption:** Areas with higher projected average maximum temperatures face a greater chance of residents coming into contact with harmful heat-related events. 
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension. 
* **Caveat:** Values in this field are 90% likely to be exceeded by the actual average maximum temperature for this scenario. 
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
