### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 20 year return period 2020 10th percentile

#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the 10th percentile (low estimate) for current (2020 window) daytime maximums during a 5% annual-probability extreme event under RCP 4.5 into a standardized relative score. By standardizing across all scenarios and time periods, it highlights how these current minimum hazards compare to the full spectrum of projected climate futures in the database.

* **Assumption:** Positive relative Z-scores denote higher temperatures than average relative to the whole dataset, identifying current locations of acute relative extreme heat hazard.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Actual temperatures during such an event are statistically 90% likely to be higher than recorded in this field for this specific scenario and period.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** current period probabilistic maximum temperature projections.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
