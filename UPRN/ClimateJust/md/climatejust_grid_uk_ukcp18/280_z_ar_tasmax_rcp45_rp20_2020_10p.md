The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 20 year return period 2020 10th percentile' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp45_rp20_2020_10p) represents the standardized low estimate of current temperatures during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave under a moderate emissions scenario.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score converts the 10th percentile (low estimate) for current (2020 window) daytime maximums during a 5% annual-probability extreme event under RCP 4.5 into a standardized relative score.
 By standardizing across all scenarios and time periods, it highlights how these current minimum hazards compare to the full spectrum of projected climate futures in the database.
Assumption: Positive relative Z-scores denote higher temperatures than average relative to the whole dataset, identifying current locations of acute relative extreme heat hazard.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure.
Caveat: Actual temperatures during such an event are statistically 90% likely to be higher than recorded in this field for this specific scenario and period.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 current period probabilistic maximum temperature projections.
