#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized "likely" mid-century projection for daytime extreme maximums that have a 1% annual probability of occurrence. Standardizing across all scenarios allows these future rare hazards to be mapped on the same statistical scale as the current baseline and identifies neighbourhoods likely to face a significant shift in their relative extreme heat profile by mid-century.

* **Assumption:** Neighbourhood disadvantage is most acute where high socio-spatial vulnerability and acute increases in projected relative hazards converge.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** These probabilistic estimates represent conditions over a 20-year window and do not reflect absolute peak temperatures during an individual event.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
