The field **'Summer average temperature low emissions 2010-2030 10th percentile'** (internal name `tas_rcp26_2019_10p`) represents the low estimate of projected average summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2010–2030 under a low emissions scenario (RCP 2.6).

### **Description**
This metric evaluates **Hazard Exposure** by identifying the geographical distribution of projected temperatures. Within the Socio-Spatial Climate Vulnerability framework, this value represents the 10th percentile, meaning there is a 90% probability that the actual average temperature for this scenario and period will be higher than this value.

*   **Assumption:** Areas with higher projected average temperatures face a greater chance of residents coming into contact with heat-related hazard events, which can lead to negative impacts on health and well-being.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension, which is used alongside socio-spatial vulnerability indices to determine overall **Heat Disadvantage**.
*   **Caveat:** These probabilistic estimates are calculated over a 25km grid and represent averages over a 20-year window; they do not reflect specific peak temperatures during individual heatwave events.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** (United Kingdom Climate Projections 2018) probabilistic projections.
