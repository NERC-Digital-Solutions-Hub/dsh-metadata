The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature very high emissions 20 year return period 2080 median' (internal name tasmax_rcp85_rp20_2080_50p) represents the central estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period under RCP 8.5.
Description
This metric identifies the likely severity of a 5% annual-chance extreme temperature event at the end of the century in a world of rapidly rising emissions
. It identifyies neighbourhoods likely to face the most acute intensification of rare heat hazards by the 2080s
.
Assumption: Neighbourhoods projected to have the highest extreme temperatures face the greatest challenge in protecting sensitive residents from negative health impacts
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for the late-century
.
Caveat: These absolute temperature values represent the average condition over a 20-year window and carry higher uncertainty due to the late-century timeframe
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
