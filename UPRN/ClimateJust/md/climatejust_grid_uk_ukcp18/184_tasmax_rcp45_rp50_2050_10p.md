The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature moderate emissions 50 year return period 2050 10th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp45_rp50_2050_10p) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period under RCP 4.5
.
Description
This projection identifies the 10th percentile (low estimate) for extremely rare heat events (2% annual probability) three decades into the future
. It maps where the minimum expected mid-century extreme heat hazard remains relatively lower assuming a global transition to a low-carbon future begins mid-century
.
Assumption: neighbourhood disadvantage is most acute where high socio-spatial vulnerability and intensified future heat hazards converge
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for future scenarios
.
Caveat: Estimates represent conditions over a 20-year window (2040–2060) centered on 2050
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
