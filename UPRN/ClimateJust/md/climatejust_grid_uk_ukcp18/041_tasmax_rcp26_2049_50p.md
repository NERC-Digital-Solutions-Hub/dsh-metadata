The field **'Summer average maximum temperature low emissions 2040-2060 median'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp26_2049_50p`) represents the central estimate of projected average daily maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2040–2060 under a low emissions scenario (RCP 2.6).

### **Description**
This provides the **50th percentile (median)** projection for mid-century daytime highs. It identifies neighbourhoods likely to experience a shift in their hottest typical temperatures as a result of climate change.

*   **Assumption:** Intensifying heat hazards increase the potential for negative health and welfare impacts, especially in neighbourhoods where personal and social factors already limit adaptive capacity.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is a primary input for the **Hazard Exposure** component of the framework.
*   **Caveat:** This median value is an average and does not reflect the absolute peak temperatures reached during extreme heatwaves within that 20-year window.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
