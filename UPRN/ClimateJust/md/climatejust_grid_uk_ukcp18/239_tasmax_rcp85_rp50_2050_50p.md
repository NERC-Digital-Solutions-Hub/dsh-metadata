The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature very high emissions 50 year return period 2050 median' (internal name tasmax_rcp85_rp50_2050_50p) represents the central estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period under RCP 8.5.
Description
This metric provides the 50th percentile (median) projection for daytime extreme maximums that have a 2% annual probability of occurrence in the 2050s
. It identifyies neighbourhoods likely to face acute intensification of extremely rare heat hazards by mid-century due to rapid use of fossil fuels
.
Assumption: Higher extreme temperatures indicate an increased exposure risk, which can cause severe well-being losses in sensitive communities
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This is a primary input for the Hazard Exposure component for the mid-century
.
Caveat: While the median is a central estimate, absolute peak temperatures during an event may still exceed these averaged projections
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
