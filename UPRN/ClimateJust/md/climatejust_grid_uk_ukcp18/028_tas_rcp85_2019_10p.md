The field **'Summer average temperature very high emissions 2010-2030 10th percentile'** (internal name `tas_rcp85_2019_10p`) represents the low estimate of projected average temperatures during summer in Wales for the period 2010–2030 under a very high emissions scenario (RCP 8.5).

### Description

This metric evaluates near-term **Hazard Exposure** under an extreme emissions trajectory, providing the **10th percentile** (low estimate). It identifies the minimum expected heat hazard for communities today if global emissions rise rapidly.

* **Assumption:** Higher average summer temperatures indicate a baseline of exposure that can lead to losses in community well-being.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Actual temperatures are statistically 90% likely to be higher than these values in this extreme emissions scenario.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
