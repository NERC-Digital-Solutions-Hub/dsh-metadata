The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature moderate emissions 50 year return period 2080 90th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp45_rp50_2080_90p`) represents the high estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period under RCP 4.5.

### Description

This metric represents the **90th percentile (high estimate)**, providing a cautious "worst-case" projection for rare daytime extreme events by late-century. It identifies where rare extreme heat hazards could become catastrophically severe at the end of the century if climate response is high under moderate emission levels.

* **Assumption:** neighbourhood disadvantage is statistically higher where high social vulnerability and acute extreme heat hazards coincide.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for future scenarios.
* **Caveat:** High-end estimates represent a scenario that is only 10% likely to be exceeded according to the model.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
