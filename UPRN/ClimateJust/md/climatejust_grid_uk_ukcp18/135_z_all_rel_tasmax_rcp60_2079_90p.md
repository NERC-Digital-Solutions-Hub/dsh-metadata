The field 'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature high emissions 2070-2090 90th percentile' (internal name z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp60_2079_90p) represents the standardized high estimate of late-century average daily maximum summer temperatures under RCP 6.0
.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score standardized the high-end late-century projection against all other dataset scenarios
. It identifies geographical areas facing acute late-century daytime heat hazard exposure relative to the entire dataset in a high global emissions path if climate sensitivity is high
.
Assumption: Areas with the highest projected relative maximum temperatures face the greatest challenge in protecting sensitive residents from heat stress
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: This is a probabilistic high-estimate representing the top 10% of likely outcomes for this specific scenario
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 late-century maximum temperature data
.
