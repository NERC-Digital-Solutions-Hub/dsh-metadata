The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 50 year return period 2050 10th percentile' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp45_rp50_2050_10p) represents the standardized low estimate of mid-century temperatures during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave under RCP 4.5.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score converts the 10th percentile mid-century projection for 2% annual-chance extreme events into a standardized score
. It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face the minimum expected intensification of relative 50-year daytime heat hazards by the 2050s in a moderate emissions future
.
Assumption: Understand the relative geographical distribution of future relative extreme hazards is necessary for ensured justice in adaptation planning
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: Standardized scores simplify complex data but mask absolute Celsius values reached during individual events
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 mid-century probabilistic data
.
