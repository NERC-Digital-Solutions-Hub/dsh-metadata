The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature very high emissions 20 year return period 2050 10th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp85_rp20_2050_10p) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period under RCP 8.5.
Description
This projection identifies the 10th percentile low estimate for 5% annual-probability extreme heat events three decades into the future (2040–2060 window)
. It maps where the minimum expected mid-century extreme heat hazard remains relatively lower in a world where use of fossil fuels expands rapidly
.
Assumption: Understanding the geographical distribution of future extreme daytime highs is essential for planning long-term resilient infrastructure
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for future scenarios
.
Caveat: This low estimate means there is a 90% probability that actual temperatures during such a mid-century event would be higher than these values
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
