The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature low emissions 20 year return period 2080 median'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp26_rp20_2080_50p`) represents the central estimate (median) of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period under RCP 2.6.

### Description

This metric identifies the likely severity of a 5% annual-chance extreme temperature event at the end of the century in a global low-emissions future. It identifyies neighbourhoods likely to face intensified extreme heat hazards by late century even with successful global climate stabilization.

* **Assumption:** Neighbourhoods projected to have the highest extreme temperatures face the greatest challenge in protecting sensitive residents from negative health impacts.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for future scenarios.
* **Caveat:** These estimates represent conditions over a 20-year time period centered on 2080 (2070–2090).
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
