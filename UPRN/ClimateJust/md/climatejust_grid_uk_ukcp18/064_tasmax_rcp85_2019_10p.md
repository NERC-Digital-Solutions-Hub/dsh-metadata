The field **'Summer average maximum temperature very high emissions 2010-2030 10th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp85_2019_10p`) represents the low estimate of projected average daily maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the current period (2010–2030) under a very high emissions scenario (RCP 8.5). 
### **Description** 
This metric evaluates current **Hazard Exposure** under an extreme fossil-fuel-intensive emissions path. It provides the **10th percentile** estimate for the hottest part of a typical summer day today. 
* **Assumption:** Higher average maximum temperatures indicate a baseline of exposure that can lead to losses in community well-being. 
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension. 
* **Caveat:** Actual daytime maximum temperatures are statistically 90% likely to be higher than these values for this scenario. 
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
