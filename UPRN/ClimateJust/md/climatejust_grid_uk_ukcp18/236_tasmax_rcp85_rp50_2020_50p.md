The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature very high emissions 50 year return period 2020 median' (internal name tasmax_rcp85_rp50_2020_50p) represents the central estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave for the current period under RCP 8.5.
Description
This metric provides the 50th percentile (median) estimate for daytime extreme maximums today that have a 2% annual chance of occurrence in a world of rapidly rising greenhouse gas emissions
. It identifies the "likely" current severity of these extremely rare heat events
.
Assumption: Neighbourhood disadvantage is higher where high social vulnerability and intensified hazards for very rare events converge
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: Median values provide a central probabilistic outlook but should be viewed alongside high-end projections for emergency planning
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
