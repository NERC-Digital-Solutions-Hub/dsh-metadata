The field **'Summer average temperature moderate emissions 2010-2030 90th percentile'** (internal name `tas_rcp45_2019_90p`) represents the high estimate of projected average summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2010–2030 under a moderate emissions scenario (RCP 4.5).

### **Description**
This metric represents the **90th percentile** estimate for the current period. It identifies areas of high heat exposure risk today if global climate patterns are more sensitive to moderate levels of greenhouse gases than the central estimates suggest.

*   **Assumption:** Higher average temperatures correlate with a greater likelihood of hazardous extreme heat events that can impact community well-being.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** This is a high estimate; values in this field are likely to be above the actual average temperature 90% of the time.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
