The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature very high emissions 20 year return period 2020 median' (internal name tasmax_rcp85_rp20_2020_50p) represents the central estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the current period under RCP 8.5.
Description
This metric provides the 50th percentile (median) estimate for current extreme daytime temperatures (5% annual probability) in an unmitigated global emissions path
. It identifies the "likely" current severity of these rare heat events for communities today
.
Assumption: Increased exposure to extreme heat leads to higher social disadvantage when it coincides with neighbourhoods containing sensitive populations
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: While the median is a central estimate, absolute peak temperatures during a specific individual event may exceed these probabilistic 20-year averages
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
