The field **'Summer average temperature low emissions 2040-2060 10th percentile'** (internal name `tas_rcp26_2049_10p`) represents the low estimate of projected average summer temperatures in Wales for the mid-century period (2040–2060) under a low emissions scenario (RCP 2.6).

### **Description**
This metric projections the **10th percentile** (low estimate) for average temperatures three decades into the future. It is part of the **Hazard Exposure** suite used to map the long-term intensification of heat hazards across Welsh LSOAs.

*   **Assumption:** Higher projected temperatures represent a greater chance of exposure to heat hazards; combined with social vulnerability, this helps identify future areas of heat disadvantage.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for future scenarios.
*   **Caveat:** These projections are based on a 25km grid cell conforming to UKCP18 standards and represent the average over the 20-year period centered on 2050.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
