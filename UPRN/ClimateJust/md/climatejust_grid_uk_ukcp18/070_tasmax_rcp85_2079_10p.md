The field **'Summer average maximum temperature very high emissions 2070-2090 10th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp85_2079_10p`) represents the low estimate of projected average daily maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2070–2090 under RCP 8.5.

### Description

This projection maps the **10th percentile** low estimate for typical daytime highs at the end of the century under an extreme fossil-fuel trajectory.

* **Assumption:** higher daytime maximum temperatures increase the likelihood of heat-related well-being losses.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for the late-century.
* **Caveat:** There is a 90% probability that the actual average daytime highs will be higher than these values for this scenario.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
