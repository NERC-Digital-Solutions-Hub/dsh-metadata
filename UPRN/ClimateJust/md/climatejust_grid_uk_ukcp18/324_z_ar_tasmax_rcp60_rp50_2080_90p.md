#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** standardized the high-end late-century projection for very rare 2% annual-chance daytime maximums against all other database scenarios. It identifies geographical areas potentially facing catastrophic late-century relative extreme heat hazard exposure relative to the entire dataset average if the climate system is highly sensitive to high greenhouse gas levels.

*   **Assumption:** Positive relative Z-scores highlight neighbourhoods where future climate change is likely to most significantly increase social inequalities.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
*   **Caveat:** Statistically, actual temperatures during such an event are 90% likely to be below these values for this specific scenario and period.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** late-century probabilistic projections.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
