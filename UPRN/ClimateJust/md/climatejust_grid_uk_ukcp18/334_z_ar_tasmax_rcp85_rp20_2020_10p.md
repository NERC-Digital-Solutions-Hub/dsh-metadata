### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature very high emissions 20 year return period 2020 10th percentile

#### Description

This metric is an **"All-Relative" Z-score** converting the 10th percentile (low estimate) for current (2020 window) daytime maximums during a 5% annual-probability extreme event into a standardized relative score. By standardizing across all scenarios and time periods, it highlights how these current minimum hazards compare to the entire spectrum of climate futures in a world tracking towards very high emissions.

* **Assumption:** Neighbourhoods with higher relative scores indicate a baseline of exposure that can lead to significant well-being losses.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is a standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** assessment.
* **Caveat:** Actual physical temperatures during such an event are statistically 90% likely to be higher than recorded in this field.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** current period probabilistic maximum temperature projections.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
