The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature moderate emissions 20 year return period 2020 10th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp45_rp20_2020_10p`) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave in the current period under a moderate emissions scenario (RCP 4.5).

### Description

This metric evaluates **Hazard Exposure** by identifying the severity of a "20-year return period" event—an extreme daytime temperature that has a 5% (1 in 20) annual probability of occurrence. It provides the **10th percentile** low estimate for the 2020 window (2010–2030), mapping the minimum expected current extreme heat hazard for communities today.

* **Assumption:** Areas with higher projected temperatures during heatwaves are likely to see more severe community well-being losses when combined with high social vulnerability.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Actual current temperatures during such an event are statistically 90% likely to be higher than these values.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
