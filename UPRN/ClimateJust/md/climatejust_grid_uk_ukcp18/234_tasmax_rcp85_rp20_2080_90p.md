The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature very high emissions 20 year return period 2080 90th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp85_rp20_2080_90p) represents the high estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period under RCP 8.5.
Description
This metric represents the 90th percentile high estimate, providing a cautious "worst-case" projection for rare daytime extreme events by late-century
. It identifies where late-century extreme heat hazards could become catastrophic under very high global emissions if the climate responds aggressively
.
Assumption: Neighbourhood disadvantage is statistically higher where high biophysical sensitivity and acute extreme heat hazards coincide
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for future scenarios
.
Caveat: This is a high-end projection; statistally, actual temperatures during such an event are 90% likely to be below these values for this specific scenario and period
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
