The field **'Summer average maximum temperature low emissions 2040-2060 10th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp26_2049_10p`) represents the low estimate of projected average maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the mid-century period (2040–2060) under a low emissions scenario (RCP 2.6).

### Description

This projection provides the **10th percentile** low estimate for typical daytime highs three decades into the future under a successful global transition to low emissions.

* **Assumption:** Higher projected maximum temperatures represent a greater chance of exposure to heat hazards; combined with social vulnerability, this helps identify future areas of heat disadvantage.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for future scenarios.
* **Caveat:** Estimates represent the average condition over a 20-year period centered on 2050.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
