The field **'Summer average temperature very high emissions 2070-2090 10th percentile'** (internal name `tas_rcp85_2079_10p`) represents the low estimate of projected summer average temperatures in Wales for the late-century period (2070–2090) under a very high emissions scenario (RCP 8.5).

### **Description**
This projection maps the **10th percentile** low estimate for temperatures at the end of the century. It provides a long-term outlook for the minimum expected baseline heat hazard across Wales under an extreme global emissions trajectory.

*   **Assumption:** Understanding the geographical distribution of future heat hazards is necessary for long-term community resilience and justice in adaptation planning.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for the late-century time horizon.
*   **Caveat:** Late-century projections are probabilistic and carry greater uncertainty regarding the actual outcomes of extreme emissions pathways.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
