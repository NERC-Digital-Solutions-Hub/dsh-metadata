The field **'Summer average maximum temperature moderate emissions 2070-2090 median'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp45_2079_50p`) represents the central estimate of projected average daily maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2070–2090 under RCP 4.5.

### Description

This metric represents the **50th percentile (median)** projection for late-century typical daytime highs. It identifies neighbourhoods likely to face a significant permanent shift in their summer climate profile by the 2080s.

* **Assumption:** neighbourhood disadvantage is most acute where high social vulnerability and intensified future heat hazards converge, identifying where the most severe well-being losses are likely to occur.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for future scenarios.
* **Caveat:** It represents an average over a 20-year window and may mask shorter, more intense periods of extreme heat stress.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
