The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature very high emissions 20 year return period 2020 10th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp85_rp20_2020_10p) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature at the hottest part of the day during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave in the current period under a very high emissions scenario (RCP 8.5).
Description
This metric evaluates Hazard Exposure by identifying the severity of a "20-year return period" event—an extreme daytime temperature with a 5% annual probability of occurrence
. It provides the 10th percentile low estimate for the 2020 window (2010–2030), mapping the minimum expected current extreme heat hazard in a world failing to commit to a low-carbon future
.
Assumption: Higher relative temperatures indicate a baseline of exposure that can lead to losses in community well-being
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: Actual current temperatures during such an event are statistically 90% likely to be higher than these values
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
