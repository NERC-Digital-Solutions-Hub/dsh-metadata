The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature very high emissions 2040-2060 median'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp85_2049_50p`) represents the standardized central estimate of projected mid-century average daily maximum summer temperatures under RCP 8.5.

### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized "likely" mid-century projection for typical daytime highs—the temperature at the hottest part of a summer's day. Standardizing across all scenarios allows these future maximums to be statistically mapped on the same scale as the present day and other warming scenarios. It identifies neighbourhoods facing acute shifts in their relative heat profile compared to the database average by the 2050s in a world where global fossil fuel use expands rapidly.

* **Assumption:** neighbourhood disadvantage is higher where high social vulnerability and intensified relative daytime heat hazards coincide.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** assessment.
* **Caveat:** These probabilistic estimates represent averages over a 20-year window (2040–2060) and may mask shorter, more intense periods of extreme heat stress.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** probabilistic mid-century maximum temperature projections.
