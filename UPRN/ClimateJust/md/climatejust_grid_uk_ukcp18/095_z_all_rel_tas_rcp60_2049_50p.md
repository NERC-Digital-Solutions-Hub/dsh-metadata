The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature high emissions 2040-2060 median'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tas_rcp60_2049_50p`) represents the standardized central estimate of mid-century summer temperatures under RCP 6.0.

### **Description**
This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized measure of likely mid-century summer temperatures in a world with continued high global emissions. It highlights neighbourhoods that will face extreme relative hazard intensification by the 2050s.

*   **Assumption:** Heat disadvantage is highest where biological sensitivity meets acute increases in projected relative temperatures.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
*   **Caveat:** Standardized scores simplify complex data but mask the absolute Celsius value.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century projections.
