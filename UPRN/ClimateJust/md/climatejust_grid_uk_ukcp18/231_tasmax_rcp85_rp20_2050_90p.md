The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature very high emissions 20 year return period 2050 90th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp85_rp20_2050_90p) represents the high estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period under RCP 8.5.
Description
This metric represents the 90th percentile high estimate, providing a cautious projection for rare daytime extreme events by mid-century under extreme emissions growth
. It identifyies where rare extreme heat hazards could become dangerously severe if global climate sensitivity is high
.
Assumption: Neighbourhood disadvantage is statistically higher where high social vulnerability and acute extreme heat hazards coincide
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for future scenarios
.
Caveat: This high-end estimate represents a scenario that is only 10% likely to be exceeded according to the probabilistic model
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
