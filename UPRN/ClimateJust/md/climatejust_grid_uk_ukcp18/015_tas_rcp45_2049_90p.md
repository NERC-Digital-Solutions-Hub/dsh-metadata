The field 'Summer average temperature moderate emissions 2040-2060 90th percentile' (internal name tas_rcp45_2049_90p) represents the high estimate of projected average summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2040–2060 under a moderate emissions scenario (RCP 4.5).
Description
This is the 90th percentile projection for mid-century temperatures. It identifies areas potentially facing acute heat hazards by the 2050s if moderate emissions are paired with high climate sensitivity
.
Assumption: Higher projected temperatures signify a greater potential for communities to come into contact with heat-related events, leading to more severe health impacts
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: This high estimate represents a scenario that is only 10% likely to be exceeded according to the probabilistic model
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
