#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the near-term **90th percentile (high estimate)** projection for exceptionally rare 2% annual-probability heat events into a standardized relative score. It identifies geographical areas facing acute current relative extreme heat exposure if global climate patterns are more sensitive to high greenhouse gas levels than central estimates suggest.

* **Assumption:** Higher relative maximum temperatures signify a greater potential for residents to experience catastrophic heat-related impacts.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Values in this field represent a high-impact climate outcome, statistically 90% likely to be higher than actual recorded temperatures for this period.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** current period probabilistic projections.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
