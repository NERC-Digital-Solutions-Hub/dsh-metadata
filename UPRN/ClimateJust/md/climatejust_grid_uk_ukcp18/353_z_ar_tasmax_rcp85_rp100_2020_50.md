### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature very high emissions 100 year return period 2020 median

#### Description

This provides a standardized measure of current severity for extremely rare 1% annual-chance heat events.

* **Assumption:** Increased exposure raises social disadvantage.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Actual peak temperatures may exceed averages.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** projections.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
