#### Description

This metric is an **"All-Relative" Z-score** converting the low estimate for current extremely rare heat events into a standardized relative score across all climate futures.

* **Assumption:** Higher values indicate increased baseline exposure.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Physical temperatures are likely higher than shown.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** data.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
