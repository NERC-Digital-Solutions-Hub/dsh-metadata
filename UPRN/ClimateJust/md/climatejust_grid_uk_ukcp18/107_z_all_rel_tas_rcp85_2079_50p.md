The field 'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature very high emissions 2070-2090 median' (internal name z_all_rel_tas_rcp85_2079_50p) represents the standardized central estimate of late-century summer average temperatures under RCP 8.5.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score provides a standardized measure of the "likely" late-century summer temperatures in an unmitigated emissions world
. Positive values denote neighbourhoods where extreme relative heat hazards will be significantly higher than the average calculated across all database years and scenarios
.
Assumption: Positive relative Z-scores highlight where future climate change is most likely to increase existing social inequalities
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: Standardized scores mask absolute Celsius values; users should review these alongside actual temperature maps
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 summer average temperature projections
.
