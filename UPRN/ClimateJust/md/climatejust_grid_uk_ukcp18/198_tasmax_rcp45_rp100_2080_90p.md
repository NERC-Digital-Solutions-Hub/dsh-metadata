The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature moderate emissions 100 year return period 2080 90th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp45_rp100_2080_90p`) represents the high estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period under RCP 4.5.

### **Description**
This metric represents the **90th percentile (high estimate)**, providing a cautious "worst-case" projection for rare daytime extreme events by the end of the century. It identifies where late-century extreme heat hazards could become catastrophic if the climate responds aggressively to greenhouse gases.

*   **Assumption:** neighbourhood disadvantage is statistically higher where high biophysical sensitivity and acute extreme heat hazards coincide.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for future scenarios.
*   **Caveat:** High estimates represent a scenario that is only 10% likely to be exceeded according to the model.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
