The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 100 year return period 2080 median' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp26_rp100_2080_50) represents the standardized central estimate of late-century temperatures during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave under RCP 2.6.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score provides a standardized measure of the likely late-century hottest part of an exceptionally rare 1% annual-chance extreme summer day.
 It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face extreme intensification of relative daytime extreme hazards compared to the database average by the 2080s even with successful global climate stabilization.
Assumption: Positive relative Z-scores highlight neighbourhoods where future climate change is likely to most significantly increase social inequalities and quality-of-life losses.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure.
Caveat: Values represent conditions over a 20-year window and do not capture absolute peak temperatures reached during a specific event.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 late-century maximum temperature projections.
