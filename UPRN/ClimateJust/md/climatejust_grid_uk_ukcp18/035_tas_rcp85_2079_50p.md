The field 'Summer average temperature very high emissions 2070-2090 median' (internal name tas_rcp85_2079_50p) represents the central estimate of projected average temperatures for the period 2070–2090 in Wales under a very high emissions scenario (RCP 8.5)
.
Description
This metric provides the 50th percentile (median) estimate for average summer temperatures in the 2080s. It identifies neighbourhoods likely to face extreme intensification of heat hazards by late century if global emissions follow an unmitigated fossil-fuel-dependent trajectory
.
Assumption: Positive values denote neighbourhoods where extreme heat hazards will be higher than average, requiring the most intensive climate adaptation efforts
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: It represents an average temperature across summer months over a 20-year period and masks shorter, more intense heat stress events
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
