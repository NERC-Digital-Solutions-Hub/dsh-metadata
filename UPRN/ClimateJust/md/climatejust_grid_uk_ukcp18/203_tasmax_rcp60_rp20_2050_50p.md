The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature high emissions 20 year return period 2050 median' (internal name tasmax_rcp60_rp20_2050_50p) represents the central estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period under RCP 6.0
.
Description
This metric provides the 50th percentile (median) projection for typical extreme daytime highs that have a 5% annual chance of occurrence in the 2050s
. It identifies neighbourhoods likely to face a significant permanent shift in their extreme temperature profile by mid-century due to high global emissions
.
Assumption: neighbourhood disadvantage is most acute where high socio-spatial vulnerability and intensified future heat hazards converge
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This is a primary input for the Hazard Exposure component for the mid-century
.
Caveat: Median values provide a central probabilistic outlook but absolute peak temperatures during a specific event may still be higher
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
