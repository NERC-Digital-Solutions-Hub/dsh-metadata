The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature high emissions 2040-2060 90th percentile'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp60_2049_90p`) represents the standardized high estimate of projected mid-century average daily maximum summer temperatures under RCP 6.0.

### **Description**
This **"All-Relative" Z-score** standardized the high-end mid-century projection for typical daytime highs against all other database scenarios. It identifies neighbourhoods facing acute mid-century daytime heat exposure relative to the entire dataset in a high emissions global future if climate sensitivity is high.

*   **Assumption:** Higher relative maximum temperatures correlate with an increased likelihood of communities coming into contact with harmful heat events.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
*   **Caveat:** This is a high-impact climate outcome, representing a scenario with only a 10% chance of being exceeded according to the probabilistic model.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** probabilistic mid-century maximum temperature data.
