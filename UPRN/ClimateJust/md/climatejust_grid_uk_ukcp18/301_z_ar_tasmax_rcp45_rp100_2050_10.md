#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the 10th percentile (low estimate) mid-century projection for 1% annual-chance extreme heat events into a standardized score. It identifies neighbourhoods where the minimum expected intensification of these exceptionally rare hazards by the 2050s (2040–2060) remains relatively low compared to the entire spectrum of climate futures in the database.

* **Assumption:** Understanding the geographical distribution of future relative extreme hazards is essential for ensuring justice in adaptation planning.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** There is a 90% probability that actual temperatures during such a mid-century event would be higher than the values recorded here.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century probabilistic data.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
