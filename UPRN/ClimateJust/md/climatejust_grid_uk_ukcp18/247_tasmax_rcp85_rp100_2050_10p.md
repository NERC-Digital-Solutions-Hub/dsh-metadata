The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature very high emissions 100 year return period 2050 10th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp85_rp100_2050_10p) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period under RCP 8.5
.
Description
This projection identifies the 10th percentile low estimate for extremely rare heat events (1% annual probability) three decades into the future (2040–2060 window)
. It maps where the minimum expected mid-century extreme heat hazard remains relatively lower in a world where use of fossil fuels expands rapidly
.
Assumption: Understanding the geographical distribution of future extremely rare heat hazards is necessary for just adaptation and resilient infrastructure planning
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for future scenarios
.
Caveat: Statistically, actual temperatures during a mid-century 100-year event are 90% likely to be higher than the values recorded here
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
