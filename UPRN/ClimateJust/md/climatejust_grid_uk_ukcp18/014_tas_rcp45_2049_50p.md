The field **'Summer average temperature moderate emissions 2040-2060 median'** (internal name `tas_rcp45_2049_50p`) represents the central estimate of projected average summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2040–2060 under a moderate emissions scenario (RCP 4.5).

### **Description**
This provides the **50th percentile (median)** projection for average summer temperatures in the 2050s. This specific scenario (RCP 4.5, 50th percentile) is the **recommended starting point** for most climate risk and vulnerability assessments.

*   **Assumption:** neighbourhoods where this increased hazard exposure coincides with high sensitivity and low adaptive capacity are identified as having high heat disadvantage.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** This projection is an average of 20 years and conforms to climate scenario outputs from the UKCP18 project.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
