The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature moderate emissions 50 year return period 2080 10th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp45_rp50_2080_10p`) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period under RCP 4.5.

### **Description**
This projection maps the **10th percentile (low estimate)** for very rare extreme heat events (2% annual probability) toward the end of the century. It provides a long-term outlook for the minimum expected intensification of catastrophic heat hazards across Wales in a world that transitions slowly to a low-carbon future.

*   **Assumption:** Understanding the geographical distribution of future rare heat hazards is necessary for long-term community resilience and justice in adaptation planning.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for the late-century.
*   **Caveat:** late-century projections carry significant probabilistic uncertainty and carry higher levels of uncertainty due to the long time scales.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
