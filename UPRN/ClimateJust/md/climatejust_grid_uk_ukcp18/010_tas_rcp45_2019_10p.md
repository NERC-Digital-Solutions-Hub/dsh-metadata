The field **'Summer average temperature moderate emissions 2010-2030 10th percentile'** (internal name `tas_rcp45_2019_10p`) represents the low estimate of projected average summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2010–2030 under a moderate emissions scenario (RCP 4.5).

### **Description**
This metric evaluates near-term **Hazard Exposure** by providing the **10th percentile** estimate for average summer temperatures under moderate emissions. It serves as a baseline for understanding the minimum expected heat hazard in the current period.

*   **Assumption:** Higher temperatures indicate a higher chance of communities experiencing heat-related health and welfare impacts.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** This low estimate means it is 90% likely that actual average temperatures will be higher than the values recorded in this field.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
