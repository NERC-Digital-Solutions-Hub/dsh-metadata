The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature moderate emissions 2040-2060 10th percentile'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tas_rcp45_2049_10p`) represents the standardized low estimate of mid-century summer average temperatures under RCP 4.5.

### **Description**
This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the 10th percentile projection for the 2050s into a standardized value. It identifyies neighbourhoods where the minimum expected mid-century heat hazard remains relatively low compared to all possible database scenarios.

*   **Assumption:** Understanding the geographical distribution of future relative heat hazards is essential for just adaptation planning.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
*   **Caveat:** These probabilistic estimates represent averages over a 20-year window (2040–2060).
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century temperature projections.
