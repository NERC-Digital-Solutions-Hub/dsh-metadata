The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature low emissions 20 year return period 2080 10th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp26_rp20_2080_10p`) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period (2080 window) under RCP 2.6.

### **Description**
This projection maps the **10th percentile** (low estimate) for rare extreme heat events toward the end of the century. It provides a long-term outlook for the minimum expected intensification of heat hazards across Wales in a world with successful carbon mitigation.

*   **Assumption:** Mapping future relative hazard distributions helps prioritize long-term resilient infrastructure for vulnerable communities.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for the late-century.
*   **Caveat:** Late-century projections carry significant probabilistic uncertainty and carry higher levels of uncertainty due to the long time scales.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
