The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature high emissions 20 year return period 2050 90th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp60_rp20_2050_90p`) represents the high estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period under RCP 6.0.

### **Description**
This metric represents the **90th percentile** high estimate, providing a cautious projection for rare daytime extreme events by the 2050s. It identifies where mid-century extreme heat hazard exposure could become acute if global climate sensitivity is high in a world of rising emissions.

*   **Assumption:** neighbourhood disadvantage is higher where high social vulnerability and acute extreme hazards for rare events coincide.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for future scenarios.
*   **Caveat:** This is a probabilistic high-estimate, representing a scenario with only a 10% chance of being exceeded.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
