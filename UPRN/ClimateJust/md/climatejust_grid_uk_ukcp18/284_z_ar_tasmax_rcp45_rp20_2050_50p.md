#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized "likely" mid-century projection for extreme daytime maximums that have a 5% annual probability of occurrence. Standardizing across all scenarios allows these future rare hazards to be mapped on the same statistical scale as the present day and more extreme warming scenarios, highlighting where intensification of hazards and high social vulnerability may converge.

* **Assumption:** Neighbourhood disadvantage is most acute where high socio-spatial vulnerability and acute increases in projected relative hazards converge.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Standardized scores identify geographical hotspots of hazard but mask absolute Celsius values reached during individual events.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century maximum temperature projections.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
