The field **'Summer average temperature moderate emissions 2070-2090 median'** (internal name `tas_rcp45_2079_50p`) represents the central estimate of projected average summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2070–2090 under a moderate emissions scenario (RCP 4.5).

### Description

This metric provides the **50th percentile (median)** estimate for average summer temperatures in the 2080s. It is used to evaluate the likely shift in heat hazard exposure for future generations in Wales under moderate emissions.

* **Assumption:** neighbourhoods projected to have the highest temperatures are the most at risk for well-being losses if they also have high biophysical sensitivity.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** It represents an average temperature across all summer months over a 20-year period and may mask shorter, more intense periods of heat stress.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
