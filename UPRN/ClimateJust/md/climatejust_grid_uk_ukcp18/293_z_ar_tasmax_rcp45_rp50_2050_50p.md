### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 50 year return period 2050 median

#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized "likely" mid-century projection for extreme daytime maximums that have a 2% annual probability of occurrence. Standardizing across all scenarios identifies neighbourhoods likely to face a significant shift in their relative extreme heat profile, allowing for direct comparison against current baselines and other futures.

* **Assumption:** Neighbourhood disadvantage is most acute where high socio-spatial vulnerability and intensified future relative extreme hazards converge.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Median values provide a central probabilistic outlook but absolute peak temperatures during an event may still be higher.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century maximum temperature projections.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
