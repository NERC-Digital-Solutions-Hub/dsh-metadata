The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 50 year return period 2050 median' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp45_rp50_2050_50p) represents the standardized central estimate of projected mid-century temperatures during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave under RCP 4.5.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score provides a standardized "likely" mid-century projection for extreme daytime maximums that have a 2% annual probability of occurrence
. Standardizing across all scenarios identifies neighbourhoods likely to face a significant shift in their relative extreme heat profile, allowing for direct comparison against current baselines and other futures
.
Assumption: Neighbourhood disadvantage is most acute where high socio-spatial vulnerability and intensified future relative extreme hazards converge
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: Median values provide a central probabilistic outlook but absolute peak temperatures during an event may still be higher
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 mid-century maximum temperature projections
.
