The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature high emissions 100 year return period 2020 median'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp60_rp100_2020_50p`) represents the central estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave for the current period under RCP 6.0.

### Description

This metric provides the **50th percentile (median)** estimate for daytime extreme maximums that have a 1% annual chance of occurrence today. It identifies the "likely" current severity of these extremely rare heat events for communities today in a world of high global emissions.

* **Assumption:** Increased exposure to extreme heat leads to higher social disadvantage when it coincides with neighbourhoods containing sensitive populations.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Median values provide a central probabilistic outlook but should be viewed alongside absolute high-end projections for emergency management.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
