The field 'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 2070-2090 90th percentile' (internal name z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp45_2079_90p) represents the standardized high estimate of late-century average daily maximum summer temperatures under RCP 4.5
.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score standardized the high-end late-century projection against all other dataset scenarios
. It identifies geographical areas potentially facing catastrophic late-century daytime heat hazard exposure relative to the entire dataset if climate sensitivity is high under moderate emission levels
.
Assumption: Positive relative Z-scores highlight neighbourhoods where future climate change is likely to most significantly increase social inequalities
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: This is a probabilistic high-estimate representing the top 10% of likely temperature outcomes for this specific scenario
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 late-century maximum temperature data
.
