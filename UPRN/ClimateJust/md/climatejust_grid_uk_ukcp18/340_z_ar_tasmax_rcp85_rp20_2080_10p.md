### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature very high emissions 20 year return period 2080 10th percentile

#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the 10th percentile projection for the 2080 window (2070–2090) during a 5% annual-probability heat event into a standardized score. It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face the minimum expected intensification of relative daytime heat hazards toward the end of the century in a world of unmitigated global emissions growth.

* **Assumption:** Understanding the relative geographical distribution of future hazards is essential for ensuring justice in adaptation planning.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Late-century projections carry significant probabilistic uncertainty and do not reflect absolute Celsius values.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** late-century probabilistic data.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
