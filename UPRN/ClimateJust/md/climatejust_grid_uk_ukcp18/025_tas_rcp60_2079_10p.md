The field **'Summer average temperature high emissions 2070-2090 10th percentile'** (internal name `tas_rcp60_2079_10p`) represents the low estimate of projected summer average temperatures in Wales for the late-century period (2070–2090) under a high emissions scenario (RCP 6.0).

### Description

This projection maps the **10th percentile** (low estimate) for temperatures towards the end of the century. It provides a long-term outlook for the minimum expected heat hazard rise across Wales under high global emissions.

* **Assumption:** Understanding the geographical distribution of future heat hazards is necessary for long-term community resilience and justice in adaptation planning.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for the late-century time horizon.
* **Caveat:** Late-century projections carry higher levels of uncertainty due to the long time scales and complex climate feedback mechanisms.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
