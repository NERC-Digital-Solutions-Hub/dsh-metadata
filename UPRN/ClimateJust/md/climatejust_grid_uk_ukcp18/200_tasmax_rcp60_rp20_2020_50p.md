The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature high emissions 20 year return period 2020 median'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp60_rp20_2020_50p`) represents the central estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the current period under a high emissions scenario (RCP 6.0).

### Description

This metric evaluates **Hazard Exposure** by identifying the likely severity of a "20-year return period" event—an extreme daytime temperature that has a 5% (1 in 20) annual probability of occurring. Within the framework, it provides the **50th percentile (median)** estimate for the 2020 window (2010–2030), representing the "likely" current severity of such events in a world where global greenhouse gas emissions continue to rise before peaking around 2060.

* **Assumption:** Areas with higher projected extreme temperatures during heatwaves face a greater risk of significant community well-being losses, especially when these hazards coincide with high social vulnerability.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension, which is combined with socio-spatial vulnerability indices to determine overall **Heat Disadvantage**.
* **Caveat:** While the median is a central estimate, absolute peak temperatures during a specific individual event may exceed these probabilistic 20-year averages.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** (United Kingdom Climate Projections 2018) probabilistic projections.
