The field **'Summer average temperature low emissions 2040-2060 median'** (internal name `tas_rcp26_2049_50p`) represents the central estimate of projected average summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2040–2060 under a low emissions scenario (RCP 2.6).

### Description

This metric represents the **50th percentile (median)** projection for mid-century summer temperatures. It provides a standard baseline for assessing how heat hazard exposure will shift by the 2050s under a low emissions pathway.

* **Assumption:** Intensifying heat hazards increase the potential for negative health and welfare impacts, especially in neighbourhoods where personal and social factors already limit adaptive capacity.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is a primary input for the **Hazard Exposure** component of the framework.
* **Caveat:** This median value is an average and does not reflect the maximum temperatures reached during extreme heatwaves within that 20-year window.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
