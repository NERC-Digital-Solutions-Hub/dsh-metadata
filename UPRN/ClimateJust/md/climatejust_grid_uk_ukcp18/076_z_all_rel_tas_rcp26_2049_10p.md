The field 'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature low emissions 2040-2060 10th percentile' (internal name z_all_rel_tas_rcp26_2049_10p) represents the standardized low estimate of projected summer average temperatures for mid-century under a low emissions scenario.
Description
This metric is an "All-Relative" Z-score, meaning it is calculated using the mean and standard deviation across all four considered temperature scenarios and all time periods in the database
. It converts the 10th percentile (low estimate) for mid-century (2040–2060) summer temperatures under RCP 2.6 into a standardized score, allowing for direct statistical comparison between this specific future world and the current baseline
.
Assumption: Higher relative temperatures indicate a greater potential for residents to come into contact with harmful heat-related events, which can adversely affect community well-being
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This is a standardized input for the Hazard Exposure component of the framework
.
Caveat: Standardized scores identify geographical hotspots of hazard relative to the entire dataset but do not provide an absolute measure of physical heat stress
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 probabilistic summer average temperature projections
.
