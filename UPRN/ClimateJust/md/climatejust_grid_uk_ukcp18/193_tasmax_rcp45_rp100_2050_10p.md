The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature moderate emissions 100 year return period 2050 10th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp45_rp100_2050_10p`) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period under RCP 4.5.

### Description

This projection identifies the **10th percentile (low estimate)** for extremely rare heat events (1% annual probability) three decades into the future. It maps where the minimum expected mid-century extreme heat hazard remains relatively lower assuming global carbon mitigation efforts begin mid-century.

* **Assumption:** Understanding the geographical distribution of future extremely rare heat hazards is necessary for just adaptation and resilient infrastructure planning.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for future scenarios.
* **Caveat:** Estimates represent conditions over a 20-year window (2040–2060) centered on 2050.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
