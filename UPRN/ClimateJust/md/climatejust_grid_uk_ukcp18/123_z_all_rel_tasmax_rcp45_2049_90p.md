The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 2040-2060 90th percentile'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp45_2049_90p`) represents the standardized high estimate of projected mid-century average daily maximum summer temperatures under RCP 4.5.

### **Description**
This **"All-Relative" Z-score** standardized the high-end mid-century projection for typical daytime highs against all other dataset scenarios. It identifies geographical areas facing acute mid-century daytime heat exposure relative to the entire dataset if the climate is highly sensitive to moderate greenhouse gas levels.

*   **Assumption:** Higher relative maximum temperatures correlate with an increased likelihood of communities coming into contact with harmful heat events.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
*   **Caveat:** This is a probabilistic high-estimate, representing a scenario with only a 10% probability of being exceeded according to the model.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** current period probabilistic maximum temperature data.
