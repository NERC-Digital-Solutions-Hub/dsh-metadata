The field **'Summer average maximum temperature high emissions 2070-2090 10th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp60_2079_10p`) represents the low estimate of projected average daily maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2070–2090 under RCP 6.0. 
### **Description** 
This metric provided the **10th percentile** low estimate for late-century daytime maximums. It identifies areas with the minimum expected intensification of heat hazards under a high global emissions trajectory. 
* **Assumption:** Mapping future daytime maximum temperatures helps prioritize resilient infrastructure for neighbourhoods likely to see significant warming. 
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for the 2080s. 
* **Caveat:** Actual temperatures are 90% likely to be higher than these values in this high-emissions late-century scenario. 
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
