The field **'Summer average temperature low emissions 2010-2030 median'** (internal name `tas_rcp26_2019_50p`) represents the central estimate of projected average summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2010–2030 under a low emissions scenario (RCP 2.6).

### Description

This metric provides the **50th percentile (median)** estimate for average temperatures during the summer months. Within the framework, it is used to identify areas with a likely chance of coming into contact with higher baseline temperatures as a result of a low-emissions climate pathway.

* **Assumption:** Higher average temperatures indicate an increased exposure risk, where the intensity and frequency of heat events are more likely to cause well-being losses in vulnerable communities.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension of the Socio-Spatial Climate Vulnerability Index.
* **Caveat:** While the median is a recommended starting point for analysis, it represents the middle of a range of probabilistic outcomes and does not account for extreme outlier years.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections on a 25km grid.
