The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature high emissions 50 year return period 2050 10th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp60_rp50_2050_10p) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period under RCP 6.0
.
Description
This projection identifies the 10th percentile low estimate for extremely rare heat events (2% annual probability) three decades into the future
. It maps where the minimum expected mid-century extreme heat hazard remains relatively lower assuming global emissions continue to follow a high trajectory
.
Assumption: Understanding the geographical distribution of future very rare heat hazards is necessary for just adaptation and resilient infrastructure planning
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for future scenarios
.
Caveat: This low estimate means there is a 90% probability that actual temperatures during a 50-year event in the 2050s would be higher than the values recorded here
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
