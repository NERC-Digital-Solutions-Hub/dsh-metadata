The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature very high emissions 100 year return period 2080 10th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp85_rp100_2080_10p) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period under RCP 8.5
.
Description
This projection maps the 10th percentile low estimate for extremely rare heat events (1% annual probability) toward the end of the century
. It provides a long-term outlook for the minimum expected intensification of catastrophic heat hazards in a world of unmitigated global emissions
.
Assumption: Mapping future rare hazard distributions helps prioritize long-term resilient infrastructure for the most vulnerable communities
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for the late-century
.
Caveat: Late-century projections carry significant probabilistic uncertainty and carry higher levels of uncertainty due to the long time scales
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
