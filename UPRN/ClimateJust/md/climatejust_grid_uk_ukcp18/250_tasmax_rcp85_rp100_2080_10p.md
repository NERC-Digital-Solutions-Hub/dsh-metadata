#### Description

This projection maps the **10th percentile** low estimate for extremely rare heat events (1% annual probability) toward the end of the century. It provides a long-term outlook for the minimum expected intensification of catastrophic heat hazards in a world of unmitigated global emissions.

*   **Assumption:** Mapping future rare hazard distributions helps prioritize long-term resilient infrastructure for the most vulnerable communities.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for the late-century.
*   **Caveat:** Late-century projections carry significant probabilistic uncertainty and carry higher levels of uncertainty due to the long time scales.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
