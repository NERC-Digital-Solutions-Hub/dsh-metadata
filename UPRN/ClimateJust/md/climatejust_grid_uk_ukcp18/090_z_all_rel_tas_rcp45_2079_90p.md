The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature moderate emissions 2070-2090 90th percentile'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tas_rcp45_2079_90p`) represents the standardized high estimate of late-century summer temperatures under RCP 4.5.

### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** standardized the high-end late-century projection against all other dataset scenarios. It identifyies areas potentially facing catastrophic future heat hazard today if climate response to moderate emissions is high.

* **Assumption:** Areas with the highest projected relative temperatures face the greatest challenge in protecting residents from heat-related health impacts.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** This is a probabilistic high-estimate representing the top 10% of likely temperature outcomes for this specific scenario.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** late-century projections.
