The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature very high emissions 50 year return period 2080 10th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp85_rp50_2080_10p) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave at the end of the century under a very high emissions scenario (RCP 8.5)
.
Description
This metric evaluates Hazard Exposure by identifying the minimum expected severity of a very rare daytime heat event—one with only a 2% (1 in 50) annual probability of occurring
. It provides the 10th percentile projection for the 2080 window (the 20-year average from 2070–2090) in a world where global fossil fuel use continues to expand rapidly
.
Assumption: Areas with higher projected temperatures during very rare extreme heatwaves face a greater risk of catastrophic impacts on community health and well-being if social vulnerability is high
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This indicator informs the Hazard Exposure dimension, which is combined with socio-spatial vulnerability indices to determine overall Heat Disadvantage
.
Caveat: These absolute temperature values represent the average condition over a 20-year window and carry higher uncertainty due to the late-century timeframe
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 (United Kingdom Climate Projections 2018) probabilistic projections
.
