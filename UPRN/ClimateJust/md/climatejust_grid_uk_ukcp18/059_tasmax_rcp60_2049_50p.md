The field 'Summer average maximum temperature high emissions 2040-2060 median' (internal name tasmax_rcp60_2049_50p) represents the central estimate of projected average daily maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2040–2060 under RCP 6.0
.
Description
This provides the 50th percentile (median) projection for average mid-century daytime maximums under a high emissions path
. It identifies neighbourhoods likely to experience a permanent shift in typical summer temperatures by the 2050s
.
Assumption: Intensifying heat hazards increase the potential for negative health and welfare impacts, especially in neighbourhoods where social factors already limit adaptive capacity
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This is a primary input for the Hazard Exposure component for the mid-century
.
Caveat: This median value is an average and does not reflect the absolute peak temperatures reached during extreme heatwaves within that window
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
