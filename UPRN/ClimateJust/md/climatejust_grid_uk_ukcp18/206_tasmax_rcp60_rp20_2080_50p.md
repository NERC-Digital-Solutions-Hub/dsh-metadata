The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature high emissions 20 year return period 2080 median'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp60_rp20_2080_50p`) represents the central estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period under RCP 6.0.

### Description

This metric identifies the likely severity of a 5% annual-chance extreme temperature event at the end of the century in a high-emissions world. It identifyies neighbourhoods likely to face extreme intensification of rare heat hazards by the 2080s.

* **Assumption:** Neighbourhoods projected to have the highest extreme temperatures face the greatest challenge in protecting sensitive residents from negative health impacts.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for future scenarios.
* **Caveat:** These estimates represent conditions over a 20-year window centered on 2080 (2070–2090) and do not reflect absolute peak temperatures during a specific individual event.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
