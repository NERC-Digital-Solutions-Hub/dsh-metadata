The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature high emissions 20 year return period 2050 10th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp60_rp20_2050_10p) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period under RCP 6.0
.
Description
This projection identifies the 10th percentile low estimate for 5% annual-probability extreme heat events three decades into the future
. It maps where the minimum expected mid-century extreme heat hazard remains relatively lower assuming global emissions peak around 2060
.
Assumption: Understanding the relative geographical distribution of future extreme daytime highs is essential for ensure justice in long-term adaptation planning
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for future scenarios
.
Caveat: This low estimate means there is a 90% probability that actual temperatures during a mid-century 20-year event would be higher than the values recorded here
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
