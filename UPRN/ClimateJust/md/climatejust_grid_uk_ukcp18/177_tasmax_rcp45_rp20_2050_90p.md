The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature moderate emissions 20 year return period 2050 90th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp45_rp20_2050_90p) represents the high estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period under RCP 4.5.
Description
This metric represents the 90th percentile high estimate, providing a cautious projection for rare daytime extreme events by mid-century under moderate global emissions.
 It identifyies where rare extreme heat hazards could become dangerously severe by the 2050s if climate sensitivity to greenhouse gases is high.
Assumption: Neighbourhood disadvantage is higher where high social vulnerability and acute rare hazards coincide.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for future scenarios.
Caveat: This is a probabilistic high-estimate, representing a scenario with only a 10% chance of being exceeded.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections.
