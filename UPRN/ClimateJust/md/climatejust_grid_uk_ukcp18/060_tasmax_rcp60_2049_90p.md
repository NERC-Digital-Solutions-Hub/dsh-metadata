The field **'Summer average maximum temperature high emissions 2040-2060 90th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp60_2049_90p`) represents the high estimate of projected average daily maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2040–2060 under RCP 6.0. 
### **Description** 
This provides a **90th percentile** high-estimate projection for mid-century typical daytime highs. It identifying where the combination of high emissions and high climate sensitivity could lead to dangerous future heat environments. 
* **Assumption:** Higher daytime highs equate to a higher likelihood of communities coming into contact with harmful heat events, driving up geographical disadvantage. 
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension. 
* **Caveat:** This is a high-end probabilistic estimate used for cautious long-term planning. 
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
