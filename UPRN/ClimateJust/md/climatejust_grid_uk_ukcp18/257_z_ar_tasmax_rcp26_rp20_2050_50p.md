### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 20 year return period 2050 median

#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized "likely" mid-century projection for extreme daytime maximums that have a 5% annual probability of occurrence. It identifies neighbourhoods likely to face a significant permanent shift in their relative extreme heat profile by the 2050s, especially where increased hazard exposure coincides with high social vulnerability.

*   **Assumption:** Neighbourhood disadvantage is most acute where high socio-spatial vulnerability and intensified future relative extreme hazards converge.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
*   **Caveat:** Standardized scores simplify complex probabilistic data but mask absolute peak temperatures during an event.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
