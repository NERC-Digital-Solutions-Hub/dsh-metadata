The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 20 year return period 2050 median' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp26_rp20_2050_50p) represents the standardized central estimate of projected mid-century 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave temperatures under RCP 2.6
.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score provides a standardized "likely" mid-century projection for extreme daytime maximums that have a 5% annual probability of occurrence
. It identifies neighbourhoods likely to face a significant permanent shift in their relative extreme heat profile by the 2050s, especially where increased hazard exposure coincides with high social vulnerability
.
Assumption: Neighbourhood disadvantage is most acute where high socio-spatial vulnerability and intensified future relative extreme hazards converge
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: Standardized scores simplify complex probabilistic data but mask absolute peak temperatures during an event
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
