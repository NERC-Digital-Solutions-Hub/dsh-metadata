The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature very high emissions 2040-2060 10th percentile'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp85_2049_10p`) represents the standardized low estimate of mid-century average daily maximum summer temperatures under a very high emissions scenario.

### **Description**
This metric is an **"All-Relative" Z-score**, meaning it is calculated using the mean and standard deviation across all four considered temperature scenarios and all time periods in the database. It converts the 10th percentile (low estimate) projection for mid-century (the 20-year window from 2040–2060) typical daytime highs into a standardized relative score. It identifies neighbourhoods where the minimum expected future hazard remains relatively low compared to the entire spectrum of climate futures.

*   **Assumption:** Higher relative daytime maximum temperatures indicate a baseline of exposure that can lead to losses in community well-being.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is a standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** Standardized scores highlight relative risk within the context of all possible warming levels but do not quantify absolute physical risk in degrees Celsius.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections on a 25km grid.
