The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature high emissions 100 year return period 2080 10th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp60_rp100_2080_10p) represents the low estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period under RCP 6.0.
Description
This projection identifies the 10th percentile low estimate for extremely rare heat events (1% annual probability) toward the end of the century. It maps where the minimum expected late-century extreme heat hazard remains relatively lower in a world where global emissions begin to stabilize after 2060
.
Assumption: Mapping future rare hazard distributions helps prioritize long-term resilient infrastructure for vulnerable communities
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for the late-century time horizon
.
Caveat: This low estimate means there is a 90% probability that actual temperatures during such a late-century event would be higher than the values recorded here
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
