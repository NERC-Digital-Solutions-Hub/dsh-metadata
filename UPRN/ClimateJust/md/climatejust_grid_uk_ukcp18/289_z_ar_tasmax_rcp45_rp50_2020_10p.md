The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 50 year return period 2020 10th percentile' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp45_rp50_2020_10p) represents the standardized low estimate of current temperatures during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave under RCP 4.5.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score converts the 10th percentile (low estimate) for the 2020 window during an extremely rare daytime heat event—one with only a 2% annual probability—into a standardized relative score
. By standardizing across all possible warming levels in the database, it highlights where the minimum expected baseline relative extreme hazard today remains relatively low
.
Assumption: Neighbourhood disadvantage is higher where high social vulnerability and intensified hazards for extremely rare events converge
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: These absolute temperature values are statistically 90% likely to be higher than recorded in this field for this specific period
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 current period probabilistic data
.
