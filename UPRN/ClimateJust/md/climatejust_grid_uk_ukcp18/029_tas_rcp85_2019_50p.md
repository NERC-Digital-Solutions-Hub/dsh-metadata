The field 'Summer average temperature very high emissions 2010-2030 median' (internal name tas_rcp85_2019_50p) represents the central estimate of projected daily average temperatures during summer in Wales for the period 2010–2030 under a very high emissions scenario (RCP 8.5)
.
Description
This represents the 50th percentile (median) estimate for current heat hazard exposure under a very high emissions path. It provides a standard baseline for assessing near-term risks in a world failing to commit to low-carbon transitions
.
Assumption: Increased heat exposure leads to higher social disadvantage when it coincides with neighbourhoods containing sensitive populations
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: It represents a 20-year average centered on 2020 on a 25km grid, meaning it may not capture local urban heat island effects
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
