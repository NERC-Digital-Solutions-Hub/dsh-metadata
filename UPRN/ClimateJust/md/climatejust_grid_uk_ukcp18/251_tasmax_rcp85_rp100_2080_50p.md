#### Description

This metric provides the **50th percentile (median)** estimate for the severity of a 1% annual-chance extreme temperature event at the end of the century. It identifies neighbourhoods likely to face the most acute intensification of rare heat hazards by the 2080s due to high global warming.

* **Assumption:** Neighbourhoods projected to have the highest extreme temperatures face the greatest challenge in protecting sensitive residents from catastrophic health impacts.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for future scenarios.
* **Caveat:** Values represent conditions over the 20-year window centered on 2080 (2070–2090) and do not reflect absolute peak temperatures during an individual event.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
