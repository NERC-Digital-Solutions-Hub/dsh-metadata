The field **'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 100 year return period 2080 median'** (internal name `z_ar_tasmax_rcp45_rp100_2080_50`) represents the standardized central estimate of projected late-century temperatures during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave under RCP 4.5.

### **Description**
This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized measure of the likely late-century hottest part of an exceptionally rare 1% annual-chance extreme summer day. It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face extreme intensification of relative daytime extreme hazards compared to the database average by the 2080s, even in a world that transitions toward a low-carbon future.

* **Assumption:** Neighbourhoods with the highest relative maximum temperatures face the greatest challenge in protecting sensitive residents from catastrophic impacts.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Values around 0 in this field denote that projected relative extreme hazards are around the average for the entire database.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** late-century maximum temperature projections.
