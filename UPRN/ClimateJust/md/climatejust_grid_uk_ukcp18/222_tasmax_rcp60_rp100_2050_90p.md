The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature high emissions 100 year return period 2050 90th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp60_rp100_2050_90p) represents the high estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period under RCP 6.0.
Description
This metric identifies the 90th percentile high estimate for daytime maximums during an extremely rare heat event by the 2050s. It maps areas potentially facing acute mid-century catastrophic heat exposure if the climate system is more sensitive to greenhouse gases than central estimates predict
.
Assumption: Neighbourhood disadvantage is statistically higher where high social vulnerability and acute extreme hazards for rare events converge
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: Statistically, actual temperatures during such an event are 90% likely to be below these high-end values for this specific scenario and period
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
