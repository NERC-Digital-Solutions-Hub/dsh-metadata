The field 'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature very high emissions 2040-2060 median' (internal name z_all_rel_tas_rcp85_2049_50p) represents the standardized central estimate of projected mid-century summer temperatures under RCP 8.5.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score provides a standardized "likely" mid-century projection for a typical summer's day average temperature in an unmitigated global emissions path
. Standardizing across all scenarios identifies neighbourhoods facing the most acute shift in their relative heat profile compared to the database average by the 2050s
.
Assumption: Neighbourhood disadvantage is most acute where high socio-spatial vulnerability meets acute increases in projected relative temperatures
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This is a standardized input for the Hazard Exposure component
.
Caveat: Standardized scores simplify complex data but do not reflect absolute peak temperatures reached during specific heatwaves
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 mid-century average temperature projections
.
