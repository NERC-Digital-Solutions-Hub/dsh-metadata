The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature high emissions 2040-2060 10th percentile'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tas_rcp60_2049_10p`) represents the standardized low estimate of mid-century summer average temperatures under RCP 6.0.

### **Description**
This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the 10th percentile mid-century estimate into a standardized score. It allowings for statistical comparison between the minimum expected intensification of heat hazards in the 2050s and the dataset average.

*   **Assumption:** Understanding the geographical distribution of future relative heat hazards is essential for protecting the most vulnerable.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
*   **Caveat:** Probabilistic estimates represent average conditions over a 20-year window centered on 2050.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century temperature projections.
