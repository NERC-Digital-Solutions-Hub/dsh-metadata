The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature moderate emissions 20 year return period 2050 median'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp45_rp20_2050_50p`) represents the central estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period under RCP 4.5.

### Description

This metric provides a standardized **50th percentile (median)** projection for daytime extreme maximums that have a 5% annual probability of occurrence. It identifies neighbourhoods likely to face a significant shift in their extreme heat profile by the 2050s, especially where increased hazard exposure coincides with low social resilience.

* **Assumption:** Higher extreme temperatures indicate an increased exposure risk, which can lead to negative impacts on community well-being.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is a primary input for the **Hazard Exposure** component for the mid-century.
* **Caveat:** Median estimates are recommended starting points but do not account for absolute "worst-case" climate response.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
