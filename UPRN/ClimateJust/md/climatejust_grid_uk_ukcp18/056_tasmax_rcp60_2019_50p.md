The field 'Summer average maximum temperature high emissions 2010-2030 median' (internal name tasmax_rcp60_2019_50p) represents the central estimate of projected average daily maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2010–2030 under RCP 6.0
.
Description
This metric provides the 50th percentile (median) estimate for typical daytime highs in the current period
. It identifies the "likely" near-term hazard baseline for communities in a world where global greenhouse gas emissions continue to rise
.
Assumption: Increased heat exposure leads to higher social disadvantage when it coincides with neighbourhoods containing sensitive populations
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: It represents a 20-year average centered on 2020 on a 25km grid and may not capture local urban heat island effects
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
