The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature high emissions 50 year return period 2080 median'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp60_rp50_2080_50p`) represents the central estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period under RCP 6.0.

### **Description**
This metric provides the **50th percentile (median)** estimate for the severity of a 2% annual-chance extreme temperature event at the end of the century. It identifies neighbourhoods likely to face intensified rare extreme heat hazards by late century due to unmitigated emissions.

*   **Assumption:** Neighbourhoods projected to have the highest extreme temperatures face the greatest challenge in protecting sensitive residents from catastrophic health impacts.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for future scenarios.
*   **Caveat:** Values represent conditions over the 20-year window centered on 2080 (2070–2090) and do not reflect absolute peak temperatures during a specific individual event.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
