The field **'Summer extreme maximum temperature moderate emissions 20 year return period 2020 90th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp45_rp20_2020_90p`) represents the high estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the current period under RCP 4.5.

### **Description**
This metric identifies the **90th percentile** high estimate for daytime maximums during a rare heat event today. It maps areas facing acute current heat exposure if global climate patterns are more sensitive to moderate levels of greenhouse gases than central estimates suggest.

*   **Assumption:** Higher temperatures equate to a higher likelihood of communities coming into contact with harmful heat events, driving up geographical disadvantage.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** High estimates represent a scenario that is only 10% likely to be exceeded according to the probabilistic model.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
