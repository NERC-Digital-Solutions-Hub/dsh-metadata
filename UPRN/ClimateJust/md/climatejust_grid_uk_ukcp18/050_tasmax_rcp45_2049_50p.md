The field 'Summer average maximum temperature moderate emissions 2040-2060 median' (internal name tasmax_rcp45_2049_50p) represents the central estimate of projected average daily maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2040–2060 under a moderate emissions scenario (RCP 4.5)
.
Description
This provides the 50th percentile (median) projection for average mid-century daytime maximums. This specific scenario (RCP 4.5, 50th percentile) is the recommended starting point for most climate risk and vulnerability assessments
.
Assumption: Neighbourhoods where this increased hazard exposure coincides with high sensitivity and low adaptive capacity are identified as having high heat disadvantage
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: This median value is an average and does not reflect the absolute peak temperatures reached during extreme heatwaves within that 20-year window
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
