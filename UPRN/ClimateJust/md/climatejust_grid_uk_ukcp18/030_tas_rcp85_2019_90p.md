The field **'Summer average temperature very high emissions 2010-2030 90th percentile'** (internal name `tas_rcp85_2019_90p`) represents the high estimate of projected summer average temperatures in Wales for the period 2010–2030 under a very high emissions scenario (RCP 8.5).

### Description

This metric represents the **90th percentile** estimate, identifying areas of acute near-term heat exposure if global climate sensitivity is high and emissions rise rapidly.

* **Assumption:** Higher temperatures signify a greater potential for communities to come into contact with heat-related events, leading to more severe health impacts.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** This is a high-end projection; values in this field are likely to be above the actual average temperature 90% of the time for this scenario.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
