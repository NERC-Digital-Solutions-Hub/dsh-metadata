### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature high emissions 100 year return period 2080 median

#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized measure of the likely late-century hottest part of an exceptionally rare 1% annual-chance extreme summer day. It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face extreme intensification of relative daytime extreme hazards compared to the database average by the 2080s in a high global emissions future.

* **Assumption:** Neighbourhoods with the highest relative maximum temperatures face the greatest challenge in protecting sensitive residents from catastrophic health impacts.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Standardized scores simplify complex data but mask absolute peak physical temperatures reached during specific heatwaves.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** late-century maximum temperature projections.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
