The field **'Summer average maximum temperature high emissions 2070-2090 90th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp60_2079_90p`) represents the high estimate of projected average daily maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2070–2090 under RCP 6.0.

### Description

This represents a **90th percentile** projection, identifying a high-end daytime temperature scenario for the late 21st century. It maps where daytime heat hazard exposure could become catastrophic under a high global emissions path.

* **Assumption:** Areas with the highest projected maximum temperatures face the greatest challenge in protecting residents from negative well-being impacts.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** This high-end estimate represents a scenario that is only 10% likely to be exceeded according to the probabilistic model.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
