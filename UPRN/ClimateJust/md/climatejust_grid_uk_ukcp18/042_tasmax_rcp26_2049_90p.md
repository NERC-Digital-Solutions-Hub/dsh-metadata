The field 'Summer average maximum temperature low emissions 2040-2060 90th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp26_2049_90p) represents the high estimate of projected average maximum daily summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2040–2060 under a low emissions scenario (RCP 2.6)
.
Description
This metric provides a 90th percentile high-estimate projection for mid-century daytime maximums
.
Assumption: Higher daytime highs equate to a higher likelihood of communities coming into contact with harmful heat events, driving up geographical disadvantage
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: Users are encouraged to view this alongside median estimates to understand the range of uncertainty in future daytime heating response
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
