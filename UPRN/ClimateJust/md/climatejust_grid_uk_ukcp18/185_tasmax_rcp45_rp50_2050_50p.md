The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature moderate emissions 50 year return period 2050 median' (internal name tasmax_rcp45_rp50_2050_50p) represents the central estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave for the mid-century period under RCP 4.5
.
Description
This metric provides a standardized 50th percentile (median) projection for daytime extreme maximums that have a 2% annual probability of occurrence in the 2050s
. It identifies neighbourhoods likely to face significantly more severe rare heat hazards by mid-century as a result of global climate change
.
Assumption: Higher extreme temperatures indicate an increased exposure risk, which can lead to negative impacts on community well-being
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This is a primary input for the Hazard Exposure component for the mid-century
.
Caveat: Absolute peak temperatures during an event may still exceed these averaged 20-year projections
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
