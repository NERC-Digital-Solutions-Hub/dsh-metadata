The field **'Summer average temperature low emissions 2010-2030 90th percentile'** (internal name `tas_rcp26_2019_90p`) represents the high estimate of projected average summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2010–2030 under a low emissions scenario (RCP 2.6).

### Description

This metric represents the **90th percentile** estimate, providing a more cautious "high-end" projection for average summer temperatures. It identifies areas that could experience significant heat exposure even under a low-emissions scenario if climate sensitivity is at the higher end of projections.

* **Assumption:** Areas projected to have higher temperatures are assumed to have a greater chance of residents experiencing heat-related events that adversely affect community health and quality of life.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** This is a probabilistic high-end estimate and should be used to plan for worst-case outcomes within the specified emissions scenario.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
