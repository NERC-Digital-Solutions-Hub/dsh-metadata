The field 'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature very high emissions 2040-2060 90th percentile' (internal name z_all_rel_tas_rcp85_2049_90p) represents the standardized high estimate of projected mid-century summer temperatures under RCP 8.5.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score standardized the high-end mid-century projection against all other dataset scenarios
. It identifies geographical areas projected to face potentially catastrophic future heat exposure by the 2050s if the climate is highly sensitive to a world of unmitigated emissions growth
.
Assumption: Higher relative temperatures indicate an increased exposure risk, which can cause severe well-being losses in sensitive communities
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: This is a probabilistic high-estimate, representing a "worst-case" scenario for the mid-century period
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 probabilistic climate projections
.
