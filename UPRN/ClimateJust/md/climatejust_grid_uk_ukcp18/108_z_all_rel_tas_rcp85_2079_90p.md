The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature very high emissions 2070-2090 90th percentile'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tas_rcp85_2079_90p`) represents the standardized high estimate of late-century summer average temperatures under RCP 8.5.

### **Description**
This **"All-Relative" Z-score** standardized the high-end late-century projection for the extreme RCP 8.5 pathway against all other database scenarios. It identifies areas potentially facing catastrophic late-century relative heat hazard exposure if climate sensitivity is high.

*   **Assumption:** Neighbourhoods with high relative temperatures face the greatest challenge in protecting residents from heat-related health and welfare impacts.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
*   **Caveat:** This probabilistic high-estimate represents the top 10% of likely temperature outcomes for this specific late-century scenario.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** probabilistic climate projections.
