The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature high emissions 100 year return period 2080 median' (internal name tasmax_rcp60_rp100_2080_50p) represents the central estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period under RCP 6.0.
Description
This metric provides the 50th percentile (median) projection for the severity of a 1% annual-chance extreme temperature event at the end of the century (2070–2090 window)
. It identifies neighbourhoods likely to face a significant shift in their extreme temperature profile by the 2080s due to high global emissions
.
Assumption: Neighbourhoods projected to have the highest extreme temperatures face the greatest challenge in protecting sensitive residents from catastrophic health impacts
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This is a primary input for the Hazard Exposure component for the late-century
.
Caveat: While the median is a central estimate, absolute peak temperatures during an event may still exceed these averaged 20-year projections
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
