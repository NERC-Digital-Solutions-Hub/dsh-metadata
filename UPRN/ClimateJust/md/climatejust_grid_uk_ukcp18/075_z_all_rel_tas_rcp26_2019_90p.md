The field 'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature low emissions 2010-2030 90th percentile' (internal name z_all_rel_tas_rcp26_2019_90p) represents the standardized high estimate (90th percentile) of summer average temperatures for 2010–2030 under RCP 2.6
.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score converts the high-end near-term temperature projection into a standardized relative score
. It identifies areas of high current heat exposure relative to the Welsh national average across the entire spectrum of considered scenarios
.
Assumption: positive Z-scores highlight neighbourhoods where current extreme temperatures are significantly above average, representing high baseline hazard exposure
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This is a standardized input for the Hazard Exposure dimension
.
Caveat: This is a relative score and does not identify absolute physical risk without being combined with social vulnerability
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 summer average temperature data
.
