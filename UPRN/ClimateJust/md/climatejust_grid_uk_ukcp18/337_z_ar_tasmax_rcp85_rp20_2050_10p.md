#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the 10th percentile mid-century projection for 5% annual-chance extreme events into a standardized score. It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face the minimum expected intensification of relative 20-year daytime heat hazards by the 2050s in a world where global use of fossil fuels expands rapidly.

* **Assumption:** Mapping future relative hazard distributions is necessary for just adaptation and resilient infrastructure planning.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Standardized scores simplify complex data but mask absolute Celsius values reached during individual events.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century probabilistic data.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
