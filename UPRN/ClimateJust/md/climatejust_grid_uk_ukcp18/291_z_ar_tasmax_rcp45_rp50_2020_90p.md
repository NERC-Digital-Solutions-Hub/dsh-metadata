The field 'Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 50 year return period 2020 90th percentile' (internal name z_ar_tasmax_rcp45_rp50_2020_90p) represents the standardized high estimate of current temperatures during a 1-in-50 year extreme heatwave under RCP 4.5.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score converts the near-term high-end projection for 2% annual-chance heat events into a standardized relative score
. It identifies areas facing acute current extreme heat exposure relative to the entire dataset average if the climate system responds more aggressively to moderate greenhouse gas levels than central estimates predict
.
Assumption: Higher relative temperatures signify a greater potential for residents to experience catastrophic heat-related impacts
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: High estimates represent a scenario that is only 10% likely to be exceeded according to the model
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 current period projections
.
