### **Description** 
This provides the **90th percentile** high-estimate projection for average daily maximums late in the century. It identifies areas potentially facing catastrophic future daytime heat if climate sensitivity is high under a moderate emissions pathway. 
* **Assumption:** neighbourhoods projected to have the highest maximum temperatures face the greatest challenge in protecting sensitive residents from heat-related health impacts. 
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension. 
* **Caveat:** This probabilistic high-end estimate is intended for cautious long-term planning, as it represents a high-impact climate outcome. 
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
