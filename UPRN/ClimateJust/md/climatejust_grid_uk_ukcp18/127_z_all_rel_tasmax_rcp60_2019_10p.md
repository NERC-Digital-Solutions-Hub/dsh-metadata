The field 'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature high emissions 2010-2030 10th percentile' (internal name z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp60_2019_10p) represents the standardized low estimate of current average daily maximum summer temperatures under a high emissions scenario
.
Description
This metric is an "All-Relative" Z-score, meaning it is calculated using the mean and standard deviation across all scenarios in the database
. It converts the 10th percentile (low estimate) for current (2010–2030) daytime maximums under RCP 6.0 into a standardized relative score to allow for direct comparison between the present and future
.
Assumption: Higher relative daytime highs indicate a baseline of exposure that can lead to losses in community well-being
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: Values in this field are statistically 90% likely to be exceeded by the actual average daytime maximum for this specific scenario
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 current period probabilistic maximum temperature data
.
