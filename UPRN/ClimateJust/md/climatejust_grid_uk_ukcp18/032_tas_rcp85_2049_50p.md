The field **'Summer average temperature very high emissions 2040-2060 median'** (internal name `tas_rcp85_2049_50p`) represents the central estimate of projected average temperatures for the period 2040–2060 in Wales under a very high emissions scenario (RCP 8.5).

### **Description**
This provides the **50th percentile (median)** projection for average mid-century temperatures. It identifies areas of potentially acute hazard intensification as a result of a fossil-fuel-intensive global future.

*   **Assumption:** Heat disadvantage is highest where biological sensitivity and low adaptive capacity meet acute increases in projected temperatures.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** It represents an average temperature across summer months and does not reflect maximum temperatures reached during individual extreme events.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
