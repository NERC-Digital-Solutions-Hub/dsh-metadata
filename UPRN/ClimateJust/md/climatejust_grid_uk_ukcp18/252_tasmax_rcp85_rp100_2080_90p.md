The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature very high emissions 100 year return period 2080 90th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp85_rp100_2080_90p) represents the high estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-100 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period under RCP 8.5
.
Description
This metric represents the 90th percentile high estimate, providing a cautious "worst-case" projection for rare daytime extreme events by late-century
. It identifies where late-century extreme heat hazards could become catastrophically severe at the end of the century if the climate responds aggressively to extreme emissions growth
.
Assumption: Neighbourhood disadvantage is statistically higher where high social vulnerability and acute extreme hazards for extremely rare events converge
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for future scenarios
.
Caveat: High estimates represent a scenario that is only 10% likely to be exceeded according to the model
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
