#### Description

This metric represents the **90th percentile** high estimate, providing a cautious "worst-case" projection for rare daytime extreme events by late-century. It identifies where late-century extreme heat hazards could become catastrophically severe at the end of the century if the climate responds aggressively to extreme emissions growth.

*   **Assumption:** Neighbourhood disadvantage is statistically higher where high social vulnerability and acute extreme hazards for extremely rare events converge.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for future scenarios.
*   **Caveat:** High estimates represent a scenario that is only 10% likely to be exceeded according to the probabilistic model.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
