The field 'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature low emissions 2070-2090 90th percentile' (internal name z_all_rel_tas_rcp26_2079_90p) represents the standardized high estimate (90th percentile) of summer average temperatures for late-century under RCP 2.6.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score standardized the high-end projection for the 2080s against all other scenarios. It identifies geographical areas facing acute late-century heat exposure relative to the entire dataset, even in a low-emissions global future
.
Assumption: Higher relative temperatures correlate with an increased likelihood of communities coming into contact with harmful heat events
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: This represents a "high-end" scenario that is statistically 90% likely to be higher than the actual average temperature for this specific period and scenario
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
