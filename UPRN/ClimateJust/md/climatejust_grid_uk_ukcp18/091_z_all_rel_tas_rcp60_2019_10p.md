The field 'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature high emissions 2010-2030 10th percentile' (internal name z_all_rel_tas_rcp60_2019_10p) represents the standardized low estimate of projected summer temperatures for the current period under a high emissions scenario.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score converts the near-term 10th percentile estimate for a high emissions path (RCP 6.0) into a standardized value. It maps the current relative baseline hazard for communities if global emissions follow a high trajectory
.
Assumption: Understanding current relative heat hazard exposure is a starting point for assessing heat disadvantage in vulnerable populations
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: values in this field are statistically 90% likely to be exceeded by the actual average temperature for this scenario
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
