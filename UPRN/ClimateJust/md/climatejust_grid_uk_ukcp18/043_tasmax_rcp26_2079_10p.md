The field **'Summer average maximum temperature low emissions 2070-2090 10th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp26_2079_10p`) represents the low estimate of projected summer average maximum temperatures in Wales for the late-century period (2070–2090) under a low emissions scenario (RCP 2.6).

### Description

This projection maps the **10th percentile** low estimate for typical daytime highs in the 2080s.

* **Assumption:** Understanding the geographical distribution of future daytime maximum temperatures is necessary for long-term community resilience and justice in adaptation planning.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for the late-century time horizon.
* **Caveat:** Late-century projections carry higher levels of uncertainty due to the long time scales and complex climate feedback mechanisms.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
