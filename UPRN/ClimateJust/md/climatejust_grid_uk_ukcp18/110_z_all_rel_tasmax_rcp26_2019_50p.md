The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature low emissions 2010-2030 median'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp26_2019_50p`) represents the standardized central estimate (median) of current average daily maximum summer temperatures under RCP 2.6.

### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized measure of the likely hottest part of a typical summer's day today in a world that successfully transitions to low global emissions. Standardizing across all scenarios identifies neighbourhoods that are currently "relatively hot" compared to the entire spectrum of climate futures in the database.

* **Assumption:** neighbourhoods with higher relative daytime temperatures are more likely to see negative well-being impacts when combined with high social sensitivity.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Values around 0 in this field denote that the LSOA's typical daytime maximum is around the average for the entire database.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** maximum temperature projections.
