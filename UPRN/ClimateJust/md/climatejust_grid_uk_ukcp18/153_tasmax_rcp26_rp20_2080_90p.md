The field 'Summer extreme maximum temperature low emissions 20 year return period 2080 90th percentile' (internal name tasmax_rcp26_rp20_2080_90p) represents the high estimate of the expected temperature during a 1-in-20 year extreme heatwave for the late-century period under RCP 2.6
.
Description
This metric represents the 90th percentile high estimate, providing a cautious "worst-case" projection for rare daytime extreme events by the end of the century under low global emissions
. It identifies areas potentially facing catastrophic late-century daytime heat hazards if global climate sensitivity is high
.
Assumption: neighbourhood disadvantage is statisticaly higher where high social vulnerability and acute extreme heat hazards coincide
.
Vulnerability Dimension: This informs the Hazard Exposure dimension for future scenarios
.
Caveat: High-end estimates represent a scenario that is only 10% likely to be exceeded according to the model
.
Data Source: Derived from UKCP18 probabilistic projections
.
