The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature high emissions 2040-2060 10th percentile'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp60_2049_10p`) represents the standardized low estimate of mid-century average daily maximum summer temperatures under RCP 6.0.

### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** converts the 10th percentile projection for mid-century (2040–2060) typical daytime highs into a standardized score. It identifies neighbourhoods projected to face the minimum expected intensification of relative daytime heat hazards by the 2050s in a world where emissions peak around 2060.

* **Assumption:** Higher relative maximum temperatures signify a greater potential for communities to come into contact with harmful heat events.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
* **Caveat:** Probabilistic estimates represent average conditions over a 20-year window centered on 2050.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century maximum temperature projections.
