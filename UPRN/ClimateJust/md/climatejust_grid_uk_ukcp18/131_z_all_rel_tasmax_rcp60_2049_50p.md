The field **'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature high emissions 2040-2060 median'** (internal name `z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp60_2049_50p`) represents the standardized central estimate of projected mid-century average daily maximum summer temperatures under RCP 6.0.

### **Description**
This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized "likely" mid-century projection for typical daytime highs. Standardizing across all scenarios allows these future daytime maximums to be statistically mapped on the same scale as the current baseline and identifies neighbourhoods facing extreme intensification of relative daytime maximums by mid-century.

*   **Assumption:** neighbourhood disadvantage is most acute where high socio-spatial vulnerability meets acute increases in projected relative temperatures.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** Standardized input for **Hazard Exposure**.
*   **Caveat:** Standardized scores simplify complex data but mask absolute Celsius temperatures reached during specific heatwaves.
*   **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** mid-century maximum temperature projections.
