The field 'Summer average Relative Z-score maximum temperature moderate emissions 2010-2030 90th percentile' (internal name z_all_rel_tasmax_rcp45_2019_90p) represents the standardized high estimate of current average daily maximum summer temperatures under RCP 4.5.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score converts the near-term high-end daytime maximum projection into a standardized relative score
. It identifies where current relative daytime heat exposure could be significantly above the dataset average if global climate sensitivity is high under moderate emissions
.
Assumption: Higher relative maximum temperatures indicate an increased exposure risk, which can cause well-being losses in sensitive communities
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: This is a high-end projection; values in this field are likely to be above the actual average maximum temperature 90% of the time
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 probabilistic climate projections
