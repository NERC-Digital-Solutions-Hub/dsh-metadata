### Summer extreme Relative Z-score maximum temperature high emissions 50 year return period 2020 median

#### Description

This **"All-Relative" Z-score** provides a standardized measure of the likely current severity of a 2% annual-chance daytime heat event today. Standardizing across all scenarios highlights neighbourhoods that are currently "relatively hot" during these very rare events compared to the full spectrum of climate futures in the database.

* **Assumption:** Neighbourhoods with higher relative scores indicate a baseline of exposure that can lead to significant well-being losses when social vulnerability is high.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is a standardized input for the **Hazard Exposure** component.
* **Caveat:** While the median is a central estimate, absolute peak temperatures during an event may still exceed these standardized 20-year averages.
* **Data Source:** Standardized from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.

#### Interpretation

This indicator is a relative Z-score. Higher values indicate greater relative exposure to extreme heat compared to the full dataset, while lower values indicate comparatively reduced exposure.
