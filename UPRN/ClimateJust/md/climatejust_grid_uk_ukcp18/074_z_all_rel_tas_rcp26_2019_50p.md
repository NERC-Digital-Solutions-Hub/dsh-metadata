The field 'Summer average Relative Z-score temperature low emissions 2010-2030 median' (internal name z_all_rel_tas_rcp26_2019_50p) represents the standardized central estimate (median) of summer average temperatures for the current period under RCP 2.6
.
Description
This "All-Relative" Z-score converts the median projected temperature into a standardized value
. By standardizing across all time periods and scenarios, it places current summer temperatures on the same statistical scale as the projected extremes of later centuries
.
Assumption: Values around 0 denote that current summer temperatures are around the average for the entire database, while negative values represent areas that are currently "relatively cool" compared to all potential futures
.
Vulnerability Dimension: Standardized input for Hazard Exposure
.
Caveat: Standardized scores simplify complex probabilistic data but mask the absolute Celsius temperature value
.
Data Source: Standardized from UKCP18 summer average temperature projections
.
