The field **'Summer average maximum temperature high emissions 2040-2060 10th percentile'** (internal name `tasmax_rcp60_2049_10p`) represents the low estimate of projected average daily maximum summer temperatures in Wales for the period 2040–2060 under RCP 6.0. 
### **Description** 
This projection provides the **10th percentile** estimate for mid-century daytime highs under high global emissions. It maps areas where heat hazard exposure is likely to remain relatively lower even as emissions continue to peak. 
* **Assumption:** Higher maximum temperatures represent a baseline risk for heat hazard-exposure that must be accounted for in adaptation planning. 
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This informs the **Hazard Exposure** dimension for mid-century scenarios. 
* **Caveat:** This estimate corresponds to the low end of the projected probability range for this scenario. 
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCP18** probabilistic projections.
