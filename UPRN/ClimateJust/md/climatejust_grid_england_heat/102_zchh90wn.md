### Change in temperature of the warmest night baseline to 2050s (90th percentile) (z-score)

#### Description

This field identifies areas facing a high-end relative increase in extreme night-time heat if global carbon mitigation is not achieved.

* **Internal name:** `zchh90wn`
* **Hazard Metric:** Standardized z-score for the projected change in the temperature of the warmest summer night from the baseline to the 2050s.
* **Statistical Context:** Uses a **high emissions scenario** (H50) and the **90th percentile (high estimate)**.


#### Data Source

Hazard-exposure indices are derived from the **UK Climate Projections 2009 (UKCP09)** probabilistic projections. Standardisation (z-scores) is relative to the **English mean**.
