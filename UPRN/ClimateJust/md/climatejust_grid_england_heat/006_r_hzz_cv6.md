### Average vulnerability and mean summer maximum temperature 2050s - High estimate (90th percentile)

#### Description

(Internal name: `r_hzz_cv6`)

This metric evaluates potential disadvantage under a low-carbon future if the climate system responds more aggressively than median projections suggest.
*   **Hazard Metric:** Mean summer maximum temperatures for the 2050s time horizon.
*   **Statistical Context:** Uses a **low emissions scenario** (L50) and the **90th percentile (high estimate)**, a scenario that is only 10% likely to be exceeded.
