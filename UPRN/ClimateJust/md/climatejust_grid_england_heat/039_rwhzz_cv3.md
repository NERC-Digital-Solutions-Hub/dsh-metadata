### Population weighted vulnerability and mean summer maximum temperature 2050s - High estimate (90th percentile)

#### Description

(Internal name: `rwhzz_cv3`)

This field identifies areas of acute risk for large numbers of sensitive residents by combining population-weighted vulnerability with a high-end mid-century temperature estimate.
*   **Hazard Metric:** Mean summer maximum temperature in the 2050s.
*   **Statistical Context:** Uses a **high emissions scenario** (H50) and the **90th percentile (high estimate)**.
