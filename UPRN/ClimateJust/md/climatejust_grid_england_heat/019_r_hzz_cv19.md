### Average vulnerability and change in the temperature of the warmest day baseline to 2050s - Low estimate (10th percentile)

#### Description

(Internal name: `r_hzz_cv19`)

This field combines average vulnerability with the minimum expected intensification of **extreme peak heat** (the single hottest day) under high global emissions.
*   **Hazard Metric:** The projected change in the temperature of the **warmest summer day** compared to the 1961–1990 baseline.
*   **Statistical Context:** Uses a **high emissions scenario** (H50) and the **10th percentile (low estimate)**.
