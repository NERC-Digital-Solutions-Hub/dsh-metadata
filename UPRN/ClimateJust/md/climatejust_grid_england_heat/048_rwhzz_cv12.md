### Population weighted vulnerability and change in mean summer maximum temperature baseline to 2050s - High estimate (90th percentile)

#### Description

(Internal name: `rwhzz_cv12`)

This field evaluates potential severe disadvantage for large numbers of residents by combining population-weighted vulnerability with a high-end estimate of temperature increases under **high emissions** (H50).

*   **Hazard Metric:** Change in mean summer maximum temperature baseline to 2050s.
*   **Statistical Context:** Uses the **90th percentile (high estimate)**; values are 90% likely to be below this threshold.
