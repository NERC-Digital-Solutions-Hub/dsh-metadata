### Population weighted vulnerability and change in the temperature of the warmest day baseline to 2050s - Low estimate (10th percentile)

#### Description

(Internal name: `rwhzz_cv19`)

This field combines population-weighted vulnerability with the minimum expected intensification of **extreme peak heat** under **high global emissions** (H50).

*   **Hazard Metric:** The projected change in the temperature of the **warmest summer day** from the 1961–1990 baseline to the 2050s.
*   **Statistical Context:** Uses the **10th percentile (low estimate)**.
