### Average vulnerability and change in the temperature of the warmest night baseline to 2050s - Low estimate (10th percentile)

#### Description

(Internal name: `r_hzz_cv28`)

This field identifies disadvantage relative to the minimum projected intensification of extreme night-time heat in a high emissions world.
*   **Hazard Metric:** The projected change in the temperature of the **warmest summer night** compared to the 1961–1990 baseline.
*   **Statistical Context:** Uses a **high emissions scenario** (H50) and the **10th percentile (low estimate)**, meaning actual values are statistically 90% likely to be higher.
