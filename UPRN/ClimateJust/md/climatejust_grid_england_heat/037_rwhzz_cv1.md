### Population weighted vulnerability and mean summer maximum temperature 2050s - Low estimate (10th percentile)

#### Description

(Internal name: `rwhzz_cv1`)

This metric identifies the minimum baseline of expected population-weighted disadvantage in a high emissions world.
*   **Vulnerability Metric:** Uses a **population-weighted representation** of socio-spatial heat vulnerability, which emphasizes the number of people in a 25km grid cell likely to be affected.
*   **Hazard Metric:** Mean summer maximum temperature in the 2050s (the 20-year window centered on 2050).
*   **Statistical Context:** Uses a **high emissions scenario** (H50) and the **10th percentile (low estimate)**.
