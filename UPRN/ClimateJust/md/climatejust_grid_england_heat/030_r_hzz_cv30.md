### Average vulnerability and change in the temperature of the warmest night baseline to 2050s - High estimate (90th percentile)

#### Description

(Internal name: `r_hzz_cv30`)

This field identifies areas at acute risk of heat disadvantage by combining average vulnerability with severe projections for extreme night-time heat.
*   **Hazard Metric:** Change in the temperature of the warmest night baseline to 2050s.
*   **Statistical Context:** Uses a **high emissions scenario** (H50) and the **90th percentile (high estimate)**.
