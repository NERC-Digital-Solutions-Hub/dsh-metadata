### Average vulnerability and change in mean summer maximum temperature baseline to 2050s - High estimate (90th percentile)

#### Description

(Internal name: `r_hzz_cv12`)

This field evaluates the potential for severe disadvantage by combining social vulnerability with the high-end estimate of temperature increases under high emissions.
*   **Hazard Metric:** Change in mean summer maximum temperature baseline to 2050s.
*   **Statistical Context:** Uses a **high emissions scenario** (H50) and the **90th percentile (high estimate)**, a scenario with only a 10% chance of being exceeded.
