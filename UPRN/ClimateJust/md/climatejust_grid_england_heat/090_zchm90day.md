### Change in mean summer maximum temperature baseline to 2050s (90th percentile) (z-score)

#### Description

This field identifies areas projected to face a high-end intensification of summer maximum temperatures compared to the English average.

* **Internal name:** `zchm90day`
* **Hazard Metric:** Represents the standardized **z-score** for the projected **change** in mean summer maximum temperatures from the 1961–1990 climate baseline to the 2050s (2040–2060).
* **Statistical Context:** Uses a **medium emissions scenario** (M50) and the **90th percentile (high estimate)**, meaning actual temperature increases are statistically 90% likely to be below this threshold.


#### Data Source

Hazard-exposure indices are derived from the **UK Climate Projections 2009 (UKCP09)** probabilistic projections. Standardisation (z-scores) is relative to the **English mean**.
