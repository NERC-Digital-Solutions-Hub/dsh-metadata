### Average vulnerability and mean summer maximum temperature 2050s - Low estimate (10th percentile)

#### Description

(Internal name: `r_hzz_cv4`)

This metric identifies the minimum baseline of heat disadvantage in a world where carbon mitigation is successful.
*   **Hazard Metric:** Mean summer maximum temperatures for the 2050s time horizon.
*   **Statistical Context:** Uses a **low emissions scenario** (L50) and the **10th percentile (low estimate)**, meaning actual values are statistically 90% likely to be higher than this threshold.
