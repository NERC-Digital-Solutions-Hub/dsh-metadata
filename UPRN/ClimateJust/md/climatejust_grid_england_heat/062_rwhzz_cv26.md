### Population weighted vulnerability and change in the temperature of the warmest day baseline to 2050s - Central estimate (50th percentile) RECOMMENDED

#### Description

(Internal name: `rwhzz_cv26`)

This is a **recommended starting point** for understanding how the mid-century intensification of peak daytime heat may affect sensitive populations when accounting for population density.

*   **Hazard Metric:** The projected change in the temperature of the **warmest summer day** from the 1961–1990 baseline to the 2050s under a **medium emissions scenario** (M50).
*   **Statistical Context:** Uses the **50th percentile (central estimate)**.
