### Change in temperature of the warmest night baseline to 2050s (10th percentile) (z-score)

#### Description

This metric identifies the minimum baseline of expected extreme night-time heat intensification assuming successful carbon mitigation.

* **Internal name:** `zchl10wn`
* **Hazard Metric:** Standardized z-score for the projected change in the temperature of the warmest summer night from the baseline to the 2050s.
* **Statistical Context:** Uses a **low emissions scenario** (L50) and the **10th percentile (low estimate)**.


#### Data Source

Hazard-exposure indices are derived from the **UK Climate Projections 2009 (UKCP09)** probabilistic projections. Standardisation (z-scores) is relative to the **English mean**.
