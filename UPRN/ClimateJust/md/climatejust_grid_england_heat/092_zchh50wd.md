### Change in the temperature of the warmest day baseline to 2050s (50th percentile) (z-score)

#### Description

This field maps the likely relative increase in extreme single-day peak temperatures under high global emissions.

* **Internal name:** `zchh50wd`
* **Hazard Metric:** Standardized z-score for the projected change in the temperature of the warmest summer day from the baseline to the 2050s.
* **Statistical Context:** Uses a **high emissions scenario** (H50) and the **50th percentile (central estimate)**, representing the median probabilistic climate outcome.


#### Data Source

Hazard-exposure indices are derived from the **UK Climate Projections 2009 (UKCP09)** probabilistic projections. Standardisation (z-scores) is relative to the **English mean**.
