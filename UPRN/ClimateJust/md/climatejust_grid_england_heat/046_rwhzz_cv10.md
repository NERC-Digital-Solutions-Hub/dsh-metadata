### Population weighted vulnerability and change in mean summer maximum temperature baseline to 2050s - Low estimate (10th percentile)

#### Description

(Internal name: `rwhzz_cv10`)

This field identifies the minimum baseline of population-weighted disadvantage relative to the projected intensification of heat hazards in a **high emissions world** (H50).

*   **Vulnerability Metric:** Uses a population-weighted representation of social vulnerability, emphasizing grid cells where larger numbers of sensitive people live. This represents current-day vulnerability conditions.
*   **Hazard Metric:** Represents the projected **change** in mean summer maximum temperature from the climate baseline (1961–1990) to the 2050s (2040–2060).
*   **Statistical Context:** Uses the **10th percentile (low estimate)**, meaning actual values are 90% likely to be higher than this threshold.
