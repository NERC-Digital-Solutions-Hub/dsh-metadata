### Average vulnerability and mean summer maximum temperature 2050s - High estimate (90th percentile)

#### Description

(Internal name: `r_hzz_cv3`)

This field identifies areas of acute heat disadvantage by combining average social vulnerability with a cautious, high-end estimate of mid-century temperatures.
*   **Hazard Metric:** Represents the average daily maximum temperature for June, July, and August in the 20-year window centered on 2050 (2040–2060).
*   **Statistical Context:** This version uses a **high emissions scenario** (H50) and the **90th percentile (high estimate)**, meaning actual temperatures are 90% likely to be below this severe threshold.
