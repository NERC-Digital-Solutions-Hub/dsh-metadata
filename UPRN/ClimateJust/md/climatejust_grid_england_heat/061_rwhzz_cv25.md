### Population weighted vulnerability and change in the temperature of the warmest day baseline to 2050s - Low estimate (10th percentile)

#### Description

(Internal name: `rwhzz_cv25`)

This field provides the minimum expected population-weighted disadvantage from **peak extreme heat** intensification for a **medium emissions pathway** (M50).

*   **Hazard Metric:** Change in the temperature of the warmest day baseline to 2050s.
*   **Statistical Context:** Uses the **10th percentile (low estimate)**.
