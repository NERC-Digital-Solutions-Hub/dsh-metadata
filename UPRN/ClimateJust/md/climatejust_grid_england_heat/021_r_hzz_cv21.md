### Average vulnerability and change in the temperature of the warmest day baseline to 2050s - High estimate (90th percentile)

#### Description

(Internal name: `r_hzz_cv21`)

This field identifies areas of acute risk for **extreme heat events** by combining social vulnerability with the high-end estimate of single-day peak temperature increases.
*   **Hazard Metric:** Change in the temperature of the warmest day baseline to 2050s.
*   **Statistical Context:** Uses a **high emissions scenario** (H50) and the **90th percentile (high estimate)**.
