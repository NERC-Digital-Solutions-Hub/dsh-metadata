### Average vulnerability and change in the temperature of the warmest day baseline to 2050s - High estimate (90th percentile)

#### Description

(Internal name: `r_hzz_cv27`)

This metric identifies areas of significant relative disadvantage under a mid-range emissions pathway if the climate system is highly sensitive to greenhouse gases.
*   **Hazard Metric:** Change in the temperature of the warmest day baseline to 2050s.
*   **Statistical Context:** Uses a **medium emissions scenario** (M50) and the **90th percentile (high estimate)**, a cautious projection where actual temperatures are 90% likely to be below this threshold.
