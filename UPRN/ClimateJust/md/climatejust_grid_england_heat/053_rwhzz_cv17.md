### Population weighted vulnerability and change in mean summer maximum temperature baseline to 2050s - Central estimate (50th percentile) RECOMMENDED

#### Description

(Internal name: `rwhzz_cv17`)

This is the **recommended starting point** for understanding how the mid-century **intensification** of heat hazards may affect sensitive populations in England, accounting for the number of people in a given area.

*   **Hazard Metric:** The projected **change** in mean summer maximum temperature from the climate baseline to the 2050s under a **medium emissions scenario** (M50).
*   **Statistical Context:** Uses the **50th percentile (central estimate)**.
