### Change in the temperature of the warmest day baseline to 2050s (90th percentile) (z-score)

#### Description

This field identifies areas facing a high-end projected increase in peak daytime temperatures if global carbon mitigation fails.

* **Internal name:** `zchh90wd`
* **Hazard Metric:** Standardized z-score for the projected change in the temperature of the warmest summer day from the baseline to the 2050s.
* **Statistical Context:** Uses a **high emissions scenario** (H50) and the **90th percentile (high estimate)**.


#### Data Source

Hazard-exposure indices are derived from the **UK Climate Projections 2009 (UKCP09)** probabilistic projections. Standardisation (z-scores) is relative to the **English mean**.
