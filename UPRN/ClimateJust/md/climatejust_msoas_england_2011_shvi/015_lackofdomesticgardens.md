The field **'Lack of domestic gardens (area of building/domestic gardens)'** (internal name `lackofdomesticgardens`) measures the ratio of domestic building area to the area of private gardens.

### Description

This indicator identifies neighbourhoods where residents have little or no access to private outdoor green space, which is a critical factor for local cooling.

* **Assumption:** Gardens provide vital drainage and cooling functions; neighbourhoods where homes have relatively small or no gardens are assumed to face more severe heatwave impacts.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Enhanced Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** There is some uncertainty because the actual cooling provided depends on how individual owners manage or pave over their gardens.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **ONS Greenspace** statistics.
