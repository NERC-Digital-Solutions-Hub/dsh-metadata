The field **'Elevation (m)'** (internal name `elevation`) represents the average height above sea level for a given area.

### Description

Elevation is used to assess **Enhanced Exposure** related to **Physical Geography**, as height can influence local temperature and exposure to climate hazards.

* **Assumption:** Lower elevations are generally associated with higher exposure to certain climate hazards, though for heat, it serves to map the physical profile of the neighbourhood's environment.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Enhanced Exposure** dimension.
* **Caveat:** While higher elevation often correlates with lower temperatures, urban heat island effects in high-altitude cities can sometimes override this natural cooling.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **Elevation data** (e.g., from the US Geological Survey) mapped to MSOA boundaries.
