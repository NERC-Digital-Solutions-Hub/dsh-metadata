The field **'Inability to Prepare index'** (internal name `inabilitytoprepareindex`) is a composite measure of the social factors that prevent people from taking precautions before a heat event.

### **Description**
The **Inability to Prepare index** assesses the average capacity of individuals within a neighbourhood to take protective measures, such as modifying their homes or obtaining information.

*   **Assumption:** Adaptive capacity is governed primarily by social factors; a person's ability to take precautions is limited by their financial resources, housing situation, and language proficiency.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is one of the five primary dimensions of the **Socio-Spatial Heat Vulnerability Index**.
*   **Caveat:** This is a relative index based on z-scores and has no defined units.
*   **Data Source:** An equally-weighted combination of indicators from the **Income**, **Tenure**, and **Information Use (Language)** domains.
