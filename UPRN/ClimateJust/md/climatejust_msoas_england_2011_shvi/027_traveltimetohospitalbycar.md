The field **'Travel time to nearest hospital by car'** (internal name `traveltimetohospitalbycar`) estimates the driving time to the closest hospital.

### **Description**
A measure of **Service Access**, this field identifies neighbourhoods where even those with private transport may face significant delays in reaching emergency care.

*   **Assumption:** Areas with higher travel times to hospitals are assumed to be more socially vulnerable due to the distance from life-saving medical assistance.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Ability to Recover** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** Higher travel times generally indicate higher vulnerability, which aligns with most SSHVI metrics.
*   **Data Source:** Calculated from **ONS Accessibility statistics**.
