The field **'High rise homes (% households with lowest floor 5th floor or above)'** (internal name `highrisehomes`) measures the proportion of dwellings in an area located on the fifth floor or higher of a building.

### **Description**
This metric identifies communities living in high-density, elevated housing, which is a key factor in **Building Characteristics** for heat vulnerability.

*   **Assumption:** Residents in high-rise dwellings are more likely to be affected by high internal temperatures during heatwaves, as heat often accumulates in upper-floor apartments.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Enhanced Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** Some tenement buildings or blocks with fewer than five floors may also experience high temperatures but are not captured by this specific threshold.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from the **2011 Census**.
