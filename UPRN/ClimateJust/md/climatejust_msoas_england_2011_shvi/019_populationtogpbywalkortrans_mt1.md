The field **'% of at risk population (no car) outside of 15 minutes by walk/public transport to nearest GP'** (internal name `populationtogpbywalkortrans_mt1`) measures the proportion of car-less households who live far from primary care.

### Description

This indicator identifies communities where logistical and mobility barriers coincide with a lack of private transport, creating severe **Service Access** vulnerability.

* **Assumption:** High proportions of car-less people living more than 15 minutes away from a GP indicate high social vulnerability, as these residents are least able to access medical help during heatwaves.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Ability to Recover** dimension.
* **Caveat:** This is a composite metric specifically targeting "at risk" populations rather than the general population.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **DfT Accessibility data** combined with **2011 Census** car ownership data.
