The field **'Distance to coast (km)'** (internal name `distancetocoast`) represents the linear distance from the centre of an MSOA to the nearest coastline.

### **Description**
Within the SSHVI framework, this metric is used to evaluate **Enhanced Exposure** based on **Physical Geography**, identifying inland areas that lack natural maritime cooling.

*   **Assumption:** Proximity to the coast is a mitigating factor for heat; coastal areas are more likely to be cooler than inland areas during heatwaves due to the cooling effects of onshore breezes.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Enhanced Exposure** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** The cooling effect of the coast typically diminishes quickly as one moves inland, and its impact can be modified by local topography.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **2011 MSOA population weighted centroids** and UK boundary lines.
