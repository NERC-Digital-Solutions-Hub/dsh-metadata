The field **'Enhanced exposure to heat index'** (internal name `enhancedexposuretoheatindex`) is a composite measure reflecting the physical characteristics of a neighbourhood that accentuate the severity of heat.

### **Description**
The **Enhanced Exposure index** accounts for how the built environment and physical geography interact with heat hazard to create uneven impacts across different locations.

*   **Assumption:** Social vulnerability is not independent of physical environment; factors like high-rise housing, a lack of green space, and inland locations "enhance" the degree of contact and the severity of impacts from a heatwave.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is one of the five primary dimensions of the **Socio-Spatial Heat Vulnerability Index**.
*   **Caveat:** This index measures the potential for enhanced impacts based on physical traits, not the actual temperature recorded during an event.
*   **Data Source:** Calculated as an equally-weighted combination of scores from the **Physical Environment**, **Physical Geography**, and **Building Characteristics** domains.
