The field **'Inability to Recover index'** (internal name `inabilitytorecoverindex`) is a composite measure of a community's capacity to obtain help and bounce back after suffering heat stress.

### **Description**
The **Inability to Recover index** evaluates the factors that enable or hinder a neighbourhood's recovery from negative heat impacts, focusing on mobility and access to essential services.

*   **Assumption:** Recovery is governed by the ability to access medical attention and the presence of social support networks to assist those affected by heat-related illness.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is one of the five primary dimensions of the **Socio-Spatial Heat Vulnerability Index**.
*   **Caveat:** Recovery capacity operates over a relatively short time scale for heat-related hazards compared to flood recovery.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from indicators in the **Social Networks**, **Mobility**, and **Service Access** domains.
