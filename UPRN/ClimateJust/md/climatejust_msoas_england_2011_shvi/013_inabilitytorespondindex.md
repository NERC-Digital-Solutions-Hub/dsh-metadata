The field **'Inability to Respond index'** (internal name `inabilitytorespondindex`) is a composite measure of the capacity of a neighbourhood to avoid heat stress during an active heat event.

### **Description**
The **Inability to Respond index** identifies factors that prevent people from taking immediate action to protect themselves during hot weather.

*   **Assumption:** Response capacity is determined by whether people know what to do, feel able to act, and have local "refuges" or support systems to contact during an event.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is one of the five primary dimensions of the **Socio-Spatial Heat Vulnerability Index**.
*   **Caveat:** Some response factors, like road density, are proxy indicators for general accessibility and may not capture all local nuances.
*   **Data Source:** An equally-weighted combination of indicators across domains including **Income**, **Language**, **Social Networks**, **Mobility**, **Crime**, **Accessibility**, and **Infrastructure**.
