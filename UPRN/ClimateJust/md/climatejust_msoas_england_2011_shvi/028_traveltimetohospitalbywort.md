The field **'Travel time to nearest hospital by walk/public transport'** (internal name `traveltimetohospitalbywort`) measures the time to reach a hospital via non-private modes of transport.

### **Description**
This indicator highlights the **Service Access** barriers faced by the most deprived residents, who often lack car ownership and rely on public services for emergency recovery.

*   **Assumption:** Longer travel times to hospitals via walking/public transport correlate with higher social vulnerability, representing a barrier to critical medical support.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Ability to Recover** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** This is one of several similar indicators used to account for relative medical accessibility.
*   **Data Source:** Recalculated from **ONS/DfT Accessibility statistics**.
