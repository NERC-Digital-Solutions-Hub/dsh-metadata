The field **'Built up area (% not greenspace)'** (internal name `builtuparea_notgreenspace`) represents the percentage of the built environment that lacks green vegetation.

### Description

This indicator measures **Enhanced Exposure** by identifying neighbourhoods with high proportions of impervious, non-vegetated surfaces which contribute to higher local temperatures.

* **Assumption:** Areas with higher proportions of green space are assumed to have better drainage and cooling functions; therefore, more built-up areas without greenspace are likely to experience more severe heatwave impacts.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Enhanced Exposure** dimension, specifically within the **Physical Environment** domain.
* **Caveat:** The cooling function of greenspace can be influenced by the type of vegetation and local management practices.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **UKCEH Land Cover Maps** and land-use statistics.
