The field **'Number of GPs within 15 minutes by car'** (internal name `populationtogpbycar_lt15`) indicates the count of General Practitioners accessible within a 15-minute drive.

### **Description**
This is a measure of **Service Access**, assessing the ease with which residents can reach primary medical care during a heat event.

*   **Assumption:** Areas with fewer GPs reachable within this timeframe are more socially vulnerable, as residents have lower access to medical help.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Ability to Recover** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** Higher values for this indicator represent *lower* vulnerability, which must be accounted for when comparing with other indicators.
*   **Data Source:** Recalculated from **ONS Accessibility statistics**.
