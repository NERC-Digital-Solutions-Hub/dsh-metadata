The field **'Number of hospitals within 30 minutes by car'** (internal name `hospitalsbycar_lt30`) indicates the count of medical facilities reachable within a half-hour drive.

### Description

This is a measure of **Service Access**, identifying communities that may face barriers to obtaining medical assistance if residents suffer from heat stress.

* **Assumption:** Neighbourhoods with a lower number of nearby hospitals are more socially vulnerable because they have reduced access to emergency medical care during hazardous heat events.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Ability to Recover** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Higher values for this indicator indicate *lower* vulnerability, which is the opposite of most other metrics in the dataset.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **DfT Accessibility statistics** and hospital location data.
