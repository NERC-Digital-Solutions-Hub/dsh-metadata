The field **'Travel time to nearest GP by walk/public transport'** (internal name `traveltimetogpbywalkortrans`) captures the average time required to reach a doctor's surgery without a car.

### Description

This metric evaluates **Service Access**, identifying communities that are geographically isolated from primary healthcare.

* **Assumption:** Residents in areas with longer travel times to the nearest GP are more socially vulnerable because they have lower access to medical help during heatwaves.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Ability to Recover** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Actual travel times can be much higher during public transport disruptions or for those with limited mobility.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **DfT/ONS Accessibility statistics**.
