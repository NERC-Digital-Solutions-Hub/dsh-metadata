The field **'Born outside UK/Ireland (%)'** (internal name `bornoutsideukireland`) represents the percentage of residents within a Middle Super Output Area (MSOA) who were born outside of the United Kingdom and Ireland.

### **Description**
Within the **Socio-Spatial Heat Vulnerability Index (SSHVI)** framework, this metric is used as a proxy indicator for **information-related vulnerability**. It identifies communities where residents may face specific barriers to building resilience against extreme heat events due to language or cultural differences.

*   **Assumption:** Higher proportions of people born outside of the UK and Ireland indicate higher social vulnerability because these individuals are statistically more likely to have difficulty understanding English-language guidance and may be less familiar with national and local support services.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs three primary dimensions of the index: **Ability to Prepare**, **Ability to Respond**, and **Ability to Recover**.
*   **Caveat:** The project notes that while this is a useful indicator for comparison with older datasets, some individuals within this group may have English as their mother tongue or face no language difficulties.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from the **2011 Census** (Table KS204).
