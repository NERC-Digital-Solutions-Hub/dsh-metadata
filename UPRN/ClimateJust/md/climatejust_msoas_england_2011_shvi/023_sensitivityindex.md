The field **'Sensitivity index'** (internal name `sensitivityindex`) is a composite measure of the personal biophysical characteristics that increase susceptibility to heat.

### **Description**
The **Sensitivity index** maps how personal factors like age and pre-existing health conditions make certain populations more likely to suffer negative health impacts from heatwaves.

*   **Assumption:** Vulnerability is partly determined by physiological factors; the very old, the very young, and those in poor health are biologically more susceptible to harm from high temperatures.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This is one of the five primary dimensions of the **Socio-Spatial Heat Vulnerability Index**.
*   **Caveat:** This index focuses on biophysical susceptibility and is often hazard-independent (i.e., sensitivity to heat is often similar to sensitivity to flooding).
*   **Data Source:** Calculated as an equally-weighted combination of indicators from the **Age** and **Health** domains.
