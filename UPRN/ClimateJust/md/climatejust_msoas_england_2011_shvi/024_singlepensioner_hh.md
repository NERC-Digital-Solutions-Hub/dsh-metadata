The field **'Single pensioner households (%)'** (internal name `singlepensioner_hh`) represents the percentage of households consisting of a person aged 65 or over living alone.

### Description

This metric is used as a proxy for **Social Isolation**, identifying individuals who may lack the networks required for assistance during a heatwave.

* **Assumption:** Areas with higher proportions of single pensioner households are more likely to have socially isolated residents, making them more vulnerable as they may lack someone to check on them or provide warnings.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Ability to Respond** and **Ability to Recover** dimensions.
* **Caveat:** Some single pensioners may have strong external social networks not captured by household composition data.
* **Data Source:** Derived from the **2011 Census** (Table QS113).
