The field **'Built up area (% area not blue space)'** (internal name `builtuparea_notbluespace`) represents the percentage of a built-up area within a Middle Super Output Area (MSOA) that does not consist of water bodies, such as lakes, rivers, or canals.

### **Description**
Within the **Socio-Spatial Heat Vulnerability Index (SSHVI)** framework, this metric is used to evaluate **Enhanced Exposure**. It identifies neighbourhoods where the lack of "blue space" may result in more severe heat impacts, as water bodies provide a vital natural cooling function that can mitigate local temperatures during extreme weather.

By measuring the extent to which an area is built-up without the presence of these cooling features, the index maps how the physical environment can either accentuate or offset the severity of a heatwave event.

*   **Assumption:** The more built-up an area is—and specifically the less it is associated with water bodies—the more likely it is that heatwave and hot weather impacts will be severe, as blue space provides a vital cooling function for the local environment.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Enhanced Exposure** dimension of the index, specifically within the **Physical Environment** domain.
*   **Caveat:** The effectiveness of blue space as a cooling agent is relative to its size and distribution within the urban fabric; additionally, this metric represents a physical attribute of the neighbourhood rather than actual recorded temperatures.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **Land Use Statistics (Generalised Land Use Database)** and **Ordnance Survey** land-use datasets.
