The field **'Number of GPs within 15 minutes by walk/public transport'** (internal name `populationtogpbywalkortrans_lt1`) counts the GPs accessible via walking or public transport within 15 minutes.

### **Description**
This focuses on **Service Access** for those without cars, which is a critical demographic during heatwaves when travel may be restricted.

*   **Assumption:** Lower numbers of GPs within 15 minutes by foot or public transport indicate higher social vulnerability due to reduced medical access.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Ability to Recover** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** High values for this indicator mean *lower* vulnerability.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **ONS/DfT Accessibility statistics**.
