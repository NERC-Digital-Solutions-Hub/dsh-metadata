The field **'% change in the number of enterprises'** (internal name `changeinenterprises`) measures the percentage increase or decrease in local businesses within a neighbourhood over a specific period.

### Description

This metric serves as a proxy for the **General Infrastructure** available to a community, identifying areas undergoing economic or service decline that may lack informal resources for heat resilience.

* **Assumption:** Shops and other community infrastructure can act as informal "cool refuges" during extreme heat events. Areas with declining levels of enterprises (represented by high negative values) are assumed to have higher vulnerability.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Ability to Respond** dimension.
* **Caveat:** A high density of retail units indicates lower vulnerability, which is the inverse of most other indicators in the dataset.
* **Data Source:** Derived from the **Office for National Statistics (ONS) Business Registers Unit (BRU)**.
