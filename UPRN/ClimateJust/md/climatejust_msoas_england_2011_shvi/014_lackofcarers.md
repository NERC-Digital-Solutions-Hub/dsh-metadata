The field **'Lack of carers (% people not providing unpaid care)'** (internal name `lackofcarers`) represents the proportion of the population that does not perform an unpaid caring role.

### Description

Used within the **Social Networks** domain, this metric identifies areas where there may be a deficit of informal community support for vulnerable residents.

* **Assumption:** Communities where more people provide unpaid care are assumed to have more extensive and effective social networks; conversely, higher proportions of people *not* providing care indicate higher vulnerability.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Ability to Respond** and **Ability to Recover** dimensions.
* **Caveat:** This is a reversed variable from the original Census data (which tracks people *providing* care) to ensure higher values indicate higher vulnerability.
* **Data Source:** Derived from the **2011 Census** (Table KS301).
