The field **'Number of hospitals within 30 minutes by walk/public transport'** (internal name `hospitalsbywalktransport_lt30`) counts the hospitals accessible via walking or public transport within 30 minutes.

### **Description**
This metric focuses on **Service Access** for individuals without private transport, highlighting those most reliant on public infrastructure for medical recovery.

*   **Assumption:** Areas with lower numbers of hospitals within this travel time are more vulnerable, as communities have lower access to medical help if they do not own a car.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Ability to Recover** dimension.
*   **Caveat:** Similar to the car-based metric, higher values for this indicator represent *lower* social vulnerability.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **DfT/ONS Accessibility statistics**.
