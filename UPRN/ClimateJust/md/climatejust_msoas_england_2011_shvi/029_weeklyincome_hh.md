The field **'Weekly household income estimate (£)'** (internal name `weeklyincome_hh`) provides an estimate of the mean weekly household income for an MSOA.

### **Description**
Used within the **Income** domain, this metric identifies areas of financial deprivation, which is a major driver of low adaptive capacity.

*   **Assumption:** Lower incomes indicate higher vulnerability because a lack of financial resources restricts people's ability to prepare for, respond to, and recover from heat events.
*   **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Ability to Prepare**, **Ability to Respond**, and **Ability to Recover** dimensions.
*   **Caveat:** For mapping, this variable is often "reversed" so that low income values are represented as high vulnerability scores.
*   **Data Source:** Derived from **ONS Average Weekly Household Net Income Estimates** (equivalised after housing costs).
