The field **'Density of retail units'** (internal name `retailunitsdensity`) measures the concentration of shops and retail establishments within an area.

### Description

Retail density is used as an indicator of **General Infrastructure** that can support community adaptation.

* **Assumption:** Shops act as informal refuges during extreme events because they are often cooler than homes. Areas with lower densities of such infrastructure are assumed to have higher vulnerability.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Ability to Respond** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Higher density values for this indicator indicate *lower* vulnerability, unlike most other metrics in the SSHVI.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **Office for National Statistics (ONS) Business Registers Unit (BRU)**.
