The field **'% of at risk population outside of 30 minutes by walk/PT to nearest hospital'** (internal name `populationtohospitalbycar_mt30`) represents the proportion of residents without private transport who live far from secondary care.

### Description

This is a critical measure of **Service Access**, identifying "medical deserts" where recovery from heat stress may be dangerously delayed.

* **Assumption:** Communities with high percentages of people more than 30 minutes from a hospital (via walking/public transport) are assumed to be more vulnerable due to the difficulty of obtaining emergency help.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Ability to Recover** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Public transport disruptions during extreme heat can further exacerbate the vulnerability represented by this metric.
* **Data Source:** Derived from **ONS/DfT Accessibility statistics**.
