The field **'Working away from home (% not working at home)'** (internal name `populationworkingawayfromhome`) indicates the percentage of the workforce that does not work from their residence.

### Description

Used within the **Mobility** domain, this metric identifies areas where residents are frequently absent from their homes, impacting community response capacity.

* **Assumption:** Neighbourhoods with high proportions of people working away from home may have slower responses to heatwaves or warnings, as there are fewer people present to assist dependents or manage the home environment.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Ability to Recover** and **Ability to Respond** dimensions.
* **Caveat:** This is calculated by subtracting home workers from 100% of the working population.
* **Data Source:** Derived from the **2011 Census** (Table QS701).
