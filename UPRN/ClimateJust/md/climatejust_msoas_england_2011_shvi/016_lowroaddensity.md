The field **'Low road density (% area not road)'** (internal name `lowroaddensity`) represents the percentage of land area that is not covered by roads.

### Description

This metric is used as a proxy for **General Accessibility**, identifying areas where the physical layout may hinder a quick response or the delivery of help during an emergency.

* **Assumption:** People living in areas with lower road density are assumed to have lower general accessibility and therefore higher social vulnerability.
* **Vulnerability Dimension:** This indicator informs the **Ability to Respond** dimension.
* **Caveat:** Road density is only a proxy for accessibility; it does not account for the quality or frequency of local transport services.
* **Data Source:** Calculated from **Land Use Statistics (Generalised Land Use Database)**.
